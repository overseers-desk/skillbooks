# MIND — notes for AI sessions

How the market holds one trading name, and whether a movement in that standing is the operator's own, in four phases (Measure, Index, Name, Decide). `mind-methodology.md` is the whole procedure. It runs when the operator calls it, on the trading name and the windows the operator names, and carries no cadence of its own.

A run is a dated document in the campaign folder. It reads the prior run before it measures anything, reads matched windows a year apart for the whole and for each target market, restates every figure and open decision it compares against, reads a panel of comparable businesses on the same instruments, windows and markets, and ends at Name with numbered findings. Decide is the operator's phase; its table sits in the run and is read by the next one. The panel comes from the campaign's register of comparable businesses and the instruments from the campaign's instrument list; this document names only their kinds.
