## Institutional context

PurePods Ltd is an MBIE-funded NZ agritourism operator running luxury glass eco-cabin experiences on working farms and scenic private properties. Key characteristics relevant to insurance exposure:

- **Physical assets in remote, exposed environments**: Glass pods in coastal, alpine, and rural NZ settings — susceptible to storm, earthquake, flood (NZ is an active seismic zone).
- **Public liability at scale**: Guest overnight stays in remote off-grid structures, far from emergency services; any personal injury event has significant liability exposure.
- **Business interruption**: Tourism revenue is weather-dependent and highly seasonal; the remote nature of pod locations means closures for access/weather are regular operational risks.
- **MBIE TGP growth trajectory**: Expanding from current 9 locations toward 30 pods means recurring capital commitment, evolving asset base, and changing insurance program requirements each year.
- **Farm/landowner partnerships**: PurePods places pods on third-party land (farmers' properties) — creating a joint-insured-or-indemnified structure with the landowner that a specialist broker should review.
- **Manufacturing in Christchurch**: In-house pod manufacture creates product liability exposure.
