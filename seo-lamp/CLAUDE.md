# LAMP — notes for AI sessions

The four-phase methodology for one product's standing in search and what that standing is worth (Locate, Attribute, Move, Prove). `lamp-methodology.md` is the whole procedure. A run is a dated document in the operator's repository; the captures it makes go into the operator's capture series, and the demand figures it uses come from the operator's market map, which is a standing artefact and not part of a run.

The methodology was restructured on 13 September 2026 after a structural audit. Runs dated before that rank by impressions and carry no prediction, control or money: read them as measurements, not as recommendations.
