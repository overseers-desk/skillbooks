# LAMP — notes for AI sessions

How a product stands in search and what that standing is worth, in four phases (Locate, Attribute, Move, Prove). `lamp-methodology.md` is the whole procedure. It runs when the operator calls it, over the range of dates and the set of products he names, and carries no cadence of its own.

A call produces two kinds of report. A run studies one product and proposes what to do about it. A movement report studies one unexplained movement and proposes nothing, since its unit is a question rather than a subject; it hands the runs their shared measurement ground. Both are read by a cold agent against the audit's classes before they are published.

A report is a dated document in the operator's repository, and a report redone because it was wrong is an edit of it rather than a second file. The captures a report makes go into the operator's capture series, committed, and the demand figures come from the operator's market map, a standing artefact and not part of any report.

Runs dated before 13 September 2026 rank by impressions and carry no prediction, control or money: read them as measurements, not as recommendations.
