# Instagram — platform module

Read before the first Instagram access in a session. No roster column; record a confirmed handle in `p_note`. Access runs through the shared logged-in session, paced across all workers by the serialised browser.

- A profile is a channel source: read the bio text and the contact button's text for a published email address before concluding none exists. Business accounts often name the operator or link the company site in the bio; follow that link for the cheaper unpaced read.
