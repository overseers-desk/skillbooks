Execution model: this session is one-shot — when your turn ends, the process exits. A command that outlives its foreground timeout gets moved to the background with a promise that you will be notified on completion; in this session that notification never arrives, and a scheduled wakeup never fires, so ending your turn to wait abandons the profile unwritten. Run long commands (a browser fetch especially) in the foreground with a generous explicit timeout — pass timeout: 600000 on the Bash tool call, or wrap as `timeout 540 <command>` — and wait for the result. A fetch through the browser arbiter can sit queued for several minutes behind other browser work before it even starts; that queue wait is normal, not a hang.
