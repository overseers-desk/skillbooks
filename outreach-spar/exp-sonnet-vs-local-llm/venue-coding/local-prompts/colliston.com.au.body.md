## Memories to Last a Lifetime

## Our Story is Helping You Start Yours

Sometimes life gives you a place so special that you can’t keep it to yourself. That’s exactly how Jamie and Kylie felt when they first laid eyes on Colliston Farm back in 2019. It was the middle of a drought, the grass was dry, and the hills looked a little weary—but the beauty of the property was undeniable. The real clincher? The creek winding its way through the land. In Jamie’s words: *“We figured if it could still look this good in a drought, imagine it when the rains come!”*

Since then, the farm has flourished into a lush backdrop where rustic charm meets natural elegance. Over the years, Jamie and Kylie have hosted family weddings and celebrations on the property, each one filled with laughter, love, and a touch of country magic. The feedback from friends and family was so glowing (and persistent!) that they decided it was selfish not to share. After all, if your farm is the kind of place that makes people ask, *“Can I get married here too?”*—well, that’s a sign.

Colliston Farm is more than just a wedding venue; it’s a labour of love. For Jamie and Kylie, it’s about creating a space where couples can celebrate their story in their own unique way. Whether it’s saying “I do” under the towering pines, celebrating in the Barn and capturing those golden-hour photos by the creek, the farm offers couples the freedom to craft a wedding that feels like them.

And while they take their role as hosts seriously, Jamie and Kylie don’t take themselves too seriously. They believe weddings should be beautiful, yes—but also relaxed, fun, and full of moments that feel natural (and maybe a little unscripted). After all, marriage is about love and laughter, not just polished photos.

In opening Colliston Farm to couples, Jamie and Kylie aren’t just offering a venue—they’re inviting people into a piece of their story. A story that started with a farm found in the middle of a drought, blossomed with family celebrations, and continues today as a place where new chapters begin for every couple who chooses to marry here.

So, why Colliston Farm? Because it’s beautiful. Because it’s real. And because Jamie and Kylie love nothing more than seeing their farm filled with the kind of joy that weddings bring.
