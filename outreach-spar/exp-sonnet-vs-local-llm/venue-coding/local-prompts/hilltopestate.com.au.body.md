## A Journey of Renewal

What could have been the end of a dream became the beginning of a new chapter

### Christmas 2023

The aftermath of the tornado that swept through our property

### Today

Rebuilt stronger and more beautiful than ever

In December 2023, a tornado swept through Tamborine Mountain, leaving a path of destruction across the region. Trees fell, buildings were damaged, and Hilltop Estate faced an uncertain future.

What followed was a journey of renewal. With the support of our community and a shared commitment to create something truly special, we rebuilt the estate with care and intention. Every beam placed, every garden replanted, and every space designed reflects both restoration and fresh possibility.

Today, Hilltop Estate stands renewed — where our heritage chapel continues its story, and our new spaces offer a setting that unites timeless character with modern elegance.

# Booking Policy

Last updated 28 April 2026

Thank you for choosing Hilltop Estate. The terms below apply to all restaurant bookings made through our website. By completing a booking, you agree to these terms.

## Bookings

Reserving a table is free. We'll send a confirmation email with your booking details and a link you can use to cancel if your plans change.

Bookings can be made for parties of 1 to 10 guests online. For larger groups, please call us on 1300 912 765 and we'll arrange your booking directly.

## Cancellations

You can cancel any time before your arrival using the link in your confirmation email — no phone call required.

We appreciate as much notice as possible so we can offer the table to other guests. Where you can, please let us know at least a few hours before your booking time.

## Changes to your booking

Online rescheduling is not currently available. To change your date, time, or party size, please email us at [email protected] or call 1300 912 765 at least 24 hours in advance and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Increases in party size are subject to availability — please let us know in advance where possible.

## Arrival times & late arrivals

Please arrive at your booked time. If you're running late, call us as soon as you can. We'll hold your table for **15 minutes past your booking time**; after that we may need to release the table to other guests.

Tables are held for a maximum of 2 hours from your arrival time so we can welcome the next guests. If you'd like a longer dining window, please mention it when booking and we'll do our best.

## No-shows

If you do not arrive and have not contacted us, your booking is treated as a no-show. Repeated no-shows may affect your ability to make future bookings with us.

## Dietary requirements & allergies

Please note any dietary requirements or allergies in the special requests field when booking, or let us know in advance by email or phone. We accommodate most requirements where possible.

**Severe allergies must be communicated before your arrival.** While we take great care in our kitchen, we cannot guarantee that any dish is entirely free from a particular allergen due to shared preparation surfaces.

## Children & accessibility

Children are warmly welcomed. We have high chairs available on request — please mention in your booking notes if you'll need one.

Both our Skyview Balcony and Indoor Dining areas are wheelchair accessible. If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us before booking so we can ensure your table suits your requirements.

## Weather & circumstances beyond our control

In the event of severe weather, power failure, or other circumstances beyond our control that prevent us from honouring your booking, we will contact you as soon as possible to reschedule.

Today, Hilltop Estate stands renewed — where our heritage chapel continues its story, and our new spaces offer a setting that unites timeless character with modern elegance.
