# Profile: Josh Muccio

## Current role
Josh Muccio is the founder and host of **The Pitch**, a startup podcast where entrepreneurs pitch real venture capital to a live investor panel. The show airs weekly (episodic cadence visible across 197+ episodes). Josh co-founded the show after purchasing it from Gimlet Media and now runs it independently through The Pitch Fund, which deploys capital alongside the show. The podcast is produced in partnership with the Vox Media Podcast Network and has accumulated 30M+ downloads.
