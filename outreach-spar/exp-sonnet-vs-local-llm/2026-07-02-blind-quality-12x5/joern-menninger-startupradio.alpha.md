## What they have said publicly
**On AI agents and SaaS repricing (startuprad.io/post/ai-agents-and-the-end-of-seat-based-saas, Mar/Apr 2026):** Menninger authored his own editorial arguing that AI agents shift SaaS value from licensed seats to completed outcomes. He identifies five themes: seat-based pricing is under pressure as agents perform multi-user work; enterprise systems (SAP-class) survive but lose interface control; defensibility moves to data, regulation, and integration depth rather than features; DACH has a temporary window before the AI advantage closes; outcome-based contracts will displace user-count contracts. He frames "real adoption metrics" as inference spend and workflow changes, not executive claims.

**On the platform's mission (startuprad.io/post/startuprad-io-europe-s-voice-on-startups-goes-global):** Menninger frames Startuprad.io as "Europe's on-demand archive of entrepreneurial intelligence — a bridge between regional innovation and global capital flows," and positions the platform's evolution as "architecting its knowledge layer" through AI, making it "more than a podcast — it's a knowledge platform."

**On LinkedIn about section (joernmenninger, Jun 2026):** "For companies entering or expanding in Europe, the hard part is often not reach. It is trust, explanation, and relevance before the first serious buyer, investor, or partner conversation."
