Pivoted Waymark's entire platform to GPT-3-based video ad copy for small businesses in 2020–2022.

**On the podcast's mission (About page):** "Through conversations with notable builders, researchers, and investors, as well as original deep-dive analysis on topics of particular interest, Nathan helps business, policy, and academic leaders stay up to date with AI developments and implications."
