# Profile: Lex Fridman

## Current role
Lex Fridman is the host and researcher of the Lex Fridman Podcast, a long-form video podcast published on YouTube and distributed across all major podcast platforms. The podcast launched in 2019 (originally titled "The Artificial Intelligence Podcast") and renamed to the Lex Fridman Podcast in 2020. Fridman is a Research Scientist at MIT's Laboratory for Information and Decision Systems (LIDS), focusing on human-AI interaction. The show runs at a weekly cadence and features wide-ranging conversations covering AI, robotics, business, geopolitics, history, and the human condition.
