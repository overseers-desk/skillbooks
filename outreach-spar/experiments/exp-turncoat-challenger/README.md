# Turncoat challenger

The persona step of the approach loop, run as three turns of one session instead of one prompt, so the reader reacts before it knows whose side it is on.

**Problem.** Blind reads of the current challenger prompt with the email withheld named the sender as the party it works for in 3 of 3 runs, citing "email draft", "role-play", "in-character", "credible" and the DONE/REVISE criteria; with a real profile inserted, 6 of 6 cited the profile's star rating, relevance assessment and campaign vocabulary. A reader that knows it is testing a pitch for the pitch's author reads analytically and forgivingly. The recipient it stands in for triages in seconds.

**Design.** Three turns of one session, named for the reader's state in each. Gaslight 1 (`prompts/gaslight-1.txt`) is a colleague's casual ask, speaker unnamed, with the contact's own facts, whatever prior correspondence the mail index holds, and the email. It says nothing of whose side the reader is on: reading an email for someone is ordinary work, and the reader takes it to serve the one who asked. Nothing later is in context, the register invites a quick read, and the only fixed output is one word, reply or bin. Gaslight 2 (`prompts/gaslight-2.txt`), a second turn, is the slow read, the sentence scores and the reader's graph in one CSV, asked as a task, with no word about how to read or what the letter is for. Turncoat (`prompts/turncoat.txt`) names the side and asks for what the writer needs. From that turn the reader is the sender's and is of no further use as the recipient. The verdict is Gaslight 1's last word, taken before the reveal; Turncoat emits none and revises nothing.

**Prior correspondence.** `__PRIOR_CORRESPONDENCE__` is the dated list of our earlier messages to the address as the mail index holds them, under a "### Prior correspondence" heading, and is empty when the index has none; the prompt claims nothing about the sender being known or new either way.

**Profile facts.** The profile body from `# Profile` up to the first of `## Catalogue evidence`, `## Relevance assessment`, `## Verification corrections`, front matter dropped, and the word "campaign" removed from the "Who they know" heading and column.

**Test.** Up to 8 round-2 letters from `../exp-writer-based-prose`, favouring ones the persona passed while carrying unresolved references, topped up with failed ones as controls. Same model as round 2 by exact id. One session per letter: stage 1, then stage 2 and stage 3 as resumed turns. One author revision per letter whose stage 1 said bin, so at most 8 emails are written. Sessions run from a directory whose path carries no repo name, under a config directory holding only the credentials, so no office methodology loads. Readout per letter in `readout.md`: round-2 verdict; Gaslight 1's last word; the phrase it quoted for any reference it did not follow; whether it called the email misdirected or a scam; Gaslight 2 rows scored 0 to 2 on resolution; Turncoat's one change. Transcripts under `runs/`.
