# Profile: Josh Muccio

## Current role
Josh Muccio is the co-founder and host of "The Pitch," an independently owned podcast since July 2022. He also serves as a general partner at The Pitch Fund, which he founded in November 2022 to invest alongside listener investors via AngelList syndicate. His show focuses on early-stage startups and has raised significant capital for featured companies.
