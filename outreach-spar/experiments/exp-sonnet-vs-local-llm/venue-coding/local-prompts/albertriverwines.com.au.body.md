## A SCENIC RIM DESTINATION

Albert River Wines is a versatile venue that caters to Weddings, Functions, Farm Tours, Dining at Auchenflower Restaurant and our Cellar Door


Albert River Wines sweeping grounds are home to two historical buildings and sprawling vineyards. Auchenflower House has been home to three former Queensland Premiers, was relocated from Auchenflower in Brisbane and is where our restaurant and cellar door is located. The buildings wrap around deck, allows for alfresco dining with a view of Tamborine Mountain and our vineyards. Enjoying delicious food and spectacular wine has never been easier. Attached is Auchenflower Ballroom, our largest Wedding and Functions space, with the capacity to host 150 guests.


Tamborine House is the original homestead on the property. A stunning mid 1800's building, which has maintained it's original historical charms. Tamborine House is a dedicated Wedding and Function venue, with a large, covered deck overlooking our vineyards, the Jacaranda lined driveway and Tamborine Mountain.

The venue caters for couples, groups, families, weddings and functions. Open seven days a week for morning tipples, lunch Wednesday to Sunday and Weddings and Functions any day by arrangement.

## Our Inspiration

Albert River Wines – Auchenflower Restaurant is a unique Australian dining experience. Our Auchenflower restaurant is located on the sweeping verandah of “Auchenflower House”, overlooking the lush green valley of vines with a stunning backdrop of Tamborine Mountain.

Our menu can be best described as modern Australian with a cultural fusion.


Our head chef Nick brings youth, experience and excitement to our menu. Being a local in the Tamborine Community Nick has wonderful knowledge in local produce, and enjoys representing it.

## Our Inspiration

At Albert River Wines, we are passionate about producing premium wines that are uniquely Queensland. Our estate-grown fruit, harvested from our Tamborine vineyards, and quality contract-grown Queensland grapes are blended to create brilliant wines that suit our climate and the preferred tastes of our state. Our commitment to quality has been recognized with numerous awards, including several gold medals at prestigious shows across Australia and the world. We are proud to be a part of the new generation of Queensland wine producers, offering great quality wine at a price that suits all of our customers.

## FARM TOURS

### 1 - 1.5 hour tour time | 8 guest minimum

Wednesday - Friday

 

Monday & Tuesday by appointment (minimum numbers apply

The friendly staff at Albert River Wines would like to welcome all Senior Tour Groups to join us for a tour of our historic colonial farm.


Enjoy a stroll through our historic 1800’s homesteads, Tamborine House, the fully restored Auchenflower House, and Taabinga Chapel – the original Taabinga Lutheran Chapel.


You can also meet our friendly farm yard animals with Yvonne taking you through a day on a sheep station and sheep dog demonstrations with the help of her wonderful Kelpies.


With comfortable seating at Auchenflower Restaurant, wheelchair access and ample parking for buses, this venue is ideal for tour groups to enjoy an outing in the Australian country.

Tours run at 11am Wednesday to Friday, bookings are essential (5543 6622)


Albert River Wines and Tamborine Farm Tours invites you to experience our piece of paradise and history
