## Our Venue

Gold Coast Farm House is situated in the beautiful Numinbah Valley, just 30 km from Surfers Paradise. With 60 acres of rolling lush green grass lawn spotted with large native trees, 2 large spring fed dam’s and surrounded by national park, it’s the perfect location for a private wedding or function.


Tony’s Creek runs the full length of the property with 3 swimming holes, a secret glow worm cave and some small waterfalls, there are multiple bush walks starting from the property for you to explore. Alternatively, just sit back, relax and enjoy watching the native bird life in the trees and wallaby’s grazing .


The 100 plus year-old Queenslander sits at the top of the property overlooking the dams and has an uninterrupted view of Springbrook National Park and Page’s Pinnacle. With its high ceilings and beautiful leadlight windows, it truly feels like you have stepped back in time.

The large deck can hold up to 40 people seated for an intimate wedding or a large family gathering. We have 4 different wedding ceremony locations to choose from – point of the dam, jetty over the dam, gazebo and under our beautiful 100 year old tree.

Our marquee or tipi site is large and level, beautifully positioned next to our dam and can accommodate up to 150 guests depending on the number of tipis you choose or the size of your marquee.

Gold Coast Farm House is private and secluded, your guests will be so surprised when they come down the driveway and around the corner and look out across the dam and your beautiful reception.

The Farm House is the passion project of Gold Coast locals, Marcia and Jeff. With a flair for creativity and design, the pair were able to envisage the property’s potential, showcasing the natural surrounds and the classic Queenslander home.


Their experience with interior design and love for all things vintage led them to lovingly restore and transform the vast rural space into a stunning country wedding venue.


Originally bought as a family property, the Farm House quickly evolved into a wedding venue with requests for a unique and beautiful location for celebrations. The innovative pair jumped at the chance to share their stunning homestead with loved up couples for their special day.


Their successful creative enterprises keep them close to central Gold Coast business districts, however their dream is to retire to the Farm House to spend their days tending to the magical Hinterland property and living the idyllic country lifestyle.

While Marcia and Jeff are still very hands-on with maintaining and enhancing the property, they have handed over bookings and venue coordination to the wider Farm House team. Experienced and approachable, the team works closely with couples and wedding planners to ensure every detail is perfectly organised for a seamless ceremony and reception.


Well versed in weddings, the Gold Coast Farm House team will be there to ensure you have a magical and stress-free day. They are available every step of the way to answer any questions, offer suggestions, and work alongside your coordinators, no matter how big or small your celebration.


You’ll love your experience with the Gold Coast Farm House team and will cherish the wonderful memories of your magical day for years to come.

## FAQ's

#### Do we have to dine under the Tipi?

You can choose where you dine – you can use the Tipi as a dance floor or intimate dining space.

# Dog Friendly Wedding at the Farm House

Is your dog more than just a pet? Would you like to include them in your special day? At the Gold Coast Farm House we warmly welcome your pets and animals to the property. With acres of lush lawns and shady trees, your dog will have the time of their lives at our dog friendly wedding venue.

There are so many wonderful ways that your dog can be involved in your wedding day. Whether they are there for moral support or you create your whole wedding theme around them, dogs are part of the family and should be included in your special day.

We invite you to bring your dog out to the Farm House when you come to view the property. Simply let us know at the time of booking your viewing and we will prepare ourselves to welcome your furry friend. It will be our pleasure.

## Walk down the aisle

Make your pooch Dog of Honour or Best Dog, or simply have them accompany you down the aisle. Involving your beloved pet in your ceremony adds a fun and unique element to your celebration and you get to have your (other) best friend by your side.

## Cute little outfits

Nothing brings immense joy quite like dressing up a dog. Bow ties, flowers, tutus, or tuxedos, the options for adorning your furry friend are endless. Providing your pet is ok with it, dressing them in theme for your wedding will put smiles on the face of every guest and make yours a celebration to remember.

## Ring Bearers and Flower Dogs

While they say “never work with children or animals”, just choose one. Forget the kids and put your puppy to work as the ring bearer or flower dog. Get creative with pouches or boxes to keep your treasures safe as your pet delivers them to you and your love.

## Dancing dogs

Let your pup loose on the dance floor and have fun including your furry best friend in your festivities. First dance with your life partner? Get your pet in on the action and make it a moment you will never forget.

## Include them in photos

Having an experienced photographer capture your memories is essential for every wedding. Ensuring all your loved ones are represented in your photos is paramount. This is also true for your furry friends. Dogs and other animals add a whimsical feel to your shots and often provide the most entertaining candid images for your album.

Whether you include your own pet or you bring in unique animals for your special day is up to you. Your celebration will be more memorable and your photos will bring joy for years to come.

## Dog theme your wedding

Create your whole wedding theme around your dog. Why not? Dogs are cute and getting creative with imagery on stationery, desserts, signage, or gifts is a fun way to honour the special relationship you have with your pooch. Keep it subtle or go all out and express to your guests just how important your four-legged friend is to you.

## Other pets and animals

Is your best furry friend a cat? Bring them along! All fluffy family members are welcome. Surprise your beloved like one our beautiful brides did, as she walked her groom’s best feline friend down the aisle, carrying their rings (See above for the very special moment between the two). Animals are deeply loved and treasured that they deserve to be present in your most important life moments.

# You’re Engaged! Now What?

## Your First Steps to Planning the Wedding of Your Dreams

Valentine’s Day has always been about love – but this year, it came with a ring. 

If you’re newly engaged (congratulations!), you might be floating on cloud nine… while also quietly wondering, *“Okay, now what do we actually do?”*

Take a deep breath. Wedding planning doesn’t have to feel overwhelming. In fact, the very first steps should feel exciting, intentional, and, most importantly, *yours*. Here’s how to begin planning your wedding in a way that sets the tone for everything that comes next.

### Pause & Celebrate This Season

Before diving into spreadsheets and Pinterest boards, give yourselves permission to just *be engaged*. Go out for a Valentine’s dinner, pop a bottle of champagne at home, or take a quiet walk together and soak it all in.

This season is short and sweet, so don’t rush past it. The best weddings begin with couples who truly enjoy this moment together.

### Dream Together Before Making Decisions

Before booking anything, sit down and talk about what you both imagine for your day.

Ask each other:

- Do we want something intimate or a big celebration?
- Are we drawn to relaxed, countryside charm or something more formal?
- What moments matter most to us – vows, food, dancing, family time?

At the Gold Coast Farm House, we often see couples fall in love with the idea of a wedding that feels personal, warm, and effortlessly beautiful – where guests feel at home and every moment feels unhurried. Finding that shared vision early makes planning feel exciting instead of overwhelming.

### Set a Comfortable Budget (Yes, Even Now)

It may not feel very Valentine’s Day–romantic, but having an honest budget conversation early on is one of the kindest things you can do for yourselves.

Think of your budget as a guide – it helps you focus on what truly matters and choose vendors and venues that align with your priorities. Many couples find that choosing a venue like the Gold Coast Farm House, where the setting itself creates so much atmosphere, allows them to simplify other decisions.

### Choose Your Date (or Your Season)

Some couples are attached to a meaningful date, while others fall in love with a season. There’s no wrong choice – just what feels right for you.

Dreaming of long summer evenings, golden sunsets, and outdoor celebrations? Or maybe you love the idea of a softer autumn light, candlelit dinners, and cozy country romance. The Gold Coast Farm House offers beautiful backdrops year-round, making it easier to choose a date that suits your vision.

### Find a Venue That Feels Like Home

Your venue sets the tone for your entire wedding day. It’s where your guests will gather, where your story will unfold, and where memories will be made.

When you visit a venue, notice how it *feels*:

- Can you picture yourselves exchanging vows here?
- Does it feel welcoming and relaxed?
- Does it reflect your style and values?

Couples often tell us that the Gold Coast Farm House feels like stepping into a place where time slows down – a space that’s elegant yet comfortable, refined yet full of heart. That feeling matters.

### Enjoy the Journey

Wedding planning isn’t just about the final day – it’s about the journey you take together to get there. There will be decisions, laughter, moments of “does this really matter?”, and so much excitement along the way.

This Valentine’s Day marks the beginning of that journey. Wherever you choose to celebrate, let your wedding planning start the same way your engagement did – with love, intention, and a little magic.

# How to Host a Dry Wedding

## Planning a Dry Wedding or Low-Alcohol Celebration? You’re Not Alone.

There’s a growing shift in the world of weddings, and we are here for it. More and more couples are saying *“cheers!”* with sparkling water, mocktails, or zero alcohol champagne. Whether it’s for personal preference, cultural reasons, health, budget, or just a vibe, you don’t *need* alcohol to have an incredible, joy-filled wedding day.

At Gold Coast Farm House, we’ve seen first-hand how dry weddings (or low-alcohol celebrations) can be just as fun, meaningful, and memorable as any party with a bar tab. The best part? Our venue is totally flexible, so you can create the experience that feels right for you.

### Why Couples Are Choosing Dry or Low-Alcohol Weddings

There are plenty of reasons why couples are skipping the booze or scaling it way back, including:

- Personal or religious beliefs
- Health and wellness goals
- Budget-conscious planning
- A guest list that includes kids or teens
- Sober guests who deserve to feel fully included
- Wanting a clear head to fully soak in the day!

And sometimes, it’s as simple as *“we just don’t drink much and don’t feel like we need it.”* Love that!

### What a Dry Wedding Can Look Like at Gold Coast Farm House

The great thing about our venue? You have complete control over your drinks menu. You can provide your own alcohol – you just need RSA-certified bar staff to serve it responsibly. For dry or low-alcohol weddings, the options are still endless.

#### A few ways we’ve seen it done:

- **Self- served or staffed non-alcoholic bar setups** serving iced teas, lemonade, sparkling waters, or kombucha
- **Mocktail menus** served by a licensed mobile bar
- **Non-alcoholic beer, wine, and spirits** displayed beautifully and served by staff or self-served
- **Champagne-style towers** using non-alcoholic sparkling wine for a toast
- **Kombucha or specialty soda bars** run by vendors or friends

Just because you’re skipping the booze doesn’t mean you can’t still have a beautiful drinks experience.

### Bonus: You’ll Save Big on Your Bar Budget

Let’s be honest, alcohol can take a big slice out of the wedding budget pie. A dry or low-alcohol wedding often means:

- Less spent on alcohol purchases
- Fewer bar staff hours needed
- Lower glassware and bar hire costs
- Less waste and post-party cleanup
- You’re also most likely to get your bus bond back as there will be much less chance of guests feeling unwell on the drive home.

All that extra room in your budget? Spend it on florals, food, entertainment or that killer acoustic duo you’ve had your eye on.

### But… Will People Still Have Fun?

Short answer: Absolutely!

We’ve hosted dry weddings where guests danced all night, played lawn games at sunset, laughed until they cried, and made the most of every moment. When the energy is good, the planning is thoughtful, and the love is strong – you don’t need a full bar to have a full heart.

### A few fun additions that keep the party rolling:

- Live band or DJ with interactive sets
- Lawn games and mingling spaces
- Coffee cart or late-night dessert truck
- A sparkler send-off or acoustic circle around the firepit
- A sunset ceremony followed by a twilight garden party

### Not Fully Dry? Try a Low-Alcohol Hybrid

Some couples opt for a low-alcohol approach instead of going fully dry. For example:

- A champagne toast only
- One signature cocktail alongside non-alcoholic versions
- Beer and wine only, served during limited hours
- A full bar- but with a heavy focus on non-alcoholic options

Because Gold Coast Farm House is a BYO-friendly venue, you can bring your own alcohol and work with an RSA-certified vendor or mobile bar to serve it—your way, legally and responsibly.

### At the End of the Day, It’s About You

There are no rules anymore. Your wedding should reflect your values, your preferences, and your lifestyle. If that means no alcohol – *amazing*. If that means just a splash of bubbly at golden hour- *perfect*.

At Gold Coast Farm House, we’re here to support whatever vision you have – dry, low-alcohol, or full bar fiesta. Either way, it’s going to be unforgettable.

## Our Venue

Gold Coast Farm House is situated in the beautiful Numinbah Valley, just 30 km from Surfers Paradise. With 60 acres of rolling lush green grass lawn spotted with large native trees, 2 large spring fed dam’s and surrounded by national park, it’s the perfect location for a private wedding or function.


Tony’s Creek runs the full length of the property with 3 swimming holes, a secret glow worm cave and some small waterfalls, there are multiple bush walks starting from the property for you to explore. Alternatively, just sit back, relax and enjoy watching the native bird life in the trees and wallaby’s grazing .


The 100 plus year-old Queenslander sits at the top of the property overlooking the dams and has an uninterrupted view of Springbrook National Park and Page’s Pinnacle. With its high ceilings and beautiful leadlight windows, it truly feels like you have stepped back in time.

The large deck can hold up to 40 people seated for an intimate wedding or a large family gathering. We have 4 different wedding ceremony locations to choose from – point of the dam, jetty over the dam, gazebo and under our beautiful 100 year old tree.

Our marquee or tipi site is large and level, beautifully positioned next to our dam and can accommodate up to 150 guests depending on the number of tipis you choose or the size of your marquee.

Gold Coast Farm House is private and secluded, your guests will be so surprised when they come down the driveway and around the corner and look out across the dam and your beautiful reception.

### Mobile Food Trucks

Gold Coast Farm House has over 60 acres for you to set up your wedding. Having so much land available lends itself to festival-inspired weddings. Let your guests mingle and choose what they want to eat from your mobile food trucks. Here are some of our favourites:

#### The Italian Job

If you are after fabulous grazing platters and canapes then the Italian Job catering are the solution. Wood fired pizzas also are perfect for a winter or summer wedding and create a great atmosphere. Who doesn’t love the aroma of fresh pizzas. Crusty pizzas and breads, your guests won’t go home hungry.

#### Zicatel Mexican

Bring bold, authentic Mexican flavour to your event with Zicatel Mexican! Chef-inspired and rooted in over a decade of restaurant experience, Zicatel serves up fresh modern street-style Mexican cuisine with wood-fired tacos, shareable snacks, loaded nachos, burritos and more, all made with locally sourced produce and tortillas traditionally pressed daily from corn masa flour.


#### Alba Catering

Give your event a unique culinary twist with Alba Catering, where traditional Japanese techniques meet exciting, handcrafted dishes. Specialising in teppanyaki, dumplings, crispy karaage and more, Alba brings the theatre of Japanese cooking to life while ensuring great taste and quality at every turn. With a passion rooted in years of kitchen experience, this team delivers delightful flavours and a welcoming ambience that will have your guests talking long after the last plate is cleared.

#### The Spanish Catering

Proudly bringing authentic Spanish flavours to weddings, corporate functions and celebrations across the Gold Coast, this team crafts menus full of traditional tapas, paella, slow-roasted meats and grazing boards that showcase the best of Spanish cuisine. Whether you’re after savoury paellas cooked fresh on-site, elegant grazing spreads, or mouth-watering tapas to share, The Spanish Catering ensures every event is filled with vibrant taste, expert service and unforgettable moments.


#### Samba Catering

Samba will bring you the best Spanish Paella and bbq skewered options for your guests. Samba and Krystal have catered many times at our Gold Coast wedding venue that they loved it so much that they got married their themselves. They are also able to offer vegetarian options, but our favourite is their seafood paella.

#### Piante Plant Based Pizza

A passionate Naples trained pizza chef, excited to cater your next event with hand stretched vegan Neapolitan pizza. On a mission to make ‘Pizza for the Planet’. Focusing on traditional techniques, we truly believe our dough sets us apart. The pizza base is really the star of the show when it comes to Neapolitan pizza.

#### Deluxe Grazing

A boutique food catering business, Deluxe Grazing is here for all your event platter and catering needs. As innovative, creative, finger food enthusiasts, we are more than willing to work with you to design a grazing table, platter, hamper, picnic box or corporate catering for any unique celebration.

#### Notorious E.A.T

Inspired by the local diner and burger scenes in the USA, Notorious E.A.T brings you the most fun, flavourful and delicious burgers you can find on the East Coast. Try their menu of mouth-watering burgers or smaller sliders, nachos, loaded fries and stretchy mozzarella sticks. Don’t forget about their epic grazing tables and delicious desserts.

#### Graze By Akita

Our grazing tables are a beautiful display of fresh seasonal fruits, a selection of antipasto including a mouth-watering choice of cured meats, a variety of dips, a large selection of cheeses (OF COURSE!), nuts, dried fruits, biscuits/crackers, freshly baked breads and of course an irresistible selection of sweets!

We pride ourselves on making each event perfect for YOU – so we can customise any board to include the things you and your loved ones enjoy and exclude those that you don’t!

## Best Wedding Musicians on the Gold Coast

A standout element of every wedding is the music- it sets the tone for the night and can turn any event from bland to a raging party or a more intimate and romantic soiree.

Though this may be an overlooked part of wedding planning, curating the perfect playlist for your wedding day will ultimately ensure all your guests are dancing all night and telling everyone they know about how great your wedding was.

When it comes to picking your music there are many ways to go: Wedding Singers, Live Bands, DJs or a carefully curated playlist.

Don’t fret- we are here to help you host the best wedding on the Gold Coast with these great vendors.

Live music is often a favourite for weddings, these performers know how to get everybody up on their feet dancing, and how to appropriately slow the night down for your romantic first dance. Their talent and expertise is unmatched, knowing exactly how to create the perfect atmosphere for your special day.

### Chris Hutchison Weddings

If you’re looking for a musician with the whole package, Chris Hutchison is the guy for you. Playing his acoustic guitar, backed by amazing vocals, Chris will be there for you for your entire day – happily entertaining guests while your bridal portraits are being taken and well into your reception dinner. Also providing DJ services, Chris leaves no stone unturned to ensure your day is one to remember.

### Janda Events

Describing your ‘wedding soundtrack’ as the bookends to your wedding day, Janda events is known for making your wedding feel like a movie soundtrack- with all the right bits in the right place. This group of talented performers come in groups, duos and solos, making it easy for you to pick the voice that you imagine being the soundtrack for your day.


### Elevation Live Band

Whether you are looking for a solo smooth acoustic singer and dj, a party-starting duo or a rocking live band complete with drums, keys and bass, Elevation Live Band has your wedding music sorted. With an extensive list of popular classics and top hits in their repertoire, you won’t have any trouble creating the ultimate wedding playlist with these guys.


### Fletcher Music

Get the party started with Fletcher Music AU, Gold Coast’s premier open-format DJ! With over a decade of experience playing everything from dancefloor hits to retro favourites at clubs, weddings, corporate functions and events, Cory Fletcher knows how to read a crowd and keep guests moving all night long. Whether it’s tight mixes, surprising mashups or feel-good grooves, you’ll be tapping your feet and dancing for more.

### Birdman Randy & The Ivory Street Preachers

Turn your wedding into a concert with these party starters. With a high-energy live show, the boys mix jazz, soul, funk and more to create an epic tracklist that will have your guests wanting more. With two 45-minute sets, Birdman Randy is made to perform a show, so is best paired with an acoustic duo or DJ set.


### Kabezza

Kabezza is, unofficially, the funkiest wedding band on this side of the border. Frontman Liam Head knows how to entertain. After only recently performing with Robbie Williams down in Melbourne, he’s back in Brissy, and ready to groove. They established themselves early on with this jazz-funk sound after playing tunes like Stevie Wonder, Bruno Mars, Earth Wind Fire, DNCE, Kool & The Gang. Don’t be shy, Kabezza is for you if you want to dance.

### TOMMY SHEEHAN

Tommy is a seasoned performer at Gold Coast Farm House, who even got married there himself. When it comes to music you won’t find Tommy without his guitar, playing a soulful loved filled tune. If you are looking for the voice to light up your ceremony and cocktail hour look no further.


### BENNY HANNA MUSIC

If you are looking for a musician who can transform from ceremony to cocktails to reception and then again after the first dance then Benny Hanna is for you. Self-described as ‘bringing the vibe before vibes were a thing.’ His mix of your preferred ceremony songs with classic Michael Jackson, Amy Winehouse, Kings of Leon and many more for the reception.


### Jai Waterhouse

Want your reception to feel like the pumping dance floor of your favourite club, with the right mix of new school and old school tunes rolled up into one carefully thought out DJ set? Jai Waterhouse is your man. His skills on the decks are second to none and will ensure your guests are on the dance floor all night long.


### Doctor will love

Doctor Love brings that exclusive showmanship to your event. Entertaining restaurants, resorts, corporate and private events worldwide, providing professional and entertaining services second to no other. To hire his services is to hire the best, as there is no other entertainer like Doctor Love. Professional and Perfectionist is just some of the words to describe the entertainer. POINT OF THE LAKE

under the treEs

THE GAZEBO

Overlooking the farm beyond with bubbling creeks running nearby, this serene and quaint location provides an elegant backdrop for all types of  ceremonies from weddings, elopements and vow renewals.

Cross the love lock bridge as the trees open up to reveal a large field in which all your friends and family can sit to watch you exchange your vows.

Embrace the magical ambience of the gazebo and decorate with whimsical multi-coloured blooms or keep it neutral with soft creams and beiges. Explore the 60-acre farm surrounding for your wedding portraiture while your guests only take a short walk to the cocktail and reception space.

Get married on the water at the pier – located on one of our three natural lakes on the property. This gorgeous timber pier over the lake makes for the ideal backdrop for all sorts of ceremonies. Located closest to the house, this wedding ceremony package allows for the longest walk to the altar, for those looking to make a dramatic entrance.

Watch as the sun dances off the water and bounces in the large trees above to make for the most picturesque backdrop.

### The Big THings

This is where the party planning starts, you and your partner will need to come together to decide on a few important factors for your day.


- What is your wedding theme? (Bohemian, whimsical, modern)
- What is your colour scheme? (Neutrals, Bold and Bright)
- Who will be part of your bridal party? (Including ring bearers and flower girls)
- Who will your celebrant be?
- What venue will you choose?
- What day are you getting married?
- Who do you want to attend?

As the day draws closer it is important to pick out who you want to make your day perfect and selecting all your vendors to make your wedding vision come to life.

- Will you be having a sit-down or stand up dinner?
- Do you want guests to serve themselves buffet style, order from a food truck or have table service?
- What sort of cuisine are you looking for?
- Will you be providing alcohol or will you hire a mobile bar?
- Who will take photos and videos?
- Will you hire a wedding stylist?
- Who will be your florist?
- Who will be making your cake?
- Will you be having a live band or a DJ?
- What will you and the bridal party be wearing?
- Who will be doing your hair and makeup?
- Where will you get your invitations from?
- What portable bathroom do I choose? (We have this sorted)

### The Personal Stuff

Your wedding is made special by little moments and those who make them so special. Don’t forget to organise things to make your day extra special.


- How will you and your partner be arriving to the venue? (Limo, sports car, helicopter)
- Where will you and your bridal party be staying?
- Will you be gifting your bridal party anything?
- What rings will you be exchanging?
- Will you write your own vows? What will they say?
- Will there be a seating chart for guests?
- What are your wedding songs?
- How will the majority of your guests get to the venue? (We have this sorted)

### The Little Things

It’s time to make this wedding your own, small things like guests books, photobooths, and signature cocktails can turn this wedding into a party.


- Will you be having a guest book?
- Do you want a photobooth or polaroid cameras?
- Will you and your partner have signature cocktails for guest to choose from?
- What sort of wedding favours will you have?

- Where will each vendor park?
- Does each vendor have enough electricity?
- How will my guests know where to go?

- Who will set up and pack up?

- Where will my guest’s park?

**Wedding Date** / 21 October 2023

**Guest Numbers** / 120

**Theme** / Cocktail Garden Party

## The Proposal

Kelly and Keenan’s engagement story is proposal perfection. The adventurous pair had been travelling around Australia for a year in a 4×4. Making their way up the east coast, they pulled into a beautiful campsite in the Snowy Mountains. The picturesque setting was begging to be photographed, so they took time out to get some travel snaps. This had been a very normal occurrence on their journey with all the stunning locations they had visited.

This shoot, however, had a twist. Kelly turned around to see Keenan down on one knee in the traditional proposal position. The couple were both speechless, just looking at one another and laughing. Although Kelly was completely shocked by the proposal, she said it very fitting for that season of their lives; just the two of them in nature.

For Kelly and Keenan their wedding ceremony was the highlight of their day. Exchanging vows was their favourite part and just like the proposal, the couple considered their ceremony “just so us”. It was fun and relaxed with laughter, joy, and the occasional tear. Officiated by Lei Newell of LoveLei Weddings, the ceremony was perfect for the loved up pair.

The formalities began with four gorgeous “Flower Grandmas” peppering the aisle with rose petals before Kelly’s arrival. The delighted crowd laughed, cried, and applauded the ladies as they set the scene for a joyful celebration. We have created their very own Flower Grandma gallery with these stunning captures of their moment.

Kelly walked down the aisle to an acoustic version of ‘Have I Told You Lately’ by Van Morrison. Kelly and Keenan chose a beautiful soft, spring colour palette for their October wedding. The bride wore a stunning white lace gown from Ella Moda. The groom and his bridal party were kitted out in natural linen suits. Bridesmaids wore satin slip dresses in soft muted tones of cream, steel blue, and pewter.

The flowers by Arctic Fox were absolutely stunning. A gorgeous spring mix of different colours and shapes made for a beautiful floral display. The floral arbour and bouquets were bursting with tones of blue, apricot, pink, purple, and white. While the flowers were busy and bright, they did not detract from the bride, groom, or the wedding party. Instead, they worked harmoniously with the soft colour palette and served as a gorgeous accent piece that brought all the other elements together.

The Lane Creators captured the event with stunning photographs. Utilising the grounds of the Farm House, their portraits and group shots were beautifully framed by the natural vegetation and green surrounds of the property.

## The Reception

Kelly and Keenan wanted their reception to feel like a “boujee backyard bbq”. The cocktail garden party offered just that, with a fun and relaxed vibe that encouraged conversation, dancing, and a really good time. The couple loved the concept of “build your own wedding” offered by the Gold Coast Farm House venue. This allowed them to completely customise their wedding celebration, choosing everything from caterers to tipis, music and layout.

The flexibility of the Farm House venue meant they could have their dream cocktail wedding with food trucks, a shot wall, and a Margarita bar. Guests were treated to Mexican street food from California Tacos, while Fern Vintage Van poured the drinks. Bassline Studios Band kept the party going with a lively set that ensured the dancefloor was always full and festive.

While the couple didn’t do a first dance, they did make a grand entrance to ABBA’s “Gimme Gimme Gimme”. Spraying Champagne as they came into the tipi, Kelly and Keenan were greeted with a rousing applause and cheers from their guests. They performed a choregraphed dance that set the tone for the evening’s festivities, as did the pouring of the Champagne tower.

## Advice

Kelly and Keenan’s wedding was a roaring success. They wouldn’t have changed a thing. The cocktail style reception was a hit, as it allowed them to mingle with everyone in a fun, party atmosphere. One of the key elements was the Margarita bar, which, along with the shot wall, they highly recommend to other couples.

Their advice is to plan your day around exactly what you want and not worry about any expectations. Creating a celebration that is a complete reflection of you and your love, is essential. After all, the most important thing to consider is that you are marrying your best friend.

The marriage of Monique and Jesse was a beautiful celebration of understated elegance. The couple, dressed in their finest, declared their love for each other under the 100 year old tallow-wood tree at The Gold Coast Farm House. Formalities quickly turned to frivolous fun as the pair celebrated with family and friends in true festival style. **Names** / Monique & Jesse

**Wedding Date** / 26 August 2023

**Guest Numbers** / 140

**Theme** / Bohemian Festival

## THE PROPOSAL

Jesse surprised Monique with an unexpected proposal in New Years Eve 2019/20. After 9 years together, there were no real plans between the pair to get engaged or married. To Monique’s surprise, Jesse did have a plan, working behind the scenes with Monique’s sister to have a ring made. The couple joined family in Wollongong where they met for a low key family photo shoot. By the river bank at happy hour, Jesse popped the question. Groups of picnic goers applauded the newly engaged lovers and wished them well. Unfortunately, a global pandemic hit a few months later, so they had to wait a few more years for their big day.

## THE CEREMONY

For many couples, the wedding ceremony is usually the highlight of the day, and for Monique and Jesse this was no exception. Jesse’s favourite moment was seeing Monique walk down the aisle to “Simply the Best”, performed by Lily Budiasa. Jesse waited for Monique’s arrival under the 100 year old tallow-wood tree. The breathtaking natural backdrop was decorated with a stunning floral arrangement by Arctic Fox. The couple chose a close friend to introduce them and facilitate the ceremony, with a representative from the Gold Coast Registry Office on hand to officiate the marriage. Monique’s highlight was standing under the canopy with Jesse as dappled light peeked through the branches, highlighting the colours of the beautiful blooms.

**” I remember it like a dream.”** 

## THE LOOK

While the theme of the wedding was Bohemian Festival, Monique and Jesse chose to stand out from the crowd with their elegant attire. Jesse was decked out in a sharp, black tuxedo while Monique was dripping in gold. Monique’s wedding gown was lovingly made by her mother based on her own design. It was a gold sheath dress that Monique customised throughout the day and night. For the ceremony, she wore a sheer, long sleeve overlay adorned with pearls. Chic and sophisticated this gave a more formal look for the nuptials. Monique then removed this and added sheer half sleeves and a neck tie in the same sheer pearled fabric.

Her blooms were a playful mix of pinks, purple, and ivory, reflecting the colours of her bridesmaid’s gowns. Hair was a simple, elegant braided bun, with natural, glowing makeup showing off Monique’s stunning natural beauty.

## THE RECEPTION

The couple had their hearts set on an outdoor wedding with tipis. When they saw the triple tipi set up at the Farm House, they knew it was their venue. The tipis reflected the festival and bohemian vibe they were hoping for and accommodated the large guest list. Farm favourites, The Italian Job, catered the wedding reception, serving their delectable wood fired pizzas for guests to devour. The spectacular Airstream Bar provided drinks for the celebration, as well as an amazing visual display for photo opportunities.

A mix of musicians entertained the revelers through each stage of the celebration. From an acoustic soloist for the quieter moments, to a live band and DJ for the party, music played an integral part in the couple’s big day. Monique’s brother accompanied the DJ on saxophone as he made is way through the party. This was a highlight and something they would suggest other couples incorporate into their celebration.

One of Jesse’s most memorable moments was their entrance into the reception. With the music pumping, Monique and Jesse were welcomed by family and friends cheering and celebrating. The night was filled with dining, drinking, and dancing under the lights. During the night, a guest encouraged the couple to take a little breather and spend a quiet moment together. With the view of the raging party under the tipis, Monique and Jesse reflected on everything that led them to this moment.

**“It was everything we wanted”**

Don’t compromise! Monique’s advice to other couples is to know what you want and make it happen. # The Wedding Day Fix List

## Wedding Mistakes You’ll Avoid by Getting Married at The Gold Coast Farm House

*And a few stress-induced meltdowns while we’re at it.*

Let’s be real, planning a wedding can feel like juggling flaming bouquets while blindfolded and trying to please 100 people you love (plus a few you had to invite). But here’s the good news – at Gold Coast Farm House, we’ve seen it *all* (and we mean all), and we’ve created a venue experience designed to take the chaos out of your celebration.

So, here’s a playful look at a few common wedding mistakes you won’t have to worry about when you get married at our hinterland haven.

### Mistake #1: Booking a Beautiful Venue with No Backup for Bad Weather

**But Not Here:**

At Gold Coast Farm House, we know the Gold Coast skies can be unpredictable. That’s why we have weather contingencies ready to go – whether that means moving your ceremony under our tipis, marquee, or adjusting your run sheet like the flexible legends we are. Rain? Wind? We’ve danced in it before.

*Bonus: Cloudy skies = dreamy photos. Your photographer will thank you.*


### Mistake #2: Getting Stuck with a Vendor You Don’t Actually Like

**But Not Here:**

We’re proudly BYO when it comes to vendors. You want a wood-fired pizza truck and a vegan gelato cart? Go for it. Want your cousin’s band to play acoustic covers of Taylor Swift all night? We support your choices. You choose the vibe, the people, the food, and the fun. No locked-in packages, no cookie-cutter rules.

*And if you need help deciding? Our Preferred Vendor list is stacked with talented, tried-and-true legends.*


### Mistake #3: Feeling Rushed From Ceremony to Reception

**But Not Here:**

Our couples often tell us their day actually *felt* like a day, not a whirlwind. With ample time on the property and flexible layouts, you can enjoy golden hour photos, cocktail hour on the lawn, and still have time to soak it all in (with a drink in hand and your dog nearby).

*Speaking of which…*


### Mistake #4: Not Being Able to Include Your Pets

**But Not Here:**

Your furry best friend deserves a seat at the party (or at least a cute bowtie and a cameo in the ceremony). Gold Coast Farm House is proudly pet friendly, and we’ve hosted everything from dapper dachshunds to floral-wearing Frenchies.

*We’ve even seen a couple of alpacas and one very photogenic cat.*


### Mistake #5: Having to Leave the Property for Good Photo Spots

**But Not Here:**

You won’t need to pile into a car and race to a cliff edge or a city street. Our property is a photographer’s dream, with built-in backdrops like tall grass, wide paddocks, lakeside reflections, and our much-loved Tallow Wood tree.

*We’ve even seen couples do golden hour portraits at the swing or sneak in a quiet moment behind the house – with breathtaking results.*


### Mistake #6: Spending Your Whole Day Inside a Function Room

**But Not Here:**

Let’s face it: if you’re getting married in the Gold Coast Hinterland, you want to *experience* it. That means open skies, fresh air, lawn games, and maybe even barefoot dancing under the stars. Whether you choose the deck, the tipis, or the marquee, the vibe is always outdoor elegance – with room to move, breathe, and celebrate.

*Also: sunsets. Our skies never miss.*


### Mistake #7: Not Having a Supportive Team Behind You

**But Not Here:**

Our team is small, experienced, and deeply invested in your day. We’re not a faceless venue with a clipboard army – we’re hands-on, heart-led, and (according to our reviews) “absolute lifesavers.” Whether it’s helping you wrangle guests or lending you a pair of wedding rings (yes, it happened), we’ve got you.

### TL;DR?

You bring the love – we’ll bring the location, flexibility, backup plans, photo ops, good vibes, and *yes* – room for your dog.

At Gold Coast Farm House, we’re here to make sure your wedding is memorable for all the right reasons.

*Ready to book your no-stress, all-vibes celebration? Contact us today to organise a viewing or learn more about our packages.*

# Our Favourite Ceremony Spots

Choosing the perfect spot to say *“I do”* is one of the most meaningful decisions you’ll make when planning your wedding. At the Gold Coast Farm House, we’re lucky to offer a variety of stunning ceremony locations, each with its own vibe, backdrop, and styling potential. Whether you’re dreaming of an intimate moment beneath the trees or a big reveal on the open lawn, we’ve got the space to match your vision.

Here’s a look at our favourite ceremony spots and a few styling ideas to bring them to life.

### The Tallow Wood Tree

If there’s one spot that makes hearts skip a beat, it’s our century-old Tallow Wood tree. Towering, twisted, and filled with character, it creates a breathtaking natural altar for couples who want something earthy and unforgettable.

Use rugs and runners to create an aisle and designated spot for your ceremony. Dress the tree in floral garlands, draped fabrics, hanging lanterns, and more. Or simply arrange floral pieces around the ceremony area, entrance and along the sides of the aisle. Decorate the aisle chairs with flowers, ribbons, or whatever you heart desires. There is so much you can do to make the Tallow Wood Tree unique to your wedding.

### The Point of the Lake

Our serene lakefront setting offers an atmosphere of calm and elegance, with water views that add a magical softness to any ceremony. The national forest creates a natural backdrop for your nuptials, and looks incredible in your wedding photos.

You are only limited by your imagination when it comes to decorating The Point of the Lake and making it yours. Use props like arch ways, arbours, plinths and of course, florals! Dress the space to suit your style and make the most of the natural beauty surrounding this popular ceremony location.

### The Pier

The Pier is an stunning ceremony location with the weathered jetty acting as a fixed backdrop surrounded by lily pads, and gorgeous greenery. Choose to share your vows on the grass in front or take it up on the boards, like a small stage. Once again, you have the freedom to decorate the space in your way by adding arbours, potted greenery, candles, and flowers galore.

The Pier is also a perfect location for bridal portraits with so many vantage points for your photographer to shoot you from. Drone footage and photos are a must if you are using The Pier as part of your Farm House wedding. The results are truly spectacular.

### the Gazebo

For a wedding ceremony location that is ready to go with its own decor, platform, and structure, The Gazebo is ideal. Grape vines wind their way around the white timber frame, with festoon lights adding a festive and whimsical addition to late afternoon ceremonies or evening photographs.

Of course, you are welcome to add more decor to this space, with garlands of florals, draped fabric, urns or candelabras. The choice is always yours at the Gold Coast Farm House.

### Other Locations

With such an expansive property and a little creativity, there really are endless ways to use the Gold Coast Farm House for your wedding ceremony. Our outdoor locations are absolutely breathtaking, but the weather can have alternative plans for your big day. Fortunately, there is always a plan B available, and when love is the priority, any location is the perfect place to exchange your vows.

We’ve hosted beautiful and heartfelt wet weather wedding ceremonies on the deck of the Farm House, under the Tipis and Marquees, and in the great outdoors (with help from our stash of clear umbrellas).

No matter which ceremony space you choose at the Gold Coast Farm House, the most beautiful thing about it is that it’s yours for the day. We’ve seen couples bring every kind of style to life, from soft romantic and boho to bold and modern.

**Your love story is unique and your ceremony space should reflect that.**

Whether you want an intimate vow exchange on the deck, a dramatic entrance through the paddock, or a sunset “I do” under the tree, we’re here to help you make it unforgettable.

Whether it’s sweet and slow, funky and fun, or completely choreographed, that first spin under the stars (or fairy lights) says so much about your style as a couple.

# Emily & Joshua | A Garden Party

Some weddings are grand. Others are deeply personal. And some, like Emily and Joshua’s, are a perfect mix of both. With soft pastels, heartfelt vows, and the natural beauty of the hinterland as their backdrop, their intimate wedding on the deck at the Gold Coast Farm House was a joyful, intimate celebration of love, family, and sentiment.

**Names** / Emily & Joshua

**Wedding Date** / 14 April 2025

**Guest Numbers** / 30

**Theme** / Garden Party

### A Private Moment to Begin Forever

For Emily and Joshua, the highlight of the day happened before the ceremony even began.

*“Our first look and private vows at the swing provided a perfect, intimate moment. We got to spend time together and share our promises privately before standing in front of our family and friends.”*


Set beneath the trees, this quiet moment allowed them to be fully present with each other before the rest of the day unfolded – a choice more and more couples are choosing to embrace. The ceremony then continued at the point of the lake with their nearest and dearest in attendance.

### Garden Party Whimsy Meets Natural Beauty

With a garden-party-inspired aesthetic, the couple leaned into soft, whimsical tones – lavender, dusty blue, sage, and bursts of pink, orange, and green filled the day with colour and playfulness. The bridesmaids wore mismatched pastel gowns, and the florals were lovingly arranged DIY-style, adding an extra layer of charm and personality.

*“The natural beauty of the venue paired perfectly with our playful, garden-party-inspired style.”*


Their reception took place on the deck – an ideal setting for a micro wedding – with sweeping views of the hinterland and an atmosphere that felt cosy, relaxed, and joy-filled.

### A Proposal Full of Joy

The journey to “I do” began with a surprise beachside picnic.

*“Joshua planned the sweetest proposal at one of our favourite Gold Coast beaches. On a quiet hill with the ocean behind us, he got down on one knee. I was bursting with joy—jumping up and down, shouting ‘yes!’”*


Private, personal, and heartfelt – just like their wedding day.

### Why They Chose Gold Coast Farm House

*“We attended a viewing and knew instantly—it was perfect for us. The beauty of the property and the relaxed, welcoming feel sealed the deal.”*


With flexible spaces, ceremony-only or full-day options, and the freedom to create a day that reflects *you*, the Gold Coast Farm House was everything Emily and Joshua were looking for in a wedding venue.

### Moments to Treasure Forever

Among all the joyful memories, one moment stands out:

*“Standing hand in hand, saying ‘I do’ surrounded by breathtaking scenery and the people we love most. It’s etched in our hearts forever.”*


### Advice to Future Couples

*“Your wedding day is about celebrating your love, so make it truly yours. Choose food you love (we highly recommend pizza—it’s fun and always a hit!). Most importantly, soak it all in—it goes by so quickly.”*

Make your pooch Dog of Honour or Best Dog, or simply have them accompany you down the aisle. Involving your beloved pet in your ceremony adds a fun and unique element to your celebration and you get to have your (other) best friend by your side.

## Cute little outfits

Nothing brings immense joy quite like dressing up a dog. Bow ties, flowers, tutus, or tuxedos, the options for adorning your furry friend are endless. Providing your pet is ok with it, dressing them in theme for your wedding will put smiles on the face of every guest and make yours a celebration to remember.

## Ring Bearers and Flower Dogs

While they say “never work with children or animals”, just choose one. Forget the kids and put your puppy to work as the ring bearer or flower dog. Get creative with pouches or boxes to keep your treasures safe as your pet delivers them to you and your love.

## Dancing dogs

Let your pup loose on the dance floor and have fun including your furry best friend in your festivities. First dance with your life partner? Get your pet in on the action and make it a moment you will never forget.

## Include them in photos

Having an experienced photographer capture your memories is essential for every wedding. Ensuring all your loved ones are represented in your photos is paramount. This is also true for your furry friends. Dogs and other animals add a whimsical feel to your shots and often provide the most entertaining candid images for your album.

Whether you include your own pet or you bring in unique animals for your special day is up to you. Your celebration will be more memorable and your photos will bring joy for years to come.

## Dog theme your wedding

Create your whole wedding theme around your dog. Why not? Dogs are cute and getting creative with imagery on stationery, desserts, signage, or gifts is a fun way to honour the special relationship you have with your pooch. Keep it subtle or go all out and express to your guests just how important your four-legged friend is to you.

## Other pets and animals

Is your best furry friend a cat? Bring them along! All fluffy family members are welcome. Surprise your beloved like one our beautiful brides did, as she walked her groom’s best feline friend down the aisle, carrying their rings (See above for the very special moment between the two). Animals are deeply loved and treasured that they deserve to be present in your most important life moments.
