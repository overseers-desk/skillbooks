# ABOUT US

The brainchild of owner and creator Cherie Uzan, The Valley Estate has been years in the making. As Cherie grew up in ‘The Valley’, it has been a long-held dream to create a one-of-a-kind venue in a location she holds close to her heart.

A kaleidoscope of creativity, Cherie’s vision for The Valley Estate draws upon grand European and industrial influences. Lavish and luxurious, the venue is a landmark wedding and events destination designed to take you on a journey that will both excite and inspire.

Conceived with weddings in mind, The Valley Estate has been crafted to allow couples to celebrate their once-in-a-lifetime moment in a truly unique space, all the while revelling in the beauty of the hinterland backdrop.
