Subject: Free circus tickets for your Upper Coomera families, this Thursday to Sunday

Iona,

CHARC Performance has a big top up on the lawn at Historic Rivermill for the school holidays, and I have free tickets to the show for your Play Matters families.

There are seven sessions, from this Thursday to Sunday:

- Thursday 24 September, 1pm
- Friday 25 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday 26 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday 27 September, 11am and 2pm

To take them up, reply with roughly how many families and I will send the code and the booking link straight back for you to pass around. Each family books the session that suits them, so there is nothing to organise at your end.

They are for your own families rather than for posting publicly. The seats are not advertised anywhere; opening week goes to local organisations that bring families together, one at a time, and yours is one of them.

If the other Gold Coast groups you coordinate would use them too, say so and I will count them in.

If a group would rather come along together instead, next week suits that better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you.

Historic Rivermill is a heritage venue on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan.
