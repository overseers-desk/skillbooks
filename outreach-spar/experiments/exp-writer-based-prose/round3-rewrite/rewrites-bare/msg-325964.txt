Subject: Free circus tickets for Beaudesert Probus — the big top opens Thursday at Mount Nathan

Hello Helen,

I'm Priyanka Das and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the heritage venue at Mount Nathan on Beaudesert Nerang Road — the one with the cafe and the animals down by the river.

A circus has its big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, and I have free tickets set aside for Beaudesert Probus members and their grandchildren. The show is a family one, WANTED – A Cirque Heist.

All you need to do is reply with a rough number. I'll hold that many seats in the club's name and send you a booking link to pass around. Members then book whichever performance suits them, so there's no outing for the club to run.

There are seven performances:

- Thursday 24 September — 1pm
- Friday 25 September — 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday 26 September — 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday 27 September — 11am and 2pm

Thursday is two days away, so an approximate number now is more use to you than an exact one later.

In case you're wondering why these are free: the same performances sell at full price and the seats aren't advertised anywhere. They go to local organisations picked by name, and your club is one of them. They're for members and their families rather than for a public notice, so the link is best kept within the club.

If the club would rather come along together instead of in ones and twos, the following week suits that better. Name a day and I'll ask our manager about holding seating.
