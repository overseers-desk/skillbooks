```
1. nowhere: count every definite/possessive expression used as if already shared ("the booking details", "the code", "our manager", "the notice a bus needs") that the email has not introduced at that point; later introduction does not excuse it; a coordinated list in one slot counts once.

3. false: recipient picked/chosen/selected/named, or seats presently held in their name; seats/tickets said not on sale or unavailable to the public; season end other than 4 Oct; free offer for a show outside 24-27 Sep. Conditional future holds ("if you reply I will hold") are not counted.
4. unsupported: no source either way - prior-contact claims, "not advertised anywhere", recipient's bylines/groups/street, distances or travel times absent from sources, "farm"/"heritage farm"; sender's name and role never counted.
5. sender: sentences whose content is the sender's side - why the offer is made, how the recipient came to be written to, what is not on offer or not for sale, the sender's own situation; greeting, sign-off and organisation-introduction excluded; anything telling the reader what they get, must do, by when or from whom is reader-side.
6. words: subject line plus greeting plus body prose, signature block excluded.
```
