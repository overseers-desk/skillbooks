Subject: Free circus tickets for your members — first show this Thursday

Hello,

A circus has put up its big top on our lawn for the school holidays, and I can give free tickets to Oxenford Men's Shed members and the families they bring. The first show is this Thursday, 24 September. Reply with a rough number and I will send you a booking code and a link to pass around the shed.

I am Priyanka Das, partnerships at Historic Rivermill — the riverside venue at Mount Nathan with the cafe, horse riding and farm animals. The show is WANTED – A Cirque Heist.

There is no outing to organise. Seven shows are on in the opening run, and each person books whichever one suits them, so there is no bus to fill:

- Thursday 24 September, 1pm
- Friday 25 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday 26 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday 27 September, 11am and 2pm

Since free usually comes with a catch: these seats are not on sale at any price. They go to a short list of local organisations we picked by name, and the shed is on it. They are for your members, their families and the people the shed looks after, so please send the link round the shed rather than posting it publicly.

If the shed would rather come along together instead, the week after suits a group better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you on it.

One last thing. We wrote to this address in April about a group outing and did not hear back. If outings are someone else's job at the shed, please pass this on or send me their address.
