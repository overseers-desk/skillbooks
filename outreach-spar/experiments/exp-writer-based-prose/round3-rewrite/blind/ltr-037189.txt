CHARC Performance has a big top up on the lawn here at Historic Rivermill for the school holidays, and I can give Beaudesert Probus members free seats at the opening shows, grandchildren included if they have them along. The first show is this Thursday 24 September at 1pm, so this one would need to go round by email or phone rather than wait for your October meeting.

All I need back is a rough number of members. I will hold that many, and if the number grows after that, tell me and I will add to it.

Members then book whichever show suits them, so there is no outing to run and no bus to fill. For any members who would rather not book on a website, send me their names and how many seats each, and I will book those seats in the club's name myself.

One thing to watch. The same shows are on sale to the public at full price, and these seats are not advertised anywhere. They are for Probus members and their families, so the details are better going out to the membership directly than onto a public page.

We are the riverside venue at Mount Nathan, out along the Beaudesert Nerang road on the site of the old arrowroot mill. We went through the clubs near us and wrote to them one at a time, and Beaudesert Probus is on that list.

If the club would rather come together and watch the one show as a group, the season runs through to Sunday 4 October. Tell me a day next week and I will come back to you about seating.

If outings are someone else's job at the club, send me their name and I will write to them directly.
