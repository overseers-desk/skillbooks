Subject: Free circus tickets for Men's Shed members and their families — opens Thursday

Hello,

A circus company, CHARC Performance, has put a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, and I have free tickets to the opening shows for Oxenford Men's Shed members and the families they bring.

We're Historic Rivermill at Mount Nathan — the riverside place on Beaudesert Nerang Road with the cafe, the horses and the alpacas, on the old arrowroot mill site. The free seats cover the seven shows from opening night through to Sunday:

- Thursday 24 September, 1pm
- Friday 25 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday 26 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday 27 September, 11am and 2pm

If the shed wants them, reply with a rough number and I'll send you the booking code and the link to pass around. Nobody has to run an outing — each family books the show that suits them. The first show is two days away, so even a rough guess back soon would help.

The tickets are for your members and the families with them, rather than for putting up on Facebook or anywhere public. These seats aren't advertised and aren't on offer to the public at any price — we went through the local organisations that bring families together and wrote to them one at a time, and yours is one.

If the shed would rather come along together as a group instead, the following week suits that better. Tell me a day that works and I'll see what can be held.

One thing worth knowing: Lia Movsisyan from our office wrote to the shed in April about a group outing here and we didn't hear back, so this may be the first of ours anyone has read. If someone else looks after the social side, could you pass it on to them.
