Subject: Free circus tickets for Pine Lake residents, first show Thursday

Dear Corey,

My name is Priyanka Das. I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage riverside venue at Mount Nathan, about 30 minutes inland from central Gold Coast.

A circus company, CHARC Performance, has put a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays. The show is a family one called WANTED – A Cirque Heist. I am writing to offer free tickets to it for Pine Lake residents and the grandchildren who visit them.

The opening week runs from this Thursday to Sunday, seven performances in all:

- Thursday 24 September, 1pm
- Friday 25 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday 26 September, 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday 27 September, 11am and 2pm

Reply with a rough number and I will send you a booking link and a code. Residents use it to book whichever performance suits them. There is nothing to pay.

Nothing needs arranging at your end. A line in the newsletter or a notice on the board is enough, and residents sort themselves out from there. No bus, no sign-up sheet, and no list for your office to keep.

One thing I would ask. Please keep it inside the village, so residents, the families who visit them, and your staff. I would rather it did not go onto a public page or a community Facebook group.

If residents would sooner travel together, that suits better the week after. A bus run needs more notice than this week allows. Tell me a day that works and I will come back to you about seating.

The tickets are for the opening week only, so a reply in the next day or two is what I need.
