Hello,

My name is Priyanka Das. I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the riverside cafe at Mount Nathan on Beaudesert Nerang Road, the one with the horses and the alpacas.

A circus company called CHARC Performance has put a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays. The show is a family one, WANTED – A Cirque Heist. I have free tickets to the opening shows for your members and the families they bring, grandchildren included.

The first show is tomorrow, Thursday 24 September, at 1pm. The free tickets cover all seven shows through to Sunday:

- Thursday at 1pm
- Friday at 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday at 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday at 11am and 2pm

If the shed would like some, reply with roughly how many and I will send back a booking code and a booking link. You pass those around the shed. Each family then books whichever show suits them. Nobody has to arrange a bus or take names.

There is no cost and no catch. These tickets are not on offer to the public at any price. We went through the local organisations that bring families together and wrote to each one, and yours is one of them. The only thing I ask is that the code stays inside the shed, so not on Facebook or anywhere public.

If the shed would rather come out together as a group, next week suits that better. Tell me a day that works and I will see what seating can be held for you.

Lia Movsisyan from our office wrote to the shed back in April about a group visit and we did not hear back, so this may be the first of ours anyone has read. If you are not the right person for it, could you pass it to whoever looks after the social side?
