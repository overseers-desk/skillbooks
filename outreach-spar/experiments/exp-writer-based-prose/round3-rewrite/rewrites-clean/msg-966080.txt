Subject: Free circus seats for your members, circus opens Thursday

Dear Helen,

A circus company has its big top up on our lawn at Mount Nathan for the school holidays, and I have free tickets to it for the Probus Club of Beaudesert, for members and their grandchildren. I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the riverside venue out along the Beaudesert Nerang Road towards Nerang.

All I need from you is a rough number. Reply with how many members you think might take them up, a guess is fine, and I will hold that many in the club's name and send you the booking details to pass around.

The show is called WANTED – A Cirque Heist and it opens this Thursday 24 September. Seven sessions go up between Thursday and Sunday:

Thursday 24 September, 1pm
Friday 25 September, 4pm and 7pm
Saturday 26 September, 4pm and 7pm
Sunday 27 September, 11am and 2pm

Members book whichever one suits them, so there is no bus to arrange and nothing for you to run.

These seats are not on sale at any price and they are not advertised anywhere, so they are yours to pass around inside the club rather than to post publicly.

If the club would sooner come along together, the following week gives you the notice that needs. Tell me a day that works and I will come back to you about seating.

Kind regards,
