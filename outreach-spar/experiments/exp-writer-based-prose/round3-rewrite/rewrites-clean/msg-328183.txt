Subject: Free circus tickets for Pine Lake residents, this Thursday to Sunday

Hello Corey,

I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, at Mount Nathan in the Gold Coast hinterland, and I have free tickets for Pine Lake residents and the families who visit them. A circus company has put a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays. The show is WANTED – A Cirque Heist, and the opening week runs this Thursday 24 September to Sunday 27 September.

There are seven performances. The daytime ones are Thursday at 1pm, Sunday at 11am and Sunday at 2pm. The rest are at 4pm and 7pm on the Friday and the Saturday.

Reply with the sessions that suit and roughly how many people, and I will send you the booking link and a code that takes the price to nil, for you to pass on. Residents book their own session, so there is no bus to arrange and nothing to organise at your end.

Anyone who comes out will find a heritage property with a cafe, horses and farm animals as well as the circus.

The free seats are not advertised anywhere, and I am offering them to a small number of villages near us. They are for Pine Lake residents and their families, so the code is best kept inside the village rather than going out on social media.

If this week is too short a run-up, or a village outing would suit you better than residents making their own way out, the week after gives the notice a bus needs. Tell me a day and I will come back to you about seating.
