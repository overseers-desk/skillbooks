Subject: Free circus seats held for your members, Thursday to Sunday at Mount Nathan

Hello,

A circus has put up its big top on our lawn for the school holidays, WANTED – A Cirque Heist, and it opens this Thursday, 24 September. I have free tickets for Oxenford Men's Shed members and the families they bring. Reply with a rough number and I will send you the booking code and the link to pass around.

I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a riverside venue at Mount Nathan with a cafe, horse riding and farm animals.

There are seven shows in the opening run: Thursday 24 September at 1pm, Friday 25th and Saturday 26th at 4pm and 7pm, and Sunday 27th at 11am and 2pm. Each person books whichever one suits them, so there is no outing to organise and no bus to fill.

The seats are not on sale at any price. They go to a short list of local organisations we picked by name, and the shed is on it. They are for your members, their families and the people the shed looks after, rather than for posting publicly.

If the shed would rather come along together, the week after suits a group better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you on it.

If outings are someone else's job at the shed, please pass this on or send me their address.
