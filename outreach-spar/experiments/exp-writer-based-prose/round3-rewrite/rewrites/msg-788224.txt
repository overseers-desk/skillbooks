This is a Kahneman's system 2 question.

Methodology bearing on it: **Writing for a reader** (News editor's hat for the subject; Forward only; One idea per sentence; LHD; SDT), **Deciding what to build** (DDL/Don't overdo: no facts invented where the source is silent), **Handling the request** (Email: this is a draft, not a transmission), and **VGA** (two writings).

Writer-based working notes:

Reader is Chinnu, owner of a Queensland family publication, cold. Questions in their head, in the order they arrive: (1) what is being offered to me and to my readers, (2) when, and how little time have I got, (3) what do I have to do, (4) is there work for my team in running it, (5) who is this. The original opens on (5) and reaches (3) in the last paragraph.

Deadline shape: today Tuesday 22 September, show opens Thursday 24 September. "This Thursday" carries the urgency without inventing a reply-by date the source does not hold.

Facts that must survive because they are needed to act: Priyanka Das; Historic Rivermill, Mount Nathan, Gold Coast hinterland, riverside, cafe/horses/farm animals; CHARC Performance; big top on the lawn for the school holidays; WANTED – A Cirque Heist; opens Thursday 24 September; family pass of four for Chinnu, any show in the opening run; five further passes for readers, same seven shows; the seven times (Thu 1pm, Fri 4pm and 7pm, Sat 4pm and 7pm, Sun 11am and 2pm); not advertised, not on public sale, going this week to a small number of Queensland family publishers; reply with a show, booking details follow; giveaway winners book their own seats, no list, nothing to collect; Eventbrite URL; signature block.

Cut: "Your readers are the families the show was made for." It tells what the substance already shows (a school-holiday circus at a farm venue), and SDT says an artefact does its work through its substance.

Arithmetic kept, not invented: one pass for Chinnu plus five for readers.

Putting on news editor's hat for the subject. The subject reader is an owner triaging an inbox who may read nothing else. The impactful answer is the editorial asset (five passes their readers can win) plus the clock (Thursday). The architect's version would name the venue and the project; the editor's names what lands for them.

Subject: Five circus passes for your readers: big top opens Thursday

Hello Chinnu,

There are five family passes held for Families Magazine to give away to readers, and one for you, at a circus that opens this Thursday.

I am Priyanka Das, from Historic Rivermill at Mount Nathan, a riverside venue in the Gold Coast hinterland with a cafe, horses and farm animals. The circus company CHARC Performance has raised a big top on our lawn for the school holidays. The show is WANTED – A Cirque Heist, and it opens on Thursday 24 September.

Your pass admits a family of four at any show in the opening run: Thursday 1pm, Friday 4pm and 7pm, Saturday 4pm and 7pm, Sunday 11am and 2pm. The five reader passes cover the same seven shows.

The passes are not advertised and are not on public sale. They go this week to a small number of Queensland family publishers.

Tell me which show you would come to and I will send the booking details. If you run the five as a reader giveaway, the winners book their own seats, so there is no list to send me and nothing to collect at your end. Readers buying their own tickets can do that at https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/wanted-a-cirque-heist-tickets-2000810786924.

Priyanka Das
Partnerships, Historic Rivermill (Colourful.land Pty Ltd)
partnerships@rivermill.au
