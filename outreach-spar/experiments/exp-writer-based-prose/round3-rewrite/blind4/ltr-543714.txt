Subject: A circus opens Thursday at Mount Nathan, free seats for Beaudesert Probus

Hello Helen,

My name is Priyanka Das. I handle partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the heritage venue at Mount Nathan on Beaudesert Nerang Road, with the cafe and the animals down by the river.

A circus has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays. It opens this Thursday 24 September. The show is a family one, WANTED – A Cirque Heist, and I have free tickets for your members and their grandchildren.

Seven performances run from Thursday through to Sunday 27th: Thursday at 1pm, Friday and Saturday at 4pm and 7pm, Sunday at 11am and 2pm. Tell me roughly how many and I will put seats aside in the club's name, then send you the booking link to pass around. Members book the performance that suits them, so there is no outing for the club to run. The tickets are for the club's own members and their families rather than for a public notice.

These free seats are not advertised anywhere, and the same performances sell at full price. They go to local organisations picked by name, and the club is one of them.

If the club would rather come together, the following week suits that better. Tell me a day and I will ask our manager about holding seating.
