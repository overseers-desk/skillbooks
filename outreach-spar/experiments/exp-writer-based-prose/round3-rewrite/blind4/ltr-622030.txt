Subject: Free circus tickets for Play Matters Gold Coast families, shows from Thursday

Dear Iona,

My name is Priyanka Das and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage venue on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan, with a cafe, horses and farm animals. We wrote to you at the end of March about a complimentary visit for Gold Coast playgroup coordinators and heard nothing back, so this may be the first you are reading of us.

A circus company has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, and I have free tickets to the family show, WANTED – A Cirque Heist, for the families in your groups. The season opens this Thursday, 24 September. Reply with a rough number of families and I will send you the booking details to pass around.

There are seven shows between Thursday and Sunday: 1pm on Thursday, then two on each of Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Six of them run from Friday afternoon onwards, so a message you forward tomorrow still reaches families in time to book. They pick whichever show suits them, and there is nothing for you to organise beyond passing it on. The tickets are for your own families and members rather than for a public post. They are not advertised anywhere. We are offering them to a handful of local organisations that bring families together, and yours is one.

If opening week is too short a run-up for your organisers, or your groups would rather come along together, the week after suits that better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you on what can be held.

Kind regards,
