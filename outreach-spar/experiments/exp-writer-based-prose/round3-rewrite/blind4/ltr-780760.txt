Subject: Free circus tickets for your Upper Coomera families, Thursday to Sunday

Iona,

We wrote to you from this address on 31 March, inviting Gold Coast playgroup coordinators out to visit Historic Rivermill, and did not hear back. This is a shorter one. Historic Rivermill is a heritage venue on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan, and CHARC Performance has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays.

I have free tickets to the show for your Play Matters families. The first one is this Thursday 24 September and there are seven through to Sunday the 27th. Thursday at 1pm, Sunday at 11am and Sunday at 2pm are the daytime ones; the rest are at 4pm and 7pm on the Friday and the Saturday.

Reply with roughly how many families and I will send the code and the booking link straight back for you to pass around. Each family books the session that suits them, so there is nothing to organise at your end.

They are for your own families rather than for posting publicly. The seats are not advertised anywhere; opening week goes to local organisations that bring families together, one at a time, and yours is one of them.

If the other Gold Coast groups you coordinate would use them too, say so and I will count them in.

If a group would rather come along together instead, next week suits that better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you.
