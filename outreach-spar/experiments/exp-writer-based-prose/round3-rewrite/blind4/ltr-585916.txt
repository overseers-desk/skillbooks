Dear Chinnu,

My name is Priyanka Das and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage riverside property at Mount Nathan in the Gold Coast hinterland, half an hour from central Gold Coast. There is a family pass of four in your name for any of those seven shows, and five more passes for you to give away to Families Magazine readers. Tell me the day that suits you and I will hold the seats and send you the booking details.

If you run the giveaway, your post can carry the ticket listing at https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/wanted-a-cirque-heist-tickets-2000810786924, and the booking details for your winners come from me to you, so they stay off the page. Whoever wins books whichever of the seven sessions suits their own week, so there is no group to bring and nothing to arrange.

The cafe and the animals are open before the daytime shows, so families can make a morning of it as well.
