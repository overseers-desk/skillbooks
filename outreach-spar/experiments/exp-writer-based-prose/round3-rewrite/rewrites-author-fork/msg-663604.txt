Subject: Free circus tickets for your playgroup families, first show Thursday

Hello Iona,

I am Priyanka Das. I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the place on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan, out past Nerang. A circus company, CHARC Performance, has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, running a family show called WANTED – A Cirque Heist. I have free tickets to the opening shows for the families in your groups.

The first show is tomorrow, Thursday 24 September, at 1pm. Friday and Saturday run at 4pm and 7pm, and Sunday at 11am and 2pm. Seven shows over the four days, and the tickets are good for any of them. We list the show at about ninety minutes. The daytime ones are Thursday at 1pm and Sunday at 11am and 2pm, which are the easier times with babies and toddlers. Being the school holidays, it takes in the older brothers and sisters too.

If you would like them, reply with a rough number of families and I will hold that many. I will send you a booking code and the link, and each family books the show that suits them. The tickets cost nothing, there is nothing to sign, and there is no outing for you to run.

One thing I would ask. These seats are not advertised and are not on offer to the public at any price. We went through the local organisations that bring families together and wrote to them one at a time, which is how yours came to be on the list. So the code is for your own families rather than for the Facebook page or anywhere public.

If one of your groups would rather come along together instead, the following week suits that better. Tell me a day and I will come back to you about seating.
