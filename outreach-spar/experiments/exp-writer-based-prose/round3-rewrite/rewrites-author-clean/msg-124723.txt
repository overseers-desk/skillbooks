Subject: Free circus tickets for your Upper Coomera families, from this Thursday

Iona,

I have free tickets for your Play Matters families to a circus that opens this Thursday, 24 September. I'm Priyanka Das, and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage venue on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan. We wrote to you on 31 March about a free visit for playgroup coordinators and didn't hear back.

The show is WANTED – A Cirque Heist, a family circus in a big top on our lawn for the school holidays. There are seven shows, from Thursday to Sunday 27 September. The daytime ones are Thursday at 1pm, and Sunday at 11am and 2pm. The others are at 4pm and 7pm on Friday and Saturday.

If you'd like them, reply with roughly how many families. I'll send you a code and a booking link straight back to pass on to them. Each family books whichever show suits them, so there's nothing for you to organise. The tickets are for your own families, so please keep the code off public pages.

These seats aren't advertised. We're giving the opening week to local groups that bring families together. If the other Gold Coast groups you coordinate would like tickets too, tell me and I'll include them.

If a group would rather come together, the following week works better for that. Tell me a day and I'll come back to you.
