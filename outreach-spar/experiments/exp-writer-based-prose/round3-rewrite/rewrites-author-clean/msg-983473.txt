Subject: Free circus tickets for Oxenford Men's Shed members and their families, from this Thursday

Hello,

My name is Priyanka Das. I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the riverside cafe at Mount Nathan on Beaudesert Nerang Road, with the horses and the alpacas.

A circus company, CHARC Performance, has put up a big top on our lawn for the school holidays. The show is called WANTED – A Cirque Heist, and it is a family show. I have free tickets to the first week for your members and the families they bring along, grandchildren included.

The first show is this Thursday, 24 September, at 1pm. The free tickets cover all seven shows up to Sunday:

- Thursday at 1pm
- Friday at 4pm and 7pm
- Saturday at 4pm and 7pm
- Sunday at 11am and 2pm

If the shed would like some, reply with roughly how many and I will send back a booking code and a link. You pass those around the shed, and each family books whichever show suits them. Nobody has to arrange a bus or collect names.

The tickets cost your members nothing, and they are not being offered to the public. We picked out local organisations that bring families together and wrote to each one. Please keep the code within the shed, so not on Facebook or anywhere public.

If the shed would rather come out as a group, the following week is better for that. Tell me a day that suits and I will see what seating we can hold for you.

Lia Movsisyan from our office wrote to the shed in April about a group visit, and we did not hear back, so this may be the first of our emails anyone has seen. If you are not the right person, could you pass it to whoever looks after the social side?
