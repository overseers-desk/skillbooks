Reply with a rough number of members and I will hold that many. If the number grows after that, tell me and I will add to it. If the club would rather come together and watch the one show as a group, the season runs through to Sunday 4 October. Tell me a day next week and I will come back to you about seating.

If outings are someone else's job at the club, send me their name and I will write to them directly.
