## 1. Rubric

```
Unit: a sentence is one body sentence; subject line, greeting, sign-off and signature block are excluded from sentence counts; words = subject + body, signature block excluded.
M1 nowhere: count every occurrence of a definite/presupposing expression whose referent the email has not introduced at that point (later introduction does not rescue it); bridging from an already-named entity is allowed; the reader's own body named as a bare definite ("the club", "the group") counts; a plain assertion of prior contact does not.
M2 scores: each sentence gets confusing/surprising/interesting 0–5 in reading order; surprising = the reader wonders why they are being told this; report means and the count of sentences at surprising 4–5.
M3 false: recipient picked/chosen/selected or seats held in their name; seats/tickets said not on sale or not available to the public; season end other than 4 October; free offer for a show outside 24–27 September.
M3 unsupported: any claim with no source either way — distances or travel times not in the sources, "heritage farm", prior-contact episodes (one per episode); sender's name and role never counted.
M4 sender: sentences whose content is why the sender offers, how the recipient came to be written to, what is not on offer/not advertised/not possible, or the sender's own situation; what the reader gets, must do, by when or from whom is reader-side and not counted.
```
