I am Priyanka Das, partnerships at Historic Rivermill, the heritage riverside venue hosting it at Mount Nathan, half an hour inland from the Gold Coast at the foot of Tamborine Mountain.

There are five family passes of four to give away to your readers, and a sixth for you or someone from The Wanderbug if any of you are up this way. The passes are held in The Wanderbug's name rather than issued as a public code. Tell me how many you want and which shows, and I will send you the booking details for the winners rather than for the post. Readers buying their own seats can do that at https://www.eventbrite.com.au/e/wanted-a-cirque-heist-tickets-2000810786924.

The free seats are not advertised anywhere. They go to a small number of writers and local organisations whose readers and members would use them. A day's notice is short, so run it on whichever of your channels suits, in whatever form you like.
