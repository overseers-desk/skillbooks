I have free seats at the opening-week shows for your residents and the families who visit them.

Reply with a rough number and I will send you a booking code and the link, for you to pass on. Residents book whichever show suits them, so there is no bus to arrange, no forms, and no group to get together at short notice.

Seven performances open the season: Thursday 24 September at 1pm, Friday and Saturday at 4pm and 7pm, and Sunday at 11am and 2pm. The daytime ones suit visiting grandchildren best. The code is for Oak Tree Victoria Point's own residents, their families and your staff, so I would ask that it stay off social media and off any noticeboard the public can read.

If a group of residents would rather come together instead, tell me a day the week after and I will ask about holding seating for them.
