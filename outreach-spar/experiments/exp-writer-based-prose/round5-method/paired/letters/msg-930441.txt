Subject: Family pass, big-top circus north of Byron, opens Thursday

Sarah,

A circus company has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, and I have a family pass of four at it, for you and whoever you bring.

I am Priyanka Das and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage venue at Mount Nathan in the Gold Coast hinterland. We sit on six hectares beside the Coomera River, with a cafe, horses, alpacas and goats. The property milled arrowroot a century ago.

The show is WANTED – A Cirque Heist. Seven performances run from this Thursday 24 September through to Sunday the 27th: Thursday at 1pm, Friday and Saturday at 4pm and 7pm, then Sunday at 11am and 2pm. Reply with the one that suits and I will send the booking details.

Opening week means you would see it before the audience does. The season then runs on to 4 October, so there are shows still standing if you want to write about it. I can sort out whatever access you need on the day. Entry to the grounds is free on any day, so the horses and the rest of the animals are there to walk around before or after the show.
