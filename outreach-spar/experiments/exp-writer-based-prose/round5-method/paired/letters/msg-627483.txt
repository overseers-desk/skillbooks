Subject: Free circus tickets for Canungra RSL members, shows start Thursday

Dear Jacqueline,

My name is Priyanka Das and I look after partnerships at Historic Rivermill, a heritage venue on the Coomera River at Mount Nathan, along the Beaudesert Nerang Road from Canungra. We have a cafe, horses and farm animals, and the property is open to visitors. My colleague Lia Movsisyan wrote to this address in April about a morning out here for your members. We have no reply to it on our side. That may just be where it landed, and it needs no answer now.

This one is a much smaller thing to say yes to. CHARC Performance has a big top up on our lawn for the school holidays, and we have free tickets to their family show, WANTED – A Cirque Heist, set aside for local organisations. They are yours for the sub-branch's members and their families if you want them. Seven shows run from tomorrow, Thursday 24 September, through Sunday, starting with the one o'clock on Thursday. Reply with a rough number and I will send you a code and a booking link to pass around. Do not be shy with the number. They are for your members and their families rather than for posting publicly.

There is nothing for you to run. No bus, no roll call, no forms: each family books whichever show suits them. If the sub-branch would rather come together as a group, the following week suits that better, so tell me a day and I will come back to you.

Kind regards,

Priyanka Das
