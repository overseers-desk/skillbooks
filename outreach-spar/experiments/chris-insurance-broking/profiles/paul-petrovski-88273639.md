## Institutional context (cargo / marine / property / liability exposure)

PCFS is a classic under-hedged mid-market freight-forwarder profile:

- **Cargo and marine exposure.** Air and sea freight forwarding means continuous cargo liability for shipments in their care, custody, and control (CCC). Forwarder liability under the BIFA/IFCBAA-style conditions or the Hague-Visby regime, plus optional marine transit cover on top, is core program content. Customs clearance adds errors-and-omissions exposure on tariff classifications and origin declarations.
- **Warehousing.** Secure, 24-hour monitored warehouse — property cover on the building and fit-out, stock-throughput policy on goods stored on behalf of customers (bailee cover), plus public liability.
- **Transport / domestic fleet.** "Door to door" domestic transport service implies a light/heavy commercial motor fleet — motor fleet, goods-in-transit, and third-party liability lines.
- **Fumigation and quarantine services.** AQIS-accredited fumigation introduces chemical-handling and professional-indemnity exposure (incorrect fumigation can spoil cargo or fail biosecurity, triggering customer and regulatory claims).
- **Multi-site footprint.** Sydney HQ, Melbourne, Brisbane — three-state schedule drives coordination complexity in the property and liability sections.
- **Membership networks.** Globalia cargo-insurance partnership (Redkik) is visible to Paul; FTA provides policy-wording industry position papers and advocacy on customs-broker PI requirements. Paul sits at the intersection of both.
- **Sponsorship discretionary spend.** NRL partnership (St George Illawarra Dragons), two football club sponsorships — indicates the firm has been consistently profitable enough to underwrite sports-marketing commitments of non-trivial size, suggesting program premium capacity beyond bare-minimum mid-market.
- **In-house claims fluency.** Silvana Petrovski's 17-year finance / insurance-claims background inside PCFS means the firm processes claims with internal expertise — the conversation is not broker-to-novice.

A corporate program for PCFS would typically attach: Marine Cargo + Forwarder's Liability (or combined Marine Transit), Property (building + contents + stock-throughput), Warehouse-keepers' legal liability, Motor Fleet + Goods-in-Transit, Public & Products Liability, Professional Indemnity (customs / freight-forwarder errors), Management Liability / D&O (owner-director + family-connected executive), optionally Cyber (customer data, EDI with consignors, customs-portal credentials). Paul is the decision-maker for all of this.
