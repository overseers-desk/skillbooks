## Institutional context (club sector, regulated licensed venue)

Licensed registered clubs in NSW sit under the Registered Clubs Act 1976 and are regulated by Liquor & Gaming NSW. Typical insurance program for a mid-sized licensed bowling club with bistro, function space, gaming floor and outdoor greens covers: property (building, contents, stock, gaming machines), business interruption tied to liquor/gaming licence continuity, public and products liability (food, beverage, function guests, court/green injuries), management liability / D&O (unpaid volunteer board, statutory compliance exposure), cyber (member databases, CTP cashless gaming systems), workers comp (icare mandated in NSW), and commercial motor for any shuttle or courtesy transport. Under a five-year growth plan that explicitly anticipates facility expansion in a rapidly-densifying catchment, the program's property sum-insured, BI indemnity period, and construction-phase cover (defects, contract works) will all be live questions at successive renewals. The CEO/GM is the program owner in a club of this size; the board endorses but does not run the broker relationship.
