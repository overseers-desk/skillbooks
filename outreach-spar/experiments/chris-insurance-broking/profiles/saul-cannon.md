## Institutional context — risk exposures the role controls

1. **SOCI Act critical-infrastructure designation.** POM is a designated critical-infrastructure asset; board-level cyber risk reporting is mandatory. ASD responded to 143 CNI incidents in FY22–23.
2. **Nov 2023 DP World cyberattack.** DP World (POM's largest stevedore tenant) was shut down at Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Fremantle via a Citrix vulnerability; ~30,000 container backlog nationally; declared a "nationally significant incident". POM's own systems were not compromised but its operational revenue depends on tenant uptime — a textbook contingent-business-interruption and supply-chain dependency exposure.
3. **30-year / $1B+ capital programme.** 2055 Development Strategy; new two-berth 14,000-TEU-capable terminal by 2036. Property, engineering, marine construction, and cost-escalation exposures on a long tail.
4. **Single city-gateway concentration.** As the principal Victorian container gateway, business interruption at POM has national supply-chain consequences — a concentration risk that shapes how BI and contingent BI are structured.
5. **50-year concession governance.** D&O and sponsor-level risk sit in the Lonsdale Consortium stack; the CEO's interface with institutional owners is atypical governance context compared with a listed or government-owned operator.
