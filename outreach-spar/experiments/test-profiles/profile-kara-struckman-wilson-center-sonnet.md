## What they have said publicly

**On XZ Utils and the dilemma of open source security (Wilson Center blog post, 2024, URL: https://www.wilsoncenter.org/blog-post/how-secure-open-source-software-dilemma-xz-utils-backdoor):** Struckman framed the XZ backdoor as exposing a structural dilemma: nearly all modern systems depend on open source software, making substitution impractical, yet the software is maintained by unpaid volunteers who cannot be expected to perform industrial-grade security review. She argued there is no quick or easy solution and that approaches must balance responsibility between an overwhelmed unpaid community and organisations that consume open source at scale without contributing review capacity. The article was analytical and policy-oriented, not technically focused.
