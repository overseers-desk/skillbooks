## Current role

**Assistant Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering** at Purdue University (Elmore Family School of ECE), West Lafayette, Indiana (September 2020 -- present, approximately 5 years 7 months). Researcher at Purdue's Center for Education and Research in Information Assurance and Security (CERIAS) and affiliated faculty with the Center for Resilient Infrastructures, Systems, and Processes (CRISP). ## Supply chain security ecosystem position

Torres-Arias occupies a distinctive structural position in the software supply chain security ecosystem. He is not a single-project contributor but holds leadership roles across three complementary CNCF/OpenSSF projects that together form the core provenance and signing infrastructure:

- **in-toto** generates provenance attestations (the metadata describing what happened during the software supply chain).
- **TUF** secures the distribution of software updates (ensuring the artefacts that reach users have not been tampered with).
- **Sigstore** provides identity-based signing and transparency logging (establishing who signed what and making it discoverable).

These three projects are the technical substrate on which any provenance certification regime would depend. Torres-Arias has explicitly described their interaction: "in-toto framework to create the evidence, then you can have Sigstore to discover it, and then you can have SPIFFE to tell you who's who." The campaign's provenance certification offering sits at the layer above these tools: it would certify that the provenance metadata they generate meets defined standards. This makes Torres-Arias's work the direct technical precursor to the campaign's core offering.

His co-authorship of NIST SP 800-204D (2024) demonstrates willingness to translate technical work into normative guidance documents -- the same mode of engagement the campaign's working groups require. His KubeCon EU 2025 panel on "the philosophical debate" of supply chain threats signals growing comfort with framing these issues beyond pure tooling.
