# Machine cadence in a personal document

**The situation.** A research residency at an AI lab; the form's answer on why the candidate wanted to work there. The employer asked applicants to write it themselves and use AI only to refine it, and a screener reads very many. A sentence that sounds machine-made costs the candidate twice: as prose, and as evidence of who wrote it.

**What the AI wrote.** A clipped first winner, which judges later described as clipped. Sentences turned round so that a place or a thing is the subject (constructed, same build): "The kitchen is where that shows, and a year of the kitchen is how I learned to see it." A closing commitment after the point was made, of the build "This is the one I want to stay with." A capping line after his own last sentence, which reached the submitted answer merged, not removed.

**What the candidate said.** "It smells so AI." He had never seen this pattern much before AI: "x is …, and y is …", its hallmark an opening on an unusual noun, of a kind no parent would say to a child. He removed the closing commitment, because AI habitually adds a closing statement when what should be said is already said; it is rubric behaviour, and shows the text was AI-edited.

**What it missed.**
- A turned-round sentence gives a small fact the cadence of a verdict. A person says it forwards (constructed): "I learned to see that by working in a kitchen for a year."
- A closing line after the point adds a summary the reader did not need, and marks the text as a model's.
- The judges rewarded the capping line (without it, every room said his sentence had no point), so ranking by the same model keeps the cadence. The cure that removes both faults is to fold the point into the sentence it caps.

**Instances.**
- The first winner, clipped throughout.
- Two turned-round sentences.
- The closing commitment.
- The capping line, merged but not removed.

**The rule it produced.** `../hire-methodology.md`, E 3, **Voice**: "speak-like with the identity `human`, for machine cadence, capping lines and turned-round sentences". E 5 says judges do not hear dryness; it does not warn that they reward the model's own cadence, which is why this check cannot be left to them.

**To re-run it.** Give a fresh agent the methodology and an invented draft answer in the candidate's hand that ends on a plain wish, and ask it to refine. Give the refinement to a fresh reader: "which sentences sound written by a model?" It passes if the reader names none, and the refinement has no sentence whose subject is a place or a thing doing a verdict's work, and no closing line that restates the paragraph.
