# The candidate's words swapped or removed

**The situation.** Tidying the candidate's spoken and typed text for a research residency at an AI lab, in the CV and on the form. His words carried choices a near-synonym does not: what the reader gets, what he is, what he wants the reader not to miss.

**What the AI wrote.**
- He put his goal as finding out how to make the agents serve a business well; the CV put it as learning that.
- He gave his residence at the grain of a group of countries; the AI proposed the country, and argued against his wording.
- His own draft said he wanted to work among the best minds in the field; the refinement removed it, noting the removal as one line in a list.
- He named a well-known backer in two places; the AI cut it to one, on a fresh reader's note.

**What the candidate said.** "To learn" sounds as if he does not care what the employer gets; "to find out", and so to improve. Of the residence: use his version. "So you decided to completely remove the smart people stuff, right?" The plain statement that he wants to be among the best should return. Of the backer: a forgivable repeat, and a detail he would rather they did not miss. "Return that."

**What it missed.**
- "To learn" says what the candidate takes. "To find out, and to improve" says what the employer gets.
- His residence wording was what he had chosen to tell a recruiter about ease of hire. The country was one he had not chosen to name.
- His motive, removed as what most applicants write, deserved to be kept and made specific, which the AI then did on his word.
- A repeat he wants seen is a choice. Removing repetition is not a reason to overrule it.

**Instances.**
- "Find out" made "learn".
- The residence made more specific than he gave it.
- The wish to work among the best removed.
- The backer cut to one mention.

**The rule it produced.** `../hire-methodology.md`, R: **The candidate's wording changes only for a reason the candidate can see.** "Every such change is listed in `examine.md` with its reason, so it can be taken back."

**To re-run it.** Give a fresh agent the methodology and an invented draft in the candidate's hand: "My aim is to find out why night-shift errors cluster, and to fix them. I trained under two of the best clinicians in the field, and I name Dr Osei again below because he taught me most." Ask for a tidy. It passes if "find out" and the repeat survive, or each change is listed with its reason for him to accept. A silent substitution is a fail.
