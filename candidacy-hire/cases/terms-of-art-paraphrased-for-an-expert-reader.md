# Terms of art paraphrased for an expert reader

**The situation.** The opening of a CV for a post managing about thirty engineers at a cloud vendor, read by a technical director. A protocol the candidate designed, and his agent work, both had exact names in their fields. The writer worked under a plain-words habit meant for general readers.

**What the AI wrote.** Paraphrase where the field has its term (constructed, same build, from another field): "which lets one hospital's records be read safely inside another hospital's system", where an expert would write of federated access under the source's own controls. Elsewhere, ordinary words for things the trade names.

**What the candidate said.** He asked whether using only the commonest words was intentional. Expressions like that one usually have a technical equivalent, and each sentence should be checked for whether it had been dumbed down for a news reader or would be better in the right terminology. Later he asked for a round swapping every ordinary expression that has an industry equivalent.

**What it missed.**
- For an expert reader, the field's term is evidence that the writer knows the field. A paraphrase reads as an outsider explaining to an outsider. The AI conceded the plain wording was intentional, and wrong for this reader.
- A style rule is a rule about a reader, and it was applied without asking who this reader was.
- Plain words can mislead an expert. In the terms round, two plain words were read in another field's sense.

**Instances.**
- The opening's phrasing of the protocol, the agent work and the bank work.
- The ordinary expressions left in the later CV, swapped at his request.

**The rule it produced.** `../hire-methodology.md`, H, **The readers' own subject and terms**: "Read what the employer has published on the matter the candidate will speak of. An expert reader expects the field's terms." E 3, **Voice**: this-guy-aint with the reader's own trade as the identity, "for a plain word where the trade has its term".

**To re-run it.** Give a fresh agent the methodology, an invented note in the candidate's words ("my system lets a clinic's data stay put and still be searched by the network"), and a posting for a health-data engineering director. Ask for one CV sentence. It passes if the sentence uses the field's terms, and the agent, asked to check it, compares each plain phrase with the term an expert would use.
