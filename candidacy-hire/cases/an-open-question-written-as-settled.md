# An open question written as settled

**The situation.** A CV for a research residency at an AI lab. The candidate's value to a research reader was his open problems, among them which of several methods he had tried for checking agents' work helps most. He wanted them left open, as a tease the reader would ask about at interview.

**What the AI wrote.** A finding stated as settled, and a single root offered for all the failures (constructed, same build: "The delays had one root: no one knew who owned the order"). One of the methods he had tried, explained at length as the answer. Of a catalogue with many categories: "The failures fall into kinds", followed by three.

**What the candidate said.** The method he had described was not the method but one of those he had tried. He did not want to speak as if he had discovered it; he wanted it as a tease. He could say that one had helped most despite its limits, which does not close the matter: "your danger is to write it as a closed conclusion". And of the three kinds: why write it as three categories rather than "take three, for example"?

**What it missed.**
- A closed finding leaves a mentor nothing to do. The AI conceded as much.
- One method written as the method claims a discovery he did not claim, and erases the comparison between methods, which is the interesting part.
- "Fall into kinds", then three, tells the reader the catalogue has three.
- Open is not vague. An earlier "in certain ways", naming nothing, read as a guess; a tease needs its members named.

**Instances.**
- The finding stated as settled in the first CV.
- One root for all the failures.
- One method written as the method, and again at risk in the next round.
- Three kinds given as the whole set.

**The rule it produced.** `../hire-methodology.md`, R, **Write in the voice of someone who knows**: "Keep open what the candidate left open. 'Three of the categories, for example' where the writer was about to write 'the failures fall into three kinds'. 'Has helped most so far' where the writer was about to conclude." And I: "A thing the candidate leaves open is left open; that choice is content."

**To re-run it.** Give a fresh agent the methodology and an invented note: a candidate tried checklists, pairing and recorded handovers to cut errors at shift change; pairing has helped most so far; the comparison is open. Ask for one CV sentence. It passes if the sentence names the three, says which has helped most and leaves the comparison open, and a fresh reader asked "is this settled?" says no.
