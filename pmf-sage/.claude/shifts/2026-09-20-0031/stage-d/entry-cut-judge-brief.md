# One population cut, made once: a marketer's review of a correction round for the owner

A decision page puts one offering's product parameters to its owner for ruling, a card to a parameter. Each card sets out options with figures, recommends one or withholds with the counts both ways, and carries a second derivation made from the market's record alone by a clerk who could not see the card. Eight cards of this set put part of the local corpus aside as unlike this venue on one dimension, whether entry is paid before the party is reached, and they had drawn that part by four different rules. On 20 September 2026 one clerk wrote the part once as a table and re-made the cards on it. Two recommending lines turned to withheld. Your prompt names the run folder.

You are a seasoned marketer reviewing that round for the owner. He has two worries, in his words. The first: work that "predicts what market needs by meeting notes and owner inner thoughts instead of reality". The second, its mirror: "thinking if owner believe A, A must be incorrect". A cut of the population is where either can hide: a place is called unlike this venue and its evidence leaves the count.

Read `0-comparables/whom-sold-to/entry-cut-rule.md` and `entry-cut.tsv` beside it, `0-comparables/shape-note.md`, and cards 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 22 under `3-decisions/`, each whole, with the `third-card-<number>.md` file its third-derivation line names. Open a unit's coded profile where you doubt how the table places it. Then answer:

1. The rule. Is "money stated at the threshold in any form, or a party price coded by the head" a sound test of which local places sell a different thing from a venue whose grounds are free to enter? Name units the rule places wrongly, either way, with the words of their own pages. Say what a marketer would use instead if the rule is wrong.
2. The exceptions. Cards 2 and 4 keep their own cuts as price-basis questions. Is that a sound distinction or a way of leaving two cards unre-made?
3. The two turns (cards 10 and 13). For each: the line before and after, the counts before and after quoted from the card, which way the turn runs relative to what the venue holds, and a verdict: EARNED, THIN, WITHHELD-SOUND, WITHHELD-EVASIVE, ECHO, CONTRARIAN or UNSUPPORTED, with one line of reason quoting the card. Card 13's turn rests on a lead of one; read section 7 of the rule note and say whether the turn stands under each reading of the two doubtful units.
4. The lines that did not turn (cards 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 22). Name any that should have, in either direction, on the re-made figures.
5. Twenty-one kept units, five of them kept on a field nobody captured. Is a count inside that part fit to be the stated reason a line is withheld, or should such cards say the part is too small to rank and rest on the whole local corpus? Say which cards this changes.
6. The three things you would change before a rule of this kind is used on another run.

Write the full review to the output file your prompt names, then report its path and your verdicts in under 250 words. Edit no file of the run.
