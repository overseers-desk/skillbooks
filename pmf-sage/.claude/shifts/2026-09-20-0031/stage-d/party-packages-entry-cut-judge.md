# The entry cut, judged

## The short of it

One clerk did the right thing in the right order: he found four rules doing one job, wrote the job down once, crossed the rules against the coded fields, and re-made every card on the result. The cross-tabulation in section 3 of the rule note is good work, and the finding under it is real. The earlier list set aside four units the shape note calls *shared* with this venue and left in thirteen of the fourteen that state money at their own threshold. That correction needed making.

The rule that replaced it does not measure what it says it measures. It says it finds the operators who, like this venue, take no money before the party is reached. What it actually finds is the operators whose pages **published a number**. Of the 21 units it keeps, eleven publish no price basis at all; of the 24 it sets aside, none. The cut is a filter on publication volume wearing the clothes of a filter on trade. Every card in this set whose runner-up option is "the page says nothing" gains units from it, and the two lines that turned are both lines where that runner-up is in the pair.

One turn survives on a different reason than the one written on it. The other does not survive at all.

---

## 1. The rule

**Not sound as written.** The test is "money stated at the threshold in any form, or a party price coded by the head". Four defects, in the order that matters.

### It selects for silence

The first limb fires only when a page prints a charge at the gate. The second fires only when a page prints a per-head figure. A page that prints neither because it prints almost nothing is kept automatically. Measured, not asserted: of the 21 kept units, **eleven** carry `V7_basis_stated` = none in the clerk's own table: the City of Gold Coast community venues, Country Paradise Parklands, Currumbin RSL, Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary, Event Cinemas, Gold Coast Turf Club, Jolly Jumps, Old Macdonald's, Paradise Country, The Star and Fenek Farms. Of the 24 set aside, **none** does. Card 2 already names what most of these are: "Four of the nine publish a room and never mention money (F2), which is the adult function-room shape rather than a decision about the unit."

So the kept 21 is, disproportionately, the club, hall, casino, turf club and cinema function-room shapes plus four mobile operators. Those are precisely the pages that publish no length (card 10), no days or hours (card 9), no host (card 12) and no rule about what may be brought (card 22). The cut hands every "nothing published" row a block of units, and it does so for a reason that has nothing to do with gates. Card 22's re-measure shows the mechanism undisguised: seventeen of the twenty-one kept units are silent on what the buyer may bring, against 23 of 45 over the whole corpus.

### The first limb counts a waiver as a charge

`V25_party_adults_entry_separate` is, in the shape note's own words, "Accompanying adults' entry is charged **or waived** separately". A page saying adults come in free sets it, and the rule then sets that unit aside as one that states money at the threshold.

Trevena Glen Farm is the case, and it is the only thing setting it aside: its row carries `V25_party_adults_entry_separate` = 1 and nothing else, on the page's own "free entry for one adult per child booked". A farm that publishes free adult entry is removed from the population of operators who charge nothing at the threshold. That is the rule contradicting itself on one unit's own words.

### The second limb is reached onto catering

Tugun Tavern is a pub with no admission of any kind. It is set aside because its coder wrote `V7_per_head` = 1 on "6 ITEMS 3 HOT & 3 COLD \$24PP" and "ADULTS - \$70PP", canapé and buffet menus carrying a "30 PAX MINIMUM". The rule note knows this. Section 7 calls Tugun "the single unit in the whole table set aside on a per-head figure that is plainly not admission", and keeps the limb anyway. The survey already codes what each figure attaches to (the V5 status and the element it sits on). A per-head limb that read the element would have cost one line of script and would not have thrown out a tavern for pricing its canapés.

### The V25 coding rule keeps units that price entry without the word

`rule-review-2026-09-20.md`, issued the same day, sets the test: "At V25, an entry code needs an entry word applied to the thing coded." Sound as a coding rule; fatal as the basis of a population.

- **Fenek Farms** is kept. Its own page: "Select a day and time you would like to book. **Gate passes** can be purchased prior to, in our online store, or can be purchased at the farm", with a separate "Prices \$10 a person" line. The entry-cut clerk's own note explains the 0: "no entry word (entry/admission/gate/ticket) is directly attached to the \$10 figure itself". A farm selling \$10 gate passes online and at the gate is in the population of operators that charge nothing at the threshold.
- **Bella's Wonderland** is kept. Its note: "Do adults need to pay an entry fee? Adults enter for free… the venue otherwise **sells admission ("BUY TICKETS") to children, its core paying customers**." A ticketed children's play centre, kept.
- **Tropical Fruit World** is kept and card 4 still leans on it as one of the two "that publish nothing at their own threshold, as we do". Any Gold Coast buyer knows Tropical Fruit World sells day admission at the gate; the corpus keeps it because the party page it captured says nothing about entry. Same for Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary and Paradise Country, both kept, both ticketed attractions in the buyer's actual world.

A code of 0 is a fact about what the page printed. The rule note says this itself, at section 4, "a unit kept by this rule is a unit that printed no charge at the threshold, not a unit proved to have none", and then builds the population out of those zeroes anyway, which is the whole of the problem.

### What a marketer would use instead

Entry status is a fact about a business, not a reading of a page, and it is the cheapest fact in this corpus to get right. Forty-five units, one visit-or-tickets page each, an hour's work: **free to enter · a gate price to enter · no site of its own** (the four mobile operators), with a fourth value for "could not be established", which stays out of every count rather than being kept by default. That cut would be stable under recoding, would not move with how chatty a page is, and would be checkable by the owner in the only way that matters, since he could read the list and say which ones are wrong.

If the run may use nothing outside the corpus, then three changes make the existing rule defensible: drop the eight units whose venue-wide scope was never captured instead of keeping five of them; drop `V25_party_adults_entry_separate` from the first limb, or split its charged and waived readings; and fire the per-head limb only where the figure attaches to admission rather than to food. On that rule Trevena Glen and Tugun Tavern come back, Fenek and Bella's should come out on a hand check, and the kept cell is smaller and honest rather than larger and wrong.

## 2. The exceptions on cards 2 and 4

**Sound, not an evasion.** The two reasons are genuinely different from each other and from the shared rule.

Card 4's is the stronger: it rules the price basis, so a cut whose second limb is the price basis would be a cut drawn on its own answer. That is not a dodge; it is the one card in the set that logically cannot take the shared rule. It also did the work. It accepted the coded correction that killed its own membership, dropping EcoPark, "the only catchment unit in the corpus that publishes a general entry fee", and correcting its recommending line's "three that let the public in free" to two.

Card 2's is thinner but still substantive: its criterion is not entry at all but "a venue whose party price is the admission has had its composition decided by its trade rather than by a decision about composition". That is a composition argument and it belongs to the parameter card 2 rules. The card then did the honest thing with the entry word, "What card 2 may not keep is the entry word", and printed both readings, 3 to 2 over its seven and 2 to 1 over the coded four.

The test for evasion is whether keeping one's own cut preserved an outcome the shared rule would have destroyed. It did not. Both cards are withheld on every population available to them. Nothing hangs on the exception, which is why it reads as reasoning rather than as a way out.

One blemish remains, and it is card 4's: with EcoPark gone the sharing cell is Tropical Fruit World and Gold Coast Equestrian Centre, and Tropical Fruit World charges at the gate in the real world. The cell that "shows which options exist" is one unit, not two.

## 3. The two turns

### Card 10: how long we get

**Before:** "no more than three hours; margin: 9 units over no length published at all; rests on: market", with the two four-hour units named as "market precedent and not market support".

**After:** "withheld; between no more than three hours and no length published at all; leans: no more than three hours, 25 of the 45 pages read against 16 publishing no length at all; because: inside the 21 units that charge nothing at their own threshold the two stand level at 10 and 10".

**Counts, from the card:** across all 45, "no more than three hours 25, more than three hours 2, no length published 16, 2 undecidable, a 9-unit lead"; across the 21, "10, 0, 10 and 1, no lead at all".

**Direction:** toward what the venue holds. The recommendation withdrawn was to shorten the four hours on sale.

**Verdict: WITHHELD-SOUND. Right outcome, wrong reason written on it.**

The reason as written does not hold. Three of the ten silent units inside the kept 21 are kept on `V25_general_entry_fee` coded 9, not captured: the City of Gold Coast community venues, Old Macdonald's and Paradise Country. Take out the three cells nobody read and the count is 10 to 7 and the recommendation stands. A tie that three uncaptured cells create is not a tie.

What does hold is the reason card 9 gives for the identical runner-up on its own parameter: a page census "cannot see a session an operator keeps for the booking step, which moves pages into silence and out of no other row". Length behaves the same way, and card 10's own seen-by clause says so, in "the lean runs toward the silent row and away from the short one, and over the whole 45 that lean is what the 9-unit lead had to survive". A lead of 9 in a row the instrument inflates by construction is thin on its face, before any cut. The card should be withheld on that, in card 9's words, over the whole 45.

One part of this turn is solid and should be kept whatever happens to the rest: both four-hour precedents do charge at the threshold, EcoPark on a published general entry fee and Collingwood on a fee with the party price stated to cover the guests' entry. Both are corpus-evidenced and neither is doubtful. The sentence the card withdrew, that Collingwood was "the single closest published precedent for the terms this offering sells under", was right to go.

### Card 13: who else is there

**Before:** "a private area inside a site that stays open around it; margin: 7 units over a reserved area, not stated to be private; rests on: market", with the margin over whole-venue exclusivity given as 12 units.

**After:** "withheld; between a private area inside a site that stays open around it and the whole venue, exclusive to the party; leans: a private area, 18 of the 45 pages read against 6 selling the whole venue; because: inside the 21 units that charge nothing at their own threshold the two stand at 6 and 5".

**Counts, from the card:** across all 45, "18 private area, 11 reserved, 6 whole venue, 1 shared, 9 outside, a 7-unit lead"; across the 21, "6, 3, 5, 0 and 7, a 1-unit lead over a runner-up that is no longer the reserved area but the whole venue".

**Direction:** away from what the venue holds. The card says so itself.

**Verdict: UNSUPPORTED.**

The whole turn is one unit wide, so the five whole-venue units decide it. They are Bella's Wonderland, Country Paradise Parklands, Doodlebugs, Event Cinemas and Fenek Farms. Two of them are in the kept population against their own pages' words, on the evidence quoted in section 1 above: Fenek sells gate passes "in our online store, or… at the farm" at "\$10 a person", and Bella's "sells admission ('BUY TICKETS') to children, its core paying customers". Take out either and the lead is 2. Take out both and it is 6 to 3, the same shape the card reported over the whole 45, and no turn at all. A third, Event Cinemas, is a cinema whose party is a private screening, which nobody enters without a ticket; its row is 0 only because the page never said so.

The card compounds this by counting units into a cell it has itself disqualified. Its own Corrections line: "three of the five buy exclusivity by trading outside opening hours, which E10 makes a Director's decision here and not a product parameter". Three of the five whole-venue units are not selling the thing the row claims to count, and they are the margin.

**Section 7, under each reading of the two doubtful units.** Section 7 puts the sensitivity where a judge can test it, which is to the clerk's credit, but it tests the wrong pair and misstates its own evidence on the unit that decides it. It says Fleay's is set aside by the first limb because its page "states that the party price covers the guests' entry and that accompanying adults' entry is charged separately". The coder's verbatim, in the clerk's own copied table, is: "Fee includes **free** entry into the park with one supervising adult" and "minimum 10 people | **Accompanying adults free**". Both codes are set by free-entry wording. The one sentence in the round that decides whether a line turns reads a waiver as a charge.

Taking section 7's four readings as it states them: both doubtful units back in, 8 to 5, "card 13's line would be carried and called thin rather than withheld"; Tugun Tavern alone back in, 7 to 5; Fleay's alone back in, 7 to 5; neither, 6 to 5 and the turn. On the evidence, Tugun Tavern plainly returns, because a pub pricing canapés at "\$24PP" is not charging admission, which section 7 concedes. So the turn already fails at 7 to 5 before Fleay's is considered. Fleay's returns too, because what set it aside is a statement that entry is free. **On the rule's own table the turn does not stand under either doubtful unit's correct reading, let alone both.** And that is before Bella's and Fenek are dealt with on the other side, which would take it past 8 to 5 to something near the whole corpus's 18 to 6.

This is the one place in the round where the owner's second worry has a case to answer. Nobody set out to contradict him. But a line that keeps what the venue sells was withdrawn on a lead of one, inside a cell built from a field that could not carry it, tested against the two units that could not restore it while the two that could were never named. The result looks like contrarianism even though the process was not; a marketer would tell him it is the process that failed, not the conclusion that was rigged.

## 4. The lines that did not turn

**None of them should have turned**, on the re-made figures or on better ones. Cards 6 and 8 rule parameters a gate does not bear on and said so plainly; card 9's silence leads on all three populations; card 11's leg is in the farm-style stratum and its direction survives; card 12 was already withheld and stays withheld; card 22 was already withheld and stays withheld.

Four statements inside them should nevertheless be withdrawn or re-marked, because they report the artefact as a finding.

**Card 12.** It presents the re-made population as strengthening the reversal: "the reversal is now carried by the catchment's own free-threshold operators rather than by a stratum swap: of the sixteen catchment units the rule keeps, ten publish no host". Those ten are, in the main, the council halls, the club, the casino, the turf club, the cinema and the wildlife park, the units kept because they publish nothing at all, which is why they publish no host. The reversal is the cut's silence bias reading itself back. The line stays withheld on the honest ground the card already carries, that the lead changes sides with the population; the sharpening should go.

**Card 9.** The third population (silence 12 of 21, leading by 7) is the most inflated of the three and adds no information. The card is right that "silence leads on all three populations", and it should stop there rather than letting the smallest and most biased count read as confirmation.

**Card 11.** The re-made cell is five farm-style units, of which White Chapel is kept on a V25 row coded 9 across every field and is itself the "1" in "4, 1 and 0": a chapel with no animals standing as the whole of the runner-up. The card's own sentence, that the cell "can only confirm a direction, never rank", is still too generous: a cell of five with one uncaptured member and one degenerate member confirms nothing. Say that, and the leg is unharmed because the leg was never there.

**Card 22.** Its 2/0/0/2/17 should not be read as counts. But its owner's-eye sentence is the best thing the cut produced anywhere in the round, because it does not depend on a marginal count: "eight of the ten farm-style units carrying it are set aside by the rule, six publishing a gate price… so the market's open permission to bring food is, on this corpus, mostly a thing operators do when the guest has already paid to come in". That is a mechanism with eight units behind it, not a lead of one. Keep it.

Cards 6 and 8 are the model for the rest of the set: parameter untouched by a gate, whole-stratum rate kept as the leg, four-unit re-measure demoted to showing which options exist. Card 8's closing line is the sentence cards 10 and 13 needed and did not write: two of its four kept units are "the pair this card already names as the catchment's furthest-apart food postures… so the small cell holds one of each by construction rather than by finding".

## 5. Is a count inside the kept 21 fit to be the stated reason a line is withheld

**No.** Not as the stated reason, and not on this cut.

The population's defects compound rather than cancel. Its membership comes from a variable the reliability note pools at 13 of 18, "a margin of about one in four", which the rule note itself says makes a V25 count "quotable as a count and never as a precise one". Five of the 21 are kept on cells nobody read. Four are mobile operators with no threshold of their own to charge at, and the phrase "charges nothing at its own threshold" is vacuously true of an operator who comes to your house. Eleven publish no price basis at all. At least two more are kept against their own pages' plain words. Put together, a cell of 21 with perhaps seven members who should not be in it cannot rank anything, and a margin of one or two inside it is not evidence of anything except the cut.

The right form is the one the brief suggests: **a card whose re-measure lands in this part says the part is too small and too selected to rank, names what is wrong with it, and rests its line on the whole local corpus with the population difference stated as a limit.** That is what cards 6 and 8 did, and what card 11 nearly did.

Which cards this changes:

- **Card 13**: changes the outcome. The withholding falls. The line goes back to the private area, reported as thin, with the population limit and the exclusivity-outside-hours caveat stated on its face.
- **Card 10**: keeps the outcome, changes the reason. Withheld on the census lean toward silence, over the whole 45, in card 9's words.
- **Card 12**: keeps the outcome, drops the "sharpening".
- **Card 9**: keeps the outcome, drops the third population as a reading.
- **Card 11**: keeps the outcome, demotes the five-unit cell from "confirms the direction" to "confirms nothing".
- **Card 22**: keeps the outcome and the qualitative finding, drops the five figures as counts.
- **Cards 2, 4, 6 and 8**: unaffected. They never asked the small cell to carry a line.

## 6. Three things to change before a rule of this kind is used on another run

**1. Verify the cut variable outside the corpus, and never define a population by a field whose 0 means "the page did not say".** Whether a place charges you to walk in is a fact about the business, obtainable in a minute per unit from the operator's own visit or tickets page. Forty-five units is an hour. A cut is the one thing in a run that touches every card at once, so it is the last place to economise on evidence. The test to apply before adopting one: hand the membership list to somebody who knows the town, and see whether he can name three that are wrong. On this list he can name Fenek, Bella's, Tropical Fruit World, Currumbin Wildlife Sanctuary and Paradise Country before he finishes the page.

**2. Write the cut and its membership down, with each unit's evidence, before any card is re-made, and check its correlations first.** The single check that would have caught this one is a crosstab of the cut against how much each unit publishes. Eleven of 21 kept publishing no price basis against 0 of 24 set aside is a result that should stop a run, because every card in the set whose runner-up is "nothing stated" is about to move for a reason that is not the cut's own. Any cut worth using should be tested against at least one variable it is supposed to be unrelated to.

**3. Set the floor for what a sub-population may be asked to do before the counts are seen, not after.** A cell whose margin sits inside the instrument's own reliability margin may describe and may not rank, and may never be the stated reason a line turns. Print the population's defects beside every figure drawn from it, such as how many members were kept on uncaptured cells and how many have no threshold at all, so the reader can see what the count is made of. And where one unit decides a turn, quote that unit's verbatim in the sentence that turns the line. Both of this round's turns were decided by cells nobody read or verbatims nobody re-read, and both would have failed that one cheap discipline.

## What to tell the owner

The round asked a fair question and asked it in the right way: one cut, written once, crossed against the record, every card re-made on it, with the sensitivities printed. That is better practice than the four private rules it replaced, and the finding against the old list is correct and worth keeping.

It is not the first of his worries. Nothing here is read off meeting notes or off what anybody thinks he believes; it is all read off pages, and where the earlier work leaned on a trade description the clerk called it out.

On the second worry, one line needs his eye. Card 13 withdrew a recommendation that matched what he sells, on a lead of one unit, inside a cell of five whose members include a farm that sells \$10 gate passes and a play centre that sells admission tickets. That withdrawal should not stand. Card 10's withdrawal should stand, but for the reason card 9 already gives, not for the tie the cut manufactured.
