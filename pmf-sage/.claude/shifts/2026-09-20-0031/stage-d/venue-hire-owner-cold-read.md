# Owner's cold read: venue hire ruling sheet

Read once, in order, from the top of `review.md` down through "Open cards" and its short paragraph. I did not open any card file. No row left me unable to say what was being asked of me.

## 1. What I now believe I'm being asked to decide

Staff want me to settle how we sell "venue hire", hiring out our spaces for family occasions and events, by ruling on 24 separate questions, from who it's for and what we call it, down to price, capacity, food, alcohol and booking mechanics. For each question the clerks have looked at what other venues do and at our own past sales, and either recommend keeping or leaving what we currently do, or say the market can't settle it and want me either to rule it myself from nothing or send someone off to find out more. There's a set order to go through them in, because some answers change what other questions even mean. Alongside all that, the page also flags several things that are simply broken in our records right now, regardless of which way any of this goes. My job today is to go row by row and either confirm, rule, commission more work, or admit I'm stuck.
