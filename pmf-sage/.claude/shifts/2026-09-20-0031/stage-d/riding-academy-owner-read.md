## 1. What I now believe I'm being asked to decide

I'm being asked to rule on 28 separate questions about how we'd run a children's riding lesson offering — age, price, group size, hours, booking terms, that kind of thing — each one already carrying a market-researched recommendation. For some I can just confirm the recommendation or pick an option outright; for others the recommendation is withheld because the market evidence doesn't settle it, and I'm expected either to rule it myself or to send someone off to check one specific thing. There's a set order to rule in, starting with who the lessons are actually for, because several of the later questions only make sense once that's answered. Separately from the ruling itself, six things are already wrong in how we operate today, whichever way I rule — wrong days on sale, wrong till prices, dead listings, a booking slot sold after we're closed, no verified instructor qualification on file, and lapsed workers' comp — and those need fixing regardless of any decision here. There's also a planning-approval question nobody else can answer for me.

- "**units**" vs "**operators**" — I read "unit" as one web page/product listing and "operator" as the business itself, since a business can apparently have more than one listing. Not defined on the front page.

## 4. The six things flagged wrong today

Yes for all six — I could tell a staff member what to do on Monday for each:

1. **Fee schedule vs booking system on private lesson days** — yes: whoever keeps the fee schedule makes the two agree.
2. **Till prices vs fee schedule (ten items, eight prices, vs four printed prices)** — yes: bring the till in line or remove the extra items.
3. **Forerunner's introductory lesson and four-lesson package still listed, unbookable** — yes: take both down.
4. **Last weekday slot (3:30–4:30pm) sold after the site closes at 3pm, with nobody rostered for it anyway** — yes: stop selling it or roster someone.
5. **No verified Blue Card or coaching qualification on file for anyone who teaches** — yes: the manager who rosters equestrian staff checks each card Monday and gets the contracted instructor's accreditation from the issuing body. (This is also why I can't honestly confirm Card 15's credential claim or Card 16's assessment-first session today, even though both look like clear rules on the numbers.)
6. **Workers' compensation policy expired, no renewal on file** — yes: whoever keeps the insurance records confirms the renewal and files the certificate.

Separately, not a "fix it Monday" item: whether riding lessons need council planning approval here has been open since November 2024, and the page is explicit that this is mine to answer, not staff's to action.
