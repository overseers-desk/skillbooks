## 1. What I now believe I am being asked to decide

I am being asked to settle, in one sitting, what the venue's kayak hire actually is and how it is sold: what it's called, what's included, who it's aimed at, how long you get, what it costs and how you pay, and the safety and weather rules around it. Most of these questions are not ready for me to decide today — the sheet itself says twenty of the twenty-eight are "withheld" because the market evidence doesn't clearly carry an answer, and it wants me either to pick between two options anyway or to send someone off to find a fact that would settle it. Before any of that, though, I'm told the product can't lawfully go on sale at all right now, because the insurance doesn't cover it and the landing structure's status is unresolved — those are for Monday, not for this sitting. So really I have three jobs: clear the two things that are wrong today, rule the handful of cards that are genuinely ready to rule or confirm, and hand the rest off as directed fact-finding rather than guessing at them myself.

## 3. Words or phrases I had to guess at

- "venue units" / "places" / "units" as the denominator, used interchangeably with "venues" across different rows (e.g. Card 0: "39 one-offer venue units"; Card 8: "12 of 17 priced places"). I guessed these mean the same thing — one countable listing per venue — but the sheet never states that a "unit" and a "venue" are the same count, or why some rows count in "places" instead.
- Card 0's "derived on 39 one-offer venue units less 13 launch-only venue units" — I guessed this means: of the venues offering only one product, 13 turned out to be a plain boat-launch rather than a kayak hire, and were subtracted out.
- Card 10's "a going-over rule on 1 of them for, ... a going-over rule on 0 of them against" — I guessed "going-over rule" means an overtime charge or extension policy for running past the hour, and that the numbers count how many of the block-time venues also state such a rule.
- Card 19's "a count of what is printed that decides nothing alone" — I guessed this means the destination-venue figure (46 silent, 6 naming an animal) isn't informative by itself, because silence there isn't evidence of absence, so it doesn't override the larger operator-level lead.
- "coded blind" (Cards 5, 28, and the later "Withheld" paragraph) — I guessed this means counted by a reader who didn't already know the venue's held value or the first clerk's recommendation, to avoid the count bending toward the expected answer.
- "set aside" / "aside" (e.g. Card 5's "paddle businesses aside 6, 2, 0"; Card 15's "outside them") — I guessed this means the same count re-run with one subgroup of the sample removed, to check whether the lead survives without it.
- "title to the bank unsettled across four records" (in the landing paragraph) — I could follow that ownership of the riverbank is unclear, but not who is meant to resolve that, or whether it's part of what the Director is asked to state on Monday.
