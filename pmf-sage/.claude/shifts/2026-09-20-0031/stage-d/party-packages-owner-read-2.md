## 1. What I believe I'm being asked to decide

I'm being asked to rule 24 questions about how we sell a children's birthday party here: who it's for, what's in the fee, how the price scales, how long it runs, how the date is held and what happens to the money, and how a parent finds us and books. On thirteen of the twenty-four the market itself can't tell me an answer, so my job there is either to pick between two named options on the strength of a stated lean, or to send it off for more checking. Six other items are just broken paperwork today regardless of how I rule, and I'm told to get those fixed Monday. There's also a standing rule of mine from 14 August that already answers part of four of these cards: no staff credential gets promised in any of them, so I only need to rule what's left on those four. I'm told to rule in a set order, not down the table, because several cards move together or answer each other.

## 3. Words or phrases I had to guess at

- **"readable units"** (used for card 1's denominator of 74, versus the 45/28/17 defined for other cards). I guessed this means pages that could actually be coded on this question, as distinct from pages that exist but were screened out or came back dead.
- **"charge per child for entry"** (cards 2, 4, 12, 22). Not defined in the front. I guessed it separates venues that sell entry to an activity per head from destination-style venues like ours that don't, and that this is meant to be the filter for "places like us," though I'm not told why.
- **"the places most like us"** (card 4). I guessed this means the same "doesn't charge per child for entry" group, but the row doesn't say so explicitly, so I'm not certain it's the same three units each time this phrase recurs.
- **"page-one slots"** (card 20). I guessed this means first-page Google search result positions.
- **"the priors pass"** (in the 14 August bound section). I guessed this means an earlier round of card-writing, before this one, that hadn't yet applied my ruling on staff credentials.
- **"the measured-in lines"** (in "What passed through"): "mark a measured population as differing from this venue's shape 99 times." I guessed this is a running count, across all the held/left comparisons, of how often the market sample looked unlike our own operation, but the sentence doesn't say what counts as one of the 99.

## 5. Three changes that would most help me rule this in one sitting

1. Put the reversal ("not carried because...") in the same breath as the lean, not after it. Several rows read as agreeing with themselves until the second half flips the answer, and I had to re-read each one to catch that.
2. Define "charge per child for entry" once, up front, since it silently decides four cards (2, 4, 12, 22), and I'm never shown why it's the right line to draw between "like us" and "not like us."
3. On card 18, state the recommendation and the evidence in the same words. "No refund, but the date can be moved" versus "a movable date leads a refund on notice" read as two different questions, not one answer and its support.
