# Venue hire: the judges' rules for the corrections round, 26 September 2026

Two judges read the venue hire page blind of each other (`venue-hire-judge-1.md`, `venue-hire-judge-2.md`). Both judge it better evidence than the survey's draft cards of 15 August (about 80 and 75 per cent sure): no unsupported or echo card against five unsupported and two echo there. Both find it withholds where its own evidence settles the question (judge 1: cards 12b, 15, 17; judge 2: 5, 9, 11, 12b, 15, 17, 19a). Their findings, merged into rules; a card is given the rule, never a verdict.

1. Silence is no option in a count, and it is no reason to withhold either. Where the sellers who state a term lean one way and the rest print nothing, the stated lean is weighed, and a withholding says what beyond silence stops it.
2. A buyer's ask for a term not on offer, a refusal, a walk-away or a displacement by a wedding counts as evidence on every card it bears on; "acceptance only" applies to sales, never to asks.
3. Where one option is the absence of a term (no minimum, no bond, no age condition) and nothing supports adding the term, the absence is recommended; where one option turns away no observed buyer and the other does, the first is recommended.
4. The venue's current value is always one of the options compared, or the card says every option drops it; it stands as the runner-up where the recommendation leaves it; keeping and dropping it need the same proof.
5. Any law, licence, insurer condition or occupancy limit that bounds a parameter is read and stated before sellers are counted, and leads the because-clause.
6. Whether the clubs, pubs and breweries (the sale part) are set aside is decided once per parameter, before counting, and the same comparison groups are used on every card that turns on the same question.
7. Whether the children's-package buyers count as this offering's buyers is decided once for the whole set, in `card-set.md`, and every card follows it (card 0 counts them; card 17 leaves them out today).
8. Each option covers one parameter; two separately decidable halves are never joined into one option.
9. A ruling-sheet row is labelled confirm only by the strength of its evidence, never because it agrees with the current value.
10. Records the venue already holds (the sales record, the enquiry exports) are counted before any new observation is commissioned.
11. A market-only reading that rests only on sellers printing nothing is stamped "declines", not "differs".
12. Where two cards answer one question, the page says so and rules them together; merging them into one card is owed to a later round, not done here. The wedding-priority question (amendment A13), which no card asks, is owed to a later derivation.

A round that moves lines one way, counted toward or away from what the venue holds and from carried to withheld or back, goes to a targeted judge before the page refreshes.
