## 3. Words and phrases I had to guess at

- "a ceremony word" (Card 2): I guessed this means some specific proposed name I'm not actually shown, rather than a category of word.
- "counted once per business rather than once per page" (Cards 4, 5): I guessed this means chains with one published hire page get counted as one vote instead of one vote per site; the row doesn't spell out the mechanics.
- "the margin both ways" (Card 14): I guessed this means "count it one way, cooling wins; count it the other way, it loses," but the row doesn't say which counting method is correct, only that both exist.
- "texts" vs "operators" (correction paragraph under "How this was made"): I guessed "texts" means published pages or copies of a page, and "operators" means distinct businesses, by analogy with the Card 4/5 business-vs-page split.
- "the framing the venue holds" (Card 5's turn, in the correction paragraph): I guessed this means "access," the option that matches how we already run bookings, but had to infer it from context rather than being told directly.
- "the measured-in lines mark a measured population as differing from this venue's shape 51 times" (under "What passed through"): I guessed "this venue's shape" means our own mix of business (weddings, funerals, tours) rather than some other meaning of "shape," but I'm not fully sure.
- "sells the same room twice" (Cards 3/19/24 joint note): I took this as a metaphor for double-booking the same decision through two different cards, not a literal double-sale.
