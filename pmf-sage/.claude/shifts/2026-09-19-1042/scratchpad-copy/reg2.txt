## 6. Substitutes for a hinterland estate wedding

A couple weighing Historic Rivermill is not only weighing the 34 rivals above; some weigh a different kind of day altogether. The survey's own scope is ceremony-and-reception venues (frame.md, Scope), so it carries no coded evidence on these substitutes directly; they are stated here as kinds, with whatever evidence the findings or the frame hold adjacent to them, on the same silence-is-a-value basis as the rest of this register.

- **A registry-office ceremony plus a restaurant reception.** No unit in this corpus is a registry office or a plain restaurant booking; the nearest coded approximation is The Old Church, whose ceremony chapel and reception are run jointly with an adjacent restaurant, the Grand Chameleon, as one sold offer rather than two independently bookable ones (frame.md, R1 table note; §8 above). No finding in this survey measures how often a Gold Coast couple chooses a registry ceremony over a venue wedding at all; it is outside this corpus's scope.
