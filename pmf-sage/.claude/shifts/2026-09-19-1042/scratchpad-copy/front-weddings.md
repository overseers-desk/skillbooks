# Decision review

The run's decision cards. Each card is a question, the ways the market answers it with a figure for each, the clerks' recommendation, and a line for the ruling. Rule from the sheet below, in the order that follows it, and open a card where a row raises a doubt. Where a card names another card, it names it by number.

## The ruling sheet

One row a card. *Your part* says what the row asks of you: confirm, rule, commission an observation, or nothing. *Recommended* reads withheld where no option is carried, and the margin column then gives the counts the card stands at. A negative margin means the largest count runs against the recommendation, which then rests on the other evidence its card names. *Rests on* says whether the recommendation stands on the market, on this venue's own buyers, or on both, and is blank on a withheld row. *What is held* says whether the recommendation keeps or leaves a value the venue already held. *Third derivation* says where a fresh clerk, reading the market alone, landed against the recommendation.

## The order of ruling

**Three things are wrong today whichever way you rule, and none of them waits on a ruling.**

The public wedding page and the brochure describe every priced package as whole-site exclusive use. The signed terms cover only the booked spaces, on three of the four package rows, and closing the site to visitors during published hours needs an approval rarely granted. Thirty-one weddings were sold under the page's promise. Card 17 is withheld between exactly those two sentences, so ruling it settles which one you sell next year and settles nothing about the document that contradicts itself now.

Two listings the operator controls publish a reception maximum of 120 guests. The wedding page publishes a ceiling of 100, the whole chair stock is 100 stackable chairs, no document says how a party above that is seated, and two secured weddings already went to 120 with a third couple holding 120 for July 2027. Card 14 is withheld between a ceiling of 101 to 149 and one of 150 or more; both readings are above the stock, and the stock is a purchase, not a ruling.

The deposit is named two ways in internal sources, a wedding deposit against the balance and an arrangement fee for booking and pre-planning; the operator's own procedure flags which entity receives it as needing clarification from the Director; and the default balance schedule is given two ways, a two-instalment default gated on a value threshold and a monthly-instalments default. Card 24 carries the deposit and Card 9 is withheld on whether it is published. Publishing it while those three stand puts all three in front of a couple in writing.

**What the sheet asks of you.** Eight rows say confirm, the recommendation keeping what this venue already holds. Eight say rule, the recommendation leaving a held value and naming after "seen by:" the instrument that could have shown that value winning and which way it leans. Seventeen say commission the observation, or rule between the two. Nothing is ruled yet, so no row asks you to revisit a decision, and no card's reading departs from one.

**The order.** Card 0 goes first with Card 1, because it carries the count of priced offers and cards 3, 6 and 22 state their own recommendation on it. **Four correction rounds, and which way each moved the set.** The first put every line to the method's test and withdrew eighteen to withheld, turning one, accommodation, toward what the venue holds. The second turned two back to recommendations, the ceremony tier and the reply's deliverable, both away from what is held. The third turned four more away, the all-in total, the season discount, both surfaces and the gallery, and withdrew three, food and drink, the small end and the deposit schedule, when the one-operator rule showed their leads were ties. The fourth, today, applied the instrument test to every line alike: it turned the hire block to a ladder that rises with the tier, toward what the venue already sells, and withdrew three that had rested on a silent count or on no instrument at all, the ceremony tier, the availability answer and the listing. The set has moved steadily from recommending toward reporting: eight rows now confirm what is held, eight leave it, and seventeen say the evidence does not separate two options and name what would.
