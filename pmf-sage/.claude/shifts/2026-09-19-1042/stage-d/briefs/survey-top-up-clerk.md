# Survey top-up clerk

Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, and the three files it sends every clerk to, `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-methodology.md`, the phase document `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-S-survey.md` and `briefs/venue-situation.md`, then `forms/card.md`, then `3-decisions/integrator-fields.md`, whose paragraph headed **Withheld, by what the observation costs**, under `## Unlock`, is your work list, then the withheld cards your prompt names under `3-decisions`, and the records each card's "carried by" clause points you to among those `2-blacklist/fence-index.md` leaves open.

Each of your cards is withheld and names the one observation that would carry it. Work the list cheapest first; where the run defers the top-up until after the owner's page, your prompt says so.

Where the records this run already holds can give that observation (a recount, a re-grouping, a join of two coded files, a coding pass over held pages or profiles), make it, and write it as a numbered finding in `{{CORPUS}}/findings-top-up.md` with its measured population, its count and its limit. Where a card's figures came from a reading narrowed to sentences beside the offering's own word, and the observation is what sellers say elsewhere on their pages (a groups, functions or bookings page), read those pages and say which reading you used. A count of yours that reads pages or profiles by judgment is made a second time by a second clerk who has not seen yours, and the finding gives both counts and where they differ, since a count made to order turns no card until it has been made twice. The second clerk is given the observation and its rule and not the card, its Priors or Held line, or which option the count would carry.

Where the observation is a fact the venue can state itself (what its craft seat, how many horses are in service, what training its staff hold), write the question and the role that can answer it under the card's number, and do not count the market in its place. Where it needs something the run does not hold (a call to a rival, a trial at the venue, a database pull the owner has not allowed), say so in one line under the card's number with what blocks it, and leave it there; an estimate in its place is worth nothing to the card. Where one observation would carry several withheld cards at once, say which, so the owner is shown one commission and not four. Leave the cards alone: the corrections clerk puts the recommended line to the test again with your finding in hand.
