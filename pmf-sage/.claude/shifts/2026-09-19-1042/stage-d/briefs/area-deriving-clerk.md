Write the cards of your area of `3-decisions/card-set.md`, one file each, `3-decisions/card-<number>-<short-name>.md`, on `forms/card.md` with every field the form names except the Priors line, which belongs to another clerk, and with the Ruled line reading "open". The Joint line names the cards yours was decided with and what changes on yours if one of them is ruled otherwise, and it names any card of another area that rules the same body of evidence, since the integrator's pairs check reads those lines and not the whole set. The buyer is unruled: your card carries every buyer whose seller's sentence it answers to, as the card set marks it, and a card answering only to a buyer the owner later strikes falls with that buyer.

On every card: each option is a way the market sells this, with its figure and source. The terms this offering sells under are one of the options where a buyer accepted them, which is where the sales record shows a buyer paying under them (a sale, an invoice), with no advantage for being the venue's and no handicap; a listing no buyer has bought under takes no option row, and the card names it on the venue's-own-record leg as listed and unbought, since the method makes an unsold present practice a prior; where the offering has never been on sale there are no such terms, no option is the venue's, and nothing in the records stands in for them. Recommend what the evidence carries and state the margin as the form asks; a thin lead is written as a thin lead. After "rests on:" write one of market, own buyers or both, and put any explanation in its own clause. Where the corpus, the rivals and the search findings between them show fewer than two ways, the card is a named gap for Survey, and you say which record would close it. A count you make yourself is written with its rule (what was counted, over which files, what was left out), so another clerk can make it again; where `0-comparables/corpus-inventory.md` shows a cell uncoded, a coded rate says so. Write options that exclude one another, since a rival counted in two options separates nothing; and where a cell holds too few operators for any lead to survive one of them coded the other way, say on the measured-in line that the cell shows which options exist and cannot rank them.

A finding of a survey file is re-read at its quote, and re-counted from the coded rows where it states a count, before your card cites it: findings have counted not-stated cells as statements and tallied the wrong thing. Cite the quote, and where the finding does not reproduce, say so, give your own count with its rule, and name the finding in your report for the survey's corrections file. Where a unit's coded cell is wrong at its quote (a code against its own note, a unit coded as priced with no amount), correct the figure on the card with the quote, leave the coded table as it is, and name the cell in your report.

Before a card leans on a coded value, read what the rows it counts are about, since a variable can gather the offering's terms with those of other things sold. Count on the offering's own rows as `3-decisions/card-set.md` defines them; where it does not, name the rows you took and report it, since which rows are the offering's can be another card's question. Report a mix for the next amendment. Where your card reads a variable or a table another card also reads, take the reading the card set states; where it states none, name the other card and the reading you took on the Joint line.

Where you re-measure a lead inside one part of the corpus to see whether it survives, the column that defines that part is checked against each unit's own description before your card leans on the cut, since a frame field assigned at the draw is often the one column nobody double-coded. Say on the card that the check was made and what it found. Where the card set names a table for the part you set aside, use that table and not a test of your own wording; where your card's question calls for another cut (a card about the price basis may rightly cut on the price basis), say on the card which cut and why.

Where a count of units would count one body's published decision several times, make it on `{{CORPUS}}/operators.tsv` as well: the option table prints units, the base the card check reads and the one-operator test runs on, and the operator count sits beside it on the measured-in line. Where the operator table and a finding disagree on whether two units are one seller, give the card's figure both ways and name the pair in your report.

Where the capability record says the venue cannot yet deliver or truthfully claim what an option needs (a credential, a staff member, a horse count, a licence), put that fact on the card's cost lines by its number; it is for the integrator's page and does not move the ranking. Where your card and another are one decision read twice, or where your options answer one half of the question and not the other, say so on the Joint line rather than writing the second card, and where the question needs the owner to settle something the market cannot answer, say that on the card instead of ranking options the evidence cannot separate. Leave `tools/card-check.py` to the orchestrator: it prints every card's landing, the other areas' included.
