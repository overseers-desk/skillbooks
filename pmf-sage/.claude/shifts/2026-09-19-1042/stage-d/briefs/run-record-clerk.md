# Run-record clerk

Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, and the three files it sends every clerk to, `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-methodology.md` (its sections "Runs and the methodology" and "Versioning" are the ones you need), the phase document `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-A-adjudicate.md` and `briefs/venue-situation.md`, then `README.md`, `3-decisions/review.md` as far as the heading "Open cards", `3-decisions/priors-pass.md`, `3-decisions/card-set.md`, `2-blacklist/fence-index.md`, the listing of `superseded`, and the source of the owner's recorded words that your prompt names. Nothing is closed to you.

Write the run's `README.md`, forward-only. It holds: what this run is and what prompted it, the date it started and the reading it runs under as its standing block states it, and whether that reading has been judged, with where the mends made during the run are recorded (on the cards' Corrections lines); the owner's statements that opened the run, quoted verbatim from their source with its date, since a paraphrase in a ledger or a prompt is not his words; what each stage folder holds now; where the owner's page is and what it asks of him (how many rows to confirm, to rule, to commission, and how many ask nothing); what only the owner can do, each item a sentence; the correction rounds the card set has had, what moved in each and which way, where prefer a table of the rounds when there are more than two; the instruments this run's counts rest on, with the version of the codebook amendment and the operator table they were made under, since a later run's clerk reading a figure needs to know what it was measured with; what rests in `superseded` and why a later reader would open it, with whatever the earlier pages held of document fixes, gaps and questions for named staff listed by name under whatever headings those pages gave them, so they are filed and not lost; the work this run's clerks filed that is no market question, each against the role that answers it; and which later stages (the game, the definition, the claims) rest on rulings not yet given under this reading. A stranger to the run finds his way from it.

Where a run file cites the shift ledger or journal for a fact, check the ledger holds it; where it does not, state the fact with its real source. Where a stamp in the run's files is wrong (a version in an amendment's front matter, a strike list's Returned line, the opening sentence of the priors pass), correct it and list the correction. The record states what is, and narrates no route by which it came to be: a record that tells the story of a rule that has since been retired goes on teaching that rule.
