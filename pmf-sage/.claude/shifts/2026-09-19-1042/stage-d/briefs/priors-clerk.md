# Priors clerk

Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, and the three files it sends every clerk to, `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-methodology.md`, the phase document `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-A-adjudicate.md` (its section "Conduct" is the one you need) and `briefs/venue-situation.md`, then `forms/card.md`, then every card file under `3-decisions`, then everything `2-blacklist/fence-index.md` closes, this run's earlier decision review and definition among it, and `{{OP}}/sot/S2-fees-and-service-hours.md`. You are the one clerk who opens the closed files, in a single pass, after the cards are written; the venue's document store is open to you for the files the fence index names, and a quotation of a document inside an earlier review is not the document. Name in your report any closed file you did not open.

On each card file append the Priors line, as one line: every held value for that parameter (the owner's rulings, terms a buyer paid for, terms listed and never bought, a standard procedure, what a draft or definition holds, a position the owner stated without ruling it), each with the file, its date, and its stated range or word. Stamp the Held line as the form says, judging in substance and not by shared words, and stamp the chip by the method's rule: the recommendation matches a held value when it falls inside the value's stated range or uses its word. A clerk that later turns a recommending line restamps the chip; you stamp it now, so that no card reaches the owner with the form's placeholder on it. Rank held values so: the owner's ruling; then terms a buyer paid for (a sale, an invoice); above terms listed and never bought; above a draft, a definition or a plan. A definition entered into the booking system the day it was written and never bought ranks as a draft, whatever the system calls it; stamp it in the draft's tier and say so in the pass. Where held values fall in tiers this ranking does not place (a standard procedure, a position the owner stated without ruling it), your prompt gives their place. Write the ranking once at the head of `3-decisions/priors-pass.md` and stamp every card by it, since stamps made by several unwritten rankings cannot be compared. A position the owner stated in a meeting or a message without ruling it is a held value and is stamped as one.

Where the owner has already ruled the parameter (a ruling is the owner's words with their date and source; a definition, plan or decisions record that holds values without his dated words holds priors, which go on the Priors line and not the Ruled line), write his ruling on the Ruled line with its date and his words, since a ruling stands until he changes it; the card tests it and does not reopen it. His words are quoted from their source, with its date: a paraphrase of them in a ledger, a prompt or a run file is not his words.

A standing bound is not a ruling of a parameter and is missed when it is looked for as one: an instruction from the owner or a director that forecloses a kind of claim or wording while leaving every option open binds the cards it touches, and one found by a judge after a pass had reported "nothing ruled" had already let a card recommend against it. Read the closed files for bounds as well as rulings and record them in a column of their own with their date and commit. Put each bound on a line of its own, `**Bound:**`, straight after the Ruled line of every card it reaches, saying which options it permits. The Ruled line keeps the one word "open" and nothing after it, since the tools read anything after "Ruled:" but "open" as a ruling. A file's own line that nothing in it is ruled is the file's word about parameters and says nothing about bounds.

Change nothing else on a card: the pass decides nothing, and a match is not a fault in the card any more than a departure is a merit. Then write `3-decisions/priors-pass.md`. It opens with a line saying it records what this pass found on its date and that the cards' Held lines and the review are the present state, since a correction round will leave this table behind; then the ranking; then a tally row for this pass (keeps, leaves, held, nothing held), which later rounds extend with a row each. Then one row per card with its recommendation, the held values, whether it matched a held value, what the run's earlier card on the parameter recommended or gated, named by its fact and not by the earlier card's number, and "mapped by subject" where no earlier card answers to it one to one (a separate column, since an earlier card is one held value among several), whether the owner had ruled it, which bounds reach it, and whether the recommendation agrees with his ruling. Close with the parameters the earlier decisions ruled that no card of this set carries, each with where it was ruled, since you are the one clerk who can see them and the owner's page otherwise drops them without a word.
