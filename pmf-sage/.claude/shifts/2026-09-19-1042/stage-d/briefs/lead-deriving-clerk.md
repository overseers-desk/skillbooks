Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, and the three files it sends every clerk to, `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-methodology.md`, the phase document `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-A-adjudicate.md` and `briefs/venue-situation.md`, then `2-blacklist/fence-index.md` before any other file of the run folder, then `forms/card.md` and, of this run's and the venue's files, only the records the fence's open list names, by the sections it opens: among them `3-decisions/strike-list.md`, `3-decisions/questions-numbered.md`, `0-comparables/shape-note.md`, `0-comparables/corpus-inventory.md`, `{{FINDINGS}}`, `{{CORPUS}}/findings-buyers-market.md`, `{{CORPUS}}/findings-buyers-operator.md`, `{{CORPUS}}/operators.tsv`, `1-competitions/capability-extract.md`, `1-competitions/search-demand-findings.md` and `1-competitions/sales-record.md`. Everything the fence index closes is closed to you, this run's earlier decisions and the fee schedule among it; where the fence and this list disagree on a file, take the closure and report the disagreement.

Write four files, each card on `forms/card.md` with every field the form names except the Priors line, which belongs to another clerk, and with the Ruled line reading "open".

1. `3-decisions/card-1-who-it-is-for.md`: the buyer card. Its options are the unstruck rows of the strike list, each with a figure from the source its row names, a capability leg with cost lines, and a seller's sentence. The owner has not ruled the buyer, and whether he is asked before the card set is his to rule; until he rules it the card says in terms that a card answering only to a buyer he strikes falls with that buyer. Name the reading its figures were measured under and the buyer rows that reading can hide, since a reading kept to sentences beside the offering's own word can miss a buyer's whole functions, events or groups page; where that decides the card, the reading that would show it is the observation that carries it.
2. `3-decisions/card-0-one-offer-or-several.md`. Make its count on what can see each option: a variable that records how a seller structures a range cannot see a seller who sells one thing only, and a count on it alone ranks nothing.
3. `3-decisions/seller-sentences.md`: for each buyer the buyer card leaves standing, which is the buyer it recommends or each side of a withheld line, what the person who takes the booking says to that buyer, two sentences at most, said to a real enquirer from the sales record where one exists and otherwise to the nearest order in it. A private buyer is never named in a seller's sentence or on a card: describe the buyer by class and kind, and quote an enquirer's words without a name, since on some runs the enquirers are participants of a disability or health scheme and a card travels into every review. The card form's "named roster profile" is read that way here.
4. `3-decisions/card-set.md`: the questions of `3-decisions/questions-numbered.md` that earn a card because their ruling would change a seller's sentence, each card taking its number from the question's, each stated in a buyer's words, and each marked with every standing buyer whose sentence its ruling changes, since the buyer is unruled and a later ruling has to be able to find the cards that fall with it; a card that changes every standing buyer's sentence is marked to all of them, and a card marked to one buyer leaves the others with no answer on that question. Prefer giving, for each standing buyer, the count of cards that fall if he strikes it. Share the cards out into areas of near-equal weight and say how many areas the set makes; the orchestrator settles the number of area clerks from it. Then the questions left to design freedom, with the reason each changes no sentence; then the work the records show that is no market question (a document to correct, a question only a named member of staff can answer, a fault in a system, a fact the records state more than one way), filed here with the role that answers each, so it is not lost and not carded. Where a regulation, the insurer's conditions or a peak body's scheme sets a parameter, say so here: prefer carding it with that rule as the row that bounds the others, a floor or a ceiling as its document says, naming whom it binds, or filing it with the role that answers it, over a census of what sellers print, which the rule overrides whatever it shows. A question with two halves earns a card for each half or a card that answers both: the set says which, since a card whose options answer one half leaves the other uncarded across the whole set. Where two cards will read one variable or one table, state here the one reading they take, and for each variable a card counts, which rows are the offering's own. List, one row an option, the options the set treats as silence (the card asks what a seller prints and the option is printing nothing), since that reading is applied by several clerks. Where `0-comparables/shape-note.md` marks a dimension on which part of the corpus differs from this operator, and cards of more than one area will set that part aside, write the part once as a table, `{{CORPUS}}/cut-<dimension>.tsv`, one row a unit, its rule in a sentence beside it, and name it in the card set with its size. A coded column draws the part only where its zero means the thing is absent and not that a page was silent about it; otherwise name the observation that would draw it, which is what each operator's own pages say of the thing itself, as work for the Survey top-up, and the cards say the part is not yet drawn. Where the part is drawn by a column that already exists (a kind of seller, a cell), check it against each unit's own description before the card set names it. Before any card leans on the table, cross it against one variable it should be unrelated to, such as how much a unit publishes, and say what that showed. The table is written once because area clerks who each draw such a part in words draw different parts, and a withheld line then gives as its reason a reversal inside a part no other card shares.

On every card: each option is a way the market sells this, with its figure and source. The terms this offering sells under are one of the options where a buyer accepted them, which is where the sales record shows a buyer paying under them (a sale, an invoice), with no advantage for being the venue's and no handicap; a listing no buyer has bought under takes no option row, and the card names it on the venue's-own-record leg as listed and unbought, since the method makes an unsold present practice a prior; where the offering has never been on sale there are no such terms, no option is the venue's, and nothing in the records stands in for them. Recommend what the evidence carries and state the margin as the form asks; a thin lead is written as a thin lead. After "rests on:" write one of market, own buyers or both, and put any explanation in its own clause. A count you make yourself is written with its rule (what was counted, over which files, what was left out), so another clerk can make it again; where `0-comparables/corpus-inventory.md` shows a cell uncoded, a coded rate says so. Write options that exclude one another, since a rival counted in two options separates nothing; and where a cell holds too few operators for any lead to survive one of them coded the other way, say on the measured-in line that the cell shows which options exist and cannot rank them. A fact the records state more than one way (a forerunner's window given as three spans) is cited from its home, which for the venue's sales is the sales record, and the other statements are filed in the card set's third list.

Where a count of units would count one body's published decision several times, make it on `{{CORPUS}}/operators.tsv` as well: the option table prints units, which is the base the card check reads and the one-operator test runs on, and the operator count sits beside it on the measured-in line, since a chain that prints one sentence across its sites can make a lead or hide one. Leave `tools/card-check.py` to the orchestrator: it prints every card's landing, the other areas' included.
