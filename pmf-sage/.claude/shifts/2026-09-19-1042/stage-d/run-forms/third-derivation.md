# Form: third derivation

One file a card, `3-decisions/third-card-<number>.md`, copied as it stands and filled. Angle brackets mark what the clerk replaces; nothing else moves. The four closing lines are each a paragraph of their own, since the operator repository's writing rules read lines without a blank between them as one paragraph.

```
## Third derivation of Card <number>

<the question, as the numbered questions file prints it>

- <a way the market sells this> · figure: <count> · population: <what it was counted over> · source: <file>
- <a way the market sells this> · figure: <count> · population: <what it was counted over> · source: <file>

Carried: <the way the evidence carries, or `tie`>

Margin: <the margin over the strongest other, in that way's unit>

Would break it: <on a tie only: the one observation that would separate them>

Counted: <what was counted, over which files, what was left out>
```

Where a lead holds in one part of the corpus and reverses in another, both readings take a line of their own under the ways, each with its population.
