# SAGE Establish — the definition the owner picks from

Establish turns the crowned design into a definition the owner picks from: claims with numbers behind them and one definition folded by stated rules. The study ends there. Migration and the displacement offer happen only after the owner chooses an offering, and the study presumes no such choice; the definition stands without them, and nothing in it waits on them. Its launch dependencies, what launching it would depend on if he chooses it, may stand beside the definition, in the conditional, never as a plan or as steps.

## The numbered claims

After the crown, the selling points are drawn as numbered claims (u1, u2 and so on) that the definition's positioning section and any outreach message cite by number.

Unique means scarce, not sole. Each claim is measured against the Survey corpus and carries its prevalence in a grounding table, so "few operators do this" has a number behind it. A sole-provider claim is admitted only through a closed-world check of the rival register or the owner's recorded ratification; the corpus cannot prove a negative about the world, only about itself.

Each claim traces to a decision card, a dated ruling, a register section, or a sentence of the crowned sheet. The claim paragraphs are written quotable-clean, with all provenance quarantined in the internal grounding table, because downstream campaign agents lift whole sentences, and a citation inside a lifted sentence travels where it should not. The claims pass the same reviewer gate as the game sheets: positioning synthesis is a known entry point for format smuggling.

## Folding the definition

The definition is folded from its inputs by stated rules rather than re-written:

- the offering's text is copied verbatim from the crowned sheet under a copy rule, floor fixes included; summarising it loses the interior detail the judges bought;
- the positioning section merges the numbered claims, citing them by number;
- the buyer-simulation cost fold carries the judges' own all-in figures and bottom-line counts into the market section, so the definition states what the product costs a real buyer all-in, not only what the operator bills.

Two passes run before it is final. A dedup pass leaves every number one home inside the document, later mentions referencing the first. Then an independent cold read: a context-free reader places the price against the market as the document presents it and flags any sentence that fails without conversation context; its price placement is folded in as the definition's own paragraph.

## Migration — every value to its home

For an offering the owner picks, its ruled values leave the run for their standing homes: prices to the fee schedule, procedures to the operating documents, copy to the website, facts about what the operator has to its own records. The run folder keeps the whole record in stage order; the homes carry the live values. Eight values go to the operator's market map, which LAMP reads, so that its first study of the offering is a measurement rather than a post-mortem: the chosen name and the terms it was chosen against with their volumes and difficulties; the owned-surface decision, or the explicit statement that no page will exist and why; the unit; the buyer, in enough detail to draw a query-family boundary; the price band and the fee-schedule section that will hold it; the value per conversion with its home named, or its absence stated; the cadence or season; and the capacity ceiling in the units the offering is sold in. The first two are unrecoverable once the offering exists. A value living in two places with no derivation between them is the defect this step exists to prevent.

## The displacement offer

The category being proven does not prove that buyers will switch, and no simulation settles switching. For an offering the owner picks, the work therefore closes outward: the definition goes to named prospects the operator already holds, typically from an owned contact roster, as a real offer. The prospects to start with are the ones whose profiles show they already buy this category from an incumbent, because their yes is a displacement, and their objection is the next run's evidence.

Where the operator runs SPAR, the displacement offer is a SPAR campaign: the claims are cited by number in the approach messages, and the decisions table bounds what any message may promise. The offer's results, bookings, questions, forwards and silences, are the first behavioural data the run has touched, and they feed the owner's next ruling round.
