# SAGE Survey — the evidence, at review grade

Survey gives the run a real market to read, so that no later phase has to reason from internal opinion. It produces four bodies of evidence and, conditionally, a fence. Everything downstream cites this material by number.

## 1. The comparables corpus — learn from, never beat

Comparable operators anywhere in the world, studied to learn what sells and how it is sold. An operator on another continent is not a rival; it is a published experiment someone else already paid for.

The discipline is a content-analysis pipeline, run in order:

1. **Registers, not search results.** A frame is populated only from enumerable published lists: statutory or funding registers, trade bodies, quality accreditations, paid directories. Web search may locate a register; a search hit never becomes a frame row. Record every register found: who runs it, entry count, who is structurally shut out.
2. **Documented selection.** Every step between register and frame is written in a coverage note. A cell that ends small is declared a census; a sampled cell states its mechanical rule. An undocumented shrink from register size to a convenient frame size is the classic corruption, and a reviewer will find it.
3. **A codebook frozen blind.** The coding manual is drafted by an agent who has not seen any frame, and frozen before collection. Standing instructions: text only; silence is a value, not a fact; no inference in either direction; a rule two coders would apply differently has failed and escalates. Changing a frozen rule takes a version, a date, and re-coding of everything coded.
4. **An adversarial frame review before collection.** An independent agent attacks the frames as enumerations: proxy populations, quota-shaped cells, censuses dressed as samples, forbidden cross-cell comparisons where register architecture is confounded with geography. Verdict per cell: go, conditional, no-go. Collection runs only on cells that pass, under their conditions.
5. **Verbatim collection, then coding.** Profiles quote what each operator publishes, with capture records (URLs, dates, what was unreachable). Coding applies the frozen codebook; an inter-coder spot check re-codes a mechanical sample blind and orders corrections before any synthesis.
6. **Numbered findings.** The synthesis emits findings as numbered statements citing coded data, so every later document writes "finding 12" instead of restating a claim it might drift.

Alongside the findings, the corpus yields an activity taxonomy with prevalence figures: what fraction of coded operators run each element. Establish's scarcity claims are measured against exactly this.

**The standing caveat, stated in the findings themselves:** the corpus reads what operators publish, and what is bought is not always printed. A module can be routinely sold by phone and appear in no brochure. Absence in the corpus is absence from publication, never absence from the market; treat a surprising absence as a question for the distributor channel below.

## 2. The rival register — the incumbents to beat

The local operators that contest the same bookings, profiled one by one and section-numbered, with the demand signal around them: which buyers already go where, what they pay all-in, the live enquiries, and the meetings behind those signals. This is the half that says who has to be displaced, and it feeds the named-buyer boundary test at Adjudicate and the judge scenarios at Game.

Buyer profiles of the whole catchment, one file per prospect, live with the operator's outreach campaigns rather than in the run: they pay three times, as demand evidence now, an outreach roster later, and the casting pool for Game's judges.

## 3. Distributor evidence — the transaction view

Intermediaries who transact across many operators hold the knowledge published text cannot: transport operators who carry the buyers, booking platforms that see conversion by price and season, brokers and underwriters who know who really runs what, trade bodies whose staff talk to every member. They answer factual aggregate questions cheaply, because answering is their sales call. Ask what is growing, what died, what buyers complain about on the way home; do not ask what to build, and do not ask for any one client's business.

This channel is the corrective for the publication blind spot in the corpus, and the cheapest evidence in the whole phase. A run that skips it should say why.

## 4. The fence — conditional

A product that existed in half-shape before the run (drafts, plans, a manager's local practice, meeting captures) has pre-decided parameters lying around, and a deriving AI will inherit them. For such a product, Survey builds the fence: an index of every file carrying pre-decided or suggestive parameters. The index generates the clean-room blacklist for Game's arms, and it is the update list for stale dimensions once the owner rules.

A wholly new product has no such debris and skips the fence. The test is honest: if no internal document could anchor a deriving agent, there is nothing to fence.

## Outputs

Frames with registers and coverage notes; the frozen codebook; the frame review; the coded corpus with numbered findings and the activity taxonomy; the rival register and demand signals; distributor evidence notes; conditionally, the fence index. All inside the run folder, survey stage, in pipeline order.
