# Decision review

The run's decision cards. Each card is a question, the ways the market answers it with a figure for each, the clerks' recommendation, and a line for the ruling. Rule from the sheet below, in the order that follows it, and open a card where a row raises a doubt. Where a card names another card, it names it by number.

## The ruling sheet

One row a card. *Recommended* reads withheld where no option is carried. *What is held* says whether the recommendation keeps or leaves a value the venue already held. *Third derivation* says where a fresh clerk, reading the market alone, landed against the recommendation.
