# Decision review

The run's decision cards. Each card is a question, the ways the market answers it with a figure for each, the clerks' recommendation, and a line for the ruling. Rule from the sheet below, in the order set out under "The order of ruling" and not from the top of the table down, and open a card where a row raises a doubt. Where a card names another card, it names it by number.

## The ruling sheet

One row a card. *Your part* says what the row asks of you: confirm, rule, or nothing; a row reading *for research* owes you nothing, since the clerks make the observation it names before it comes back. *The recommendation* is the first sentence of the card's plain words. *Ruled* says whether you have already ruled the question; where a card's recommendation departs from your ruling, your ruling stands until you rule again, and where the card's evidence was rewritten after you ruled, the row asks you to rule again or confirm. The card itself holds the counts, what the business already held, and where a second reading of the market alone landed.
