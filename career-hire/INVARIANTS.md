# Invariants — HIRE

Rules the rest of the methodology does not contradict. Where a procedure, a module or an example here disagrees with one, the invariant wins and the other is the bug.

- No document is begun before `hirer.md` and `interview.md` exist for this application; a repair to one already under way is made against them.
- A fact about the candidate enters from the candidate's words or a document, with its source beside it; what the writer worked out is an assumption in the register.
- What the candidate said stays off the page stays off, whatever the sources hold.
- An earlier document for another post supplies facts and no sentences.
- A judge's brief holds the advertisement and what the candidate or a named source said, and nothing the session inferred.
- The candidate's own wording changes only with a reason recorded where the candidate will read it.
- An application does not wait on the candidate. What only the candidate could settle is decided by a stated default and entered in the assumption register with a confidence score, where the candidate agrees with it or corrects it.
- Where an employer's rule for applicants says who drafts, that order is kept.
- The methodology, its modules and its cases name roles; the candidate, the employers and the clients of any run are named only in that run's own folder, outside this repository.
