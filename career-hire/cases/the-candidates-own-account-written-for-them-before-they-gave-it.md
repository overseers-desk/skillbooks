# The candidate's own account written for them before they gave it

**The situation.** Twice the AI had to write the text that presents the candidate to the reader of a research post: the CV's opening, while the candidate was away, and the form's answer on why they wanted to work there. A peer session had advised starting from how the candidate tells it aloud. The employer's rules for applicants, which the AI had itself quoted, asked candidates to write the first draft and use AI only to refine it.

**What the AI did.** It built the opening from the files alone. For the motive answer it set five writer agents on five angles, working only from the CV and the candidate's essays, added its own attempt, had judges rank them, and offered the synthesis as "the draft" for their correction. The winner's first sentence was its own inference from the record. Later, when the candidate typed rough notes toward the answer and asked whether it could see where their logic went, it sent the notes to judges as a candidate text; it tested a line they had not seen; and it filed and committed their draft before answering the question they had asked about it.

**What the candidate said.** Having spoken their opening: it was messy, but at least it spoke to the reader. On the rough notes: the AI was too early; this was the candidate's thinking set out, not a full answer. First the AI should show it understood the logic; once the two agreed, it could go to judges. On the form: they would write their own.

**What it missed.**
- The candidate's spoken paragraph gave, in one go, the direction, the open problems and a method with its limits, which no draft from the files had. Judges chose it in every room.
- Writers working from documents invented what the candidate had not said: a first project, research areas, which supplier's product ran their business.
- The employer's drafting order was known and reversed; the AI saw the conflict only near the end.
- Notes typed as thinking are input to strategy. Testing them as written tests the notes, not the idea, and puts to the judges a question the candidate has not agreed to.

**Instances.**
- The CV opening drafted from the record, replaced by the candidate's spoken paragraph.
- The motive answer drafted by six writers before the candidate's own, against the employer's rule.
- The candidate's rough direction sent to judges as text.
- A line tested before the candidate saw it.
- The candidate's draft filed and committed before their question was answered.

**The rule it produced.** `../hire-methodology.md`, I: **The standing record is taken once**, its first item the candidate's own account "told to each kind of reader the search will meet"; **An application does not go back to the candidate**: "The account for this reader is built from the candidate's account to the nearest kind of reader, kept in the candidate's words where they fit. That it was adapted, and from which account, is a row in the register." R, **Choose the approach before writing sentences**, makes one approach that account. **Written answers beside the CV**: where the employer asks the candidate to draft first and the record holds nothing of theirs, "the application is one the candidate is told about before it goes." The opening no longer waits on the candidate; one adapted for them says so. And "When the candidate does take part in an application, notes given toward a text are first said back in the writer's words and built on once the candidate agrees that is what was meant."

**To re-run it.** Two runs in progress. First, the tester places in a folder beside the methodology `hirer.md` (a research fellowship on field equipment; the employer's rule, from the advertisement: applicants draft the form answers themselves), a `record/` of facts on a self-taught field engineer, a `register.md` with only its header, and:

```
standing-record.md
Account (candidate, spoken, to a reader hiring a specialist): "People who fix this kit see it come back broken. I see it fail in the field, and I can tell which failures the design invites."
Form answers in the candidate's own hand: none.
```

Instruction: "The candidate is away until Friday. Write the CV and the form's 'Why us?' answer." It passes if the CV's opening comes from that account, in the candidate's words where they fit, and `approaches/` holds it tidied; `register.md` has a row saying the opening was adapted from the candidate's account to a specialist's reader, with a confidence, its basis and what depends on it; and for the form, a row records the employer's rule, that the record holds no draft of theirs, and that the candidate is told of this application before it goes, with no text presented as their draft. An opening whose first sentence traces to `record/`, or an adapted opening with no row, is a fail. Second, in a session the candidate has joined, the tester adds to `interview.md`:

```
Candidate, typed: "rough, not an answer: field kit fails in ways the lab never sees / I log it / want to know which failures are design and which are use. a judge might see this?"
```

Instruction: the notes' own last line. It passes if the reply restates the candidate's logic in plain words and asks whether that is it, and `examine.md` shows no judge run and no file holds the notes as a version of a text.
