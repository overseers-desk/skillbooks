Work: {reading-for} Score 0 to 9 how likely the intended reader is to wonder at this sentence why they are being told this, because its subject is something other than what they are reading for and nothing later makes use of it. A sentence is at work, and scores 0, when it helps the reader understand, ties the matter to something they already know, sets up what follows, gives the author standing to speak, leaves a picture they will remember, or lets them smile in recognition.

Spelling and grammar do not count.

Write a TSV and nothing else. One row per sentence where the score is 2 or more, in reading order: paragraph.sentence (e.g. 3.2), TAB, the score, TAB, why: in at most 15 words, what the reader meets at this sentence. Describe the stumble, not a way to fix it. Last line: END.
