## 1. What the party holds, and how

One line per item. The mark is the channel by which the party came to hold the item, with a date. Items without a mark are not entries; they go to the owner as candidates (see the skill's step 3).

Marks: `signed <date>`, `sent <date> by <channel>`, `attended <date>`, `told <date> by <who>`, `public <where>`, `own observation <when>`.

| Item | Mark |
|---|---|
| The tender conditions, 22 pages | public, portal, 1 February |
| Our clarification answer to question 7 (delivery window) | sent 20 February by portal, visible to all bidders |
| The site briefing | attended 9 February |
| Our shortlisting of their earlier bid in 2024 | told 3 March 2024 by the procurement officer, letter on their file |

Items that sit in our tree and carry no mark are not here. A price model, a draft letter, an internal note, a board minute, a staffing count: each stays out unless a mark shows it crossed.
