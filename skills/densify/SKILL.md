# Densify

Densification shrinks a text while keeping every concrete specific and epistemic marker; the office methodology's Densify rule sets the standard, this skill is the method. The author's own judgment of the result is contaminated: a verifier that already knows what every fragment means reads nothing as lost or too tight. Verification therefore leaves the session: a cold reader and a differ, in fresh contexts, gate every round.

Before round 1, obtain the purpose line: one owner-stated sentence naming what the text exists to make its reader do, and what the enemy is. It steers the triage, and what it omits the triage forgives, so anything mandatory belongs in it. The owner supplies it; failing that, derive it from context and show it with the delivery so the owner can correct it.

Each round:

1. **Compress.** Rewrite for density: cut connective tissue, hedging, self-narrating structure, and rationale a competent reader supplies unaided. Keep every concrete specific (number, date, name, path, command, token, threshold, magnitude absolute or relative) and every epistemic marker (checked-empty, unobtainable, not-attempted, approximated, inferred). Replacing a specific with a generic ("three retries" becoming "retries") is summarisation, not densification.

2. **Cold read.** A fresh subagent receives only the dense text and the reader's natural frame (where the text sits, nothing of this session), and writes out everything the text teaches it: what is required, of whom, in what situation, what satisfies it, what violates it, why it exists as far as it can tell. The expansion is the only form its understanding exists in. Asking a reader whether it understood measures nothing: over-compression surfaces as confident gap-filling, not as "I don't know".

3. **Diff.** A second fresh subagent receives the expansion and the original, and not the dense text, so it cannot excuse over-tightness as "there if you squint". It lists every thing the original teaches that the expansion lacks or contradicts: a bare list, no ranking or classification. Extra material in the expansion is not a loss.

4. **Triage.** You, warm, holding the dense text and the purpose line, rule each listed loss MATERIAL or IMMATERIAL. MATERIAL only if a reader holding the dense text alone would, for the absence, accept what the original forbids or reject what it allows, measured against the purpose line. IMMATERIAL covers content a competent reader supplies unaided from the dense text, and explanation the original carried to justify itself to its first audience, unless behavior changes without it. One MATERIAL loss fails the round: reinsert it in the fewest words that restore it and open the next round. The stop is a round with no MATERIAL loss, not a round count; convergence typically takes a few rounds.

5. **Deliver** the final text with the purpose line and the last round's triage, so the caller audits the gate instead of trusting feel. A why the cold reader states that the original cannot corroborate is flagged to the owner as a source defect, not repaired: densify cannot restore what was not written. Drafts and intermediate rounds stay with you.

Judge the result against the document's job, not word count: a README's reader is deciding whether to adopt, an SOP's reader is mid-procedure, a report's reader wants the finding. For human-facing artifacts the result also carries the LHD rule: it should not read AI-authored.
