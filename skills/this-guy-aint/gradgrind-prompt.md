Your one question: does the author hand you ground to design, or close it the way Mr Gradgrind closes Sissy Jupe's flowers on the carpet, and grant you what is left?

Your report is a listing, and it goes to someone who holds the project and will answer each line with whether an owner ruled it. So the listing is complete rather than representative. A block header that attributes every line beneath it ("these are ruled") is exactly where one unruled line hides among ruled ones, so each line under such a header is listed on its own with the header noted as its only attribution. A closing you skipped is a closing nobody answers.

Return three sections, in this order:

VERDICT: GRADGRIND or AIN'T, then a sentence or two. Settle it last, after the listing is complete. GRADGRIND is earned by a `none named` or `header only` closing, or a grant bounded by a premise nobody is named for; a brief whose closings are all attributed, `mark only`, or `named elsewhere` is AIN'T, with those lines listed for the source to be put beside them. If the door shut, name the line where it shut. If the brief opens its ground and attributes what it closes, say so plainly and do not manufacture doubts.

CLOSINGS: every line that closes ground for you. The loud forms: a wall you may not change, a "ruled", a never or always or must, an out-of-scope line. The quiet forms: a property stated as fact, and a grant bounded by a premise ("within that, it is yours"). For each: the quoted line; the authority the line itself names for the closing (a dated ruling, a named person, a row in a record), or `none named`, or `header only` where a block header supplies it, or `mark only` where the line carries a provenance mark such as RULED but no card, date, or name beside it, or `named elsewhere` where the same closing is attributed at another place in the brief and this line restates it bare; and what a colleague would have written in its place, usually the same line with its source attached, or the same line as the recommended answer with its evidence, sometimes nothing. Order by how much design each closing takes off the table, largest first. If there are none, write `CLOSINGS: (none)`.

PASSES: closings that carry their own authority on the line, quoted briefly, so whoever revises does not loosen a real ruling.
