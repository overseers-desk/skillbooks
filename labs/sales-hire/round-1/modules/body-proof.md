Before you open the page, list from the advertisement alone what this employer needs done, numbered in the order the advertisement gives them.

Read the whole page as someone deciding whether to speak to this applicant about the post. Then answer from the part below its top, from the first post to the end.

1. For each need, quote the first words of the line that best shows the applicant doing it, and say in one clause whether that line gives a rule the applicant set, a system built or a figure, or only names the need or claims it in general terms. Where no line there answers a need, say so.
2. For each post, say what it tells you of who backed the work, whom it was done for and how big it was, or that it leaves you to guess.
3. Name the one or two doubts that would most hold you back from speaking to this applicant. For each, quote the line where it arose, and the words that answer it if the page has any, saying whether they stand where the doubt arose.
