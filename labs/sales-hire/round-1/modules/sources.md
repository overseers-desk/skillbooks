Trace every figure and every claim of fact in the document, from its first line to its last, to its source.

Where the source is the candidate's own words, it is the quotation the candidate file or `brief.md` records, with where they were said, read to the end of its sentence. A document is read in its own file, not as an entry or a note quotes it: where a file quotes another, read that other. A quoted claim is read against its source past the quotation marks, to the end of the sentence.

A row holds when the document's verb and its limiting words are the source's. The source's year or its nouns under a stronger verb, or without the place or the "most" that bounded the claim, is a step past it. A superlative needs a source a reader could open, and a title or a date is given as the referee would give it.

Write one row for each claim, in the document's order: its line, the document's words, the source's file and place, the source's words to the end of their sentence, and the verdict. The verdict is "holds"; or "past its source", with what the source supports; or "no source found", with the files searched.
