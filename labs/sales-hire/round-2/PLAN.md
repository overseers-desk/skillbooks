## Carried over from round 1

Round 1 left some versions neither kept nor dropped. Each was written and run through writers at a small size, and showed neither a gain large enough to keep nor a loss large enough to drop. What becomes of each:

- **`v9-leads` and `v10-leads-stays-out`, what the first lines of the page answer:** carried over as one dimension. It has a new ground from the real applications: of eight pages written again, the two that fell were the two whose earlier page opened in the post's own frame and whose new page did not. What would settle it is sixteen examined pages a text, or a cheaper design that compares openings over one body.
- **`v12-body`:** not carried over as written. Its pages ranked a third of a place better, inside the spread between pages of one text, and none of the four did what the version asks, so its wording does not survive the checks. The row below on what Examine may do to a page is where that belongs.
- **`v11-several`, several pages and a choice among them:** kept. In each of four sets of four examined pages an agent following its text chose the page the lab's pair judges preferred, and the chosen pages rank 0.62 better than their sets' means (a one-sided test puts that above zero at about 4 per cent; four sets cannot show half a rank). It is released with its price, that each application is written several times, and is not a dimension of round 2.

`v13-first-post` and `v14-outside-trace` were dropped and are not carried over.

The four changes made between 26 and 28 November (the sales wording, duplication removed, what the employer needs done as a thought, and the candidate file as the writers' record) are kept together in the method at `33dd2626`: with all four in, pages rank 0.34 better than the pages written before round 1 over eight posts, with a standard error of 0.27 (round 1's worklog of 28 November). They were not told apart, and telling them apart is not carried over, since no loss was found for any one of them to explain.

## Shape

Round 1's loop and its lessons. Few texts, each a named set of replacements on the released text. Writers blind, effort set on the command line and recorded, examined pages. The released text written in the same batch and judged against itself. Every examined page placed among the same nine other candidates' pages and compared in pairs, no follow-up question. What counts for each dimension fixed before any count. The running session directs; scripts and subagents write, read and count.

Sizes round 1 measured: pages of one text differ by about a rank; eight pages a text see about 1.2 ranks four times in five, and sixteen about one rank; an examined page takes 30 to 55 minutes of one writer and 10 to 26 dollars as reported; a judge who gives the whole order costs about 18 cents.
