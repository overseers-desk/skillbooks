# Plan for round 2

Round 2 has not started. It starts when round 1 is closed, with each of its versions kept, dropped or left open, and when the ten CVs now being written for the candidate's real applications under the released text have been delivered and judged. It starts from the method as the owner then releases it, and it is held to the section "What a round is" of `../ROADMAP.md`.

## What it will take up, in order of ground

Each row is a change to something the series keeps. W means seen on pages written by writers, H seen by hand or by re-measuring, R reasoned and untried.

| Dimension | The change | Ground | What would count |
|---|---|---|---|
| What Examine may do to a page | The finished page goes out only where it stands no worse than its own first full draft; the rule for stopping names that | W: over six runs the checks moved a page by a standard deviation of 1.1 ranks either way and bettered it by half a rank on the mean, and a first draft's rank says nothing of its finished page's | finished pages rank better than under the released text, and fewer end below their first draft |
| The method's own judges | The approach and the last version are chosen by where a page stands among other candidates' pages, not between two versions of one person's page | W: between texts the winner of a pair stands no better among other candidates than the loser, and a rewording can decide a pair. Needs other people's CVs in a consumer's folder, which is the owner's word | the approach chosen ranks better among other candidates than the one the pair judges choose |
| Which check earns its cost | Each of checks 1 to 5 left out in turn | W for the checks as a whole: half a rank for two to three times the minutes; none measured alone. He writes a hundred or more CVs | a check whose removal costs no rank is dropped, and the minutes saved are reported |
| The writer's conditions | Reasoning effort, and the office's instructions loaded or not, each against the same text | R: untried; the writers' profile now sets a higher effort than round 1 ran at | rank and minutes under each |
| A line under the name that names the kind | The page carries the kind of post the candidate is put forward as, above the summary | R: a reader files a candidate under a kind within seconds; no page compared in round 1 has such a line | a blind reader says what the candidate is, and rank |
| The length of the top | A limit on what stands above the first heading | H: the worst-placed pages' summaries ran 226 to 323 words and the best 114 to 170, which goes with who wrote the page | rank, and what the reader had no use for |
| The first reader and the one who decides | A second seat, the screener who can refuse and cannot hire | R: roadmap A2, untried | a page passes both seats |

Whatever of round 1's versions is left open comes first, with the number of pages that settles it. What the ten real CVs show the released text gets wrong is added above this table as it is found; a fault found there outranks a reasoned row.

## Shape

Round 1's loop and its lessons. Few texts, each a named set of replacements on the released text. Writers blind, effort set on the command line and recorded, finished pages. The released text written in the same batch and judged against itself. Every finished page placed among the same nine other candidates' pages and compared in pairs, no follow-up question. What counts for each dimension fixed before any count. The running session directs; scripts and subagents write, read and count.

Sizes round 1 measured: pages of one text differ by about a rank; eight pages a text see about 1.2 ranks four times in five, and sixteen about one rank; a finished page takes 30 to 55 minutes of one writer and 10 to 26 dollars as reported; a judge who gives the whole order costs about 18 cents.
