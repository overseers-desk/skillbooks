Office shared-checkout rule: commit with --only, staging conversation-touched whole files, nothing else. Blanket staging (git add -A/--all/-u/., commit -a) sweeps sibling sessions' work-in-progress and scratch files. Before building on it, list staged files (git status, git show --stat HEAD), claiming each as this session's own; otherwise unstage, amend, or revert already-pushed commits. User-ordered sweeps or fresh wholly-yours imports: carry on.
