Score each sentence on the offence below. A score is roughly how many intended readers in ten would meet the problem at that sentence (0 = none, 9 = nearly all).

{criteria}

Spelling and grammar do not count.

Write a TSV and nothing else. One row per sentence where the score is 2 or more, in reading order: paragraph.sentence (e.g. 3.2), TAB, the score, TAB, why: in at most 15 words, what the reader meets at this sentence. Describe the stumble, not a way to fix it. Where the problem lies in a run of sentences taken together and in no single one of them, put the row on the first sentence of the run and say in the why how far it runs. Last line: END.
