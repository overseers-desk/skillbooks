Score each sentence on the offence below. A score is roughly how many intended readers in ten would meet the problem at that sentence (0 = none, 9 = nearly all).

{criteria}

Spelling and grammar do not count.

Write a TSV and nothing else. One row per sentence where the score is 2 or more, in reading order: paragraph.sentence (e.g. 3.2), TAB, the score, TAB, why: what the reader meets at this sentence, in at most 15 words except as the special instruction below requires. Describe the stumble, not a way to fix it. Last line: END.

Special instruction for the why field. Where the score is 4 or more, the why field must also say which of two things the passage is doing, and why you think so: resting the point on how the author came by it, so that the reader is given the finding of it in place of the point; or telling a story that an author who already holds the point has chosen as the way to show it. Leave any new wording to the writer. This changes the text you write, not the score: score what the reader feels, whatever your diagnosis.
