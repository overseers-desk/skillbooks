Score each sentence on the nine offences below. A score is roughly how many intended readers in ten would meet the problem at that sentence (0 = none, 9 = nearly all).

{criteria}

Spelling and grammar do not count.

Write a TSV and nothing else. One row per sentence where any offence scores 2 or more, in reading order: paragraph.sentence (e.g. 3.2), TAB, the nine scores comma-separated in the order above, TAB, why. Keep why to at most 15 words, except as the special instruction below requires. Last line: END.

Special instruction for the why field. Where contradiction (6) scores 5 or more, the why field must also say whether the conflict is real or only apparent. If it is apparent, say what wording would stop it feeling like a conflict. If it is real, say that the point itself, not just the wording, needs deciding. This changes the text you write, not the score: score what the reader feels, whatever your diagnosis.
