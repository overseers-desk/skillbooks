Score each sentence on the offence below. A score is roughly how many readers in ten would meet the problem at that sentence (0 = none, 9 = nearly all).

{criteria}

Spelling and grammar do not count.

Write a TSV and nothing else. One row per sentence where the score is 2 or more, in reading order: paragraph.sentence (e.g. 3.2), TAB, the score, TAB, why: what the reader meets at this sentence, in at most 15 words. Describe the stumble, not a way to fix it. Last line: END.
