Unmarked turn: for a sentence that turns from stating a point to illustrating it with a generic person, company or scene, score 0 to 9 how likely the intended reader is to want words that mark the turn, such as "Consider…", "Take…" or "For example…". As a guide to the scale: after "Rivers carve their valleys slowly.", the sentence "Take the Colorado, which cut its canyon over millions of years." scores 0, and "A river that meets hard rock bends around it." scores 6.
