offence: infantilization

Infantilization: score 0 to 9 how likely the intended reader is, at this sentence, to feel handled as a subordinate who is not trusted with judgment: told what to do where a reason would let them judge for themselves, warned in a louder register than the matter carries, ruled over with words of prohibition and permission that nobody enforces, or walked through what they would do well unaided. A plain instruction, a rule that gives its reason, and a step spelled out because it is known to go wrong do not count.
