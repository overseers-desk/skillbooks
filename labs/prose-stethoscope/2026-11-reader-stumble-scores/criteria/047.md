Glib: score 0 to 9 how likely a reader is to find this sentence glib: a confident line that sounds settled and says less than it seems to, or a line shaped as a saying where a plain statement belongs: two clauses joined by "and" or "so", the second capping the first, as in "The kitchen is where a restaurant is made, and the menu is how it is sold" or "A recipe is a list of steps, and steps are what a cook can follow"; nearly every reader hears that shape, whatever the line says. A quotation, a saying offered as one, or verse does not count.
