What my grandfather said

My grandfather kept a hardware shop for forty years. When a customer asked why he stocked so many sizes of screw, he would answer: "The shelf is where a shop makes its promise, and the till is where it keeps it."

I thought of him last week, when our supplier dropped half its range. As the saying goes, home is where the heart is, and for him the shop was home.

We have ordered the missing sizes from a second supplier.
