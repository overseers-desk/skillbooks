Filing supplier invoices

Invoices from about forty suppliers arrive in the accounts inbox each week. This procedure files each one in the shared Invoices folder and records it in the invoice log.

Read each email through before filing its invoice, since a later message in a thread sometimes corrects the invoice attached to an earlier one. Take the invoice date from the invoice itself.

File each invoice in its supplier's folder, named as the invoice date, the supplier and the invoice number, such as 2026-03-04 Northwind 1182.pdf. Keep each supplier's folder flat. Attachments larger than 10 MB are usually scanned statements rather than invoices, so open one only when its email says it holds an invoice.

The supplier portal marks an invoice Paid as soon as a payment is scheduled, days before the money leaves the account, so take the payment date from the invoice's detail page and not from the badge. Payments go through the accounts team, who check each invoice against its purchase order, so this procedure files invoices and pays none. Leave every email in the accounts inbox where it is, since the auditors read the inbox as the record of what arrived.

Contact with suppliers stays with the accounts team. Where an invoice looks wrong, note what is wrong in the log, and the accounts team will take it up with the supplier.

What is known about each supplier is in suppliers.md, and the naming rules and their exceptions are in naming.md. Where an invoice could belong to two suppliers, file it under the one that issued it and note the other in the log; if you find a better rule, use it and say why in the log.

The log has a column for each field the accounts team uses, and a row left half filled tells them which invoice still needs reading. When every invoice from the week is filed and logged, the task is done.
