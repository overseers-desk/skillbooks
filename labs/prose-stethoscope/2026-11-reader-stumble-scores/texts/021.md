Night shift

For a year I ran the night shift at one of a parcel company's four depots. Most of the late departures began at handover, when one crew left before the next had picked up the scanners.

The handover is where the delay shows, and the night shift is how I learned to see it.

We moved the scanner swap ten minutes earlier. Late departures fell by half within a month.

Where the first depot took a month to change, the other three took a week each. The company made the earlier swap its rule at all four.
