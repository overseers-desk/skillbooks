Feeding out

The shed is where we keep the hay. I carry two bales down to the yard before seven, and the horses are fed by half past.

Last winter a bale went mouldy at the back of the stack. I pulled the stack apart and that is how I found the leak in the roof.

The roofer came on the Tuesday. This is what he charged: two hundred dollars for the sheet and an hour's labour.
