Filing supplier invoices

Invoices from about forty suppliers arrive in the accounts inbox each week. This procedure files each one in the shared Invoices folder and records it in the invoice log.

**CRITICAL:** You MUST read every invoice email in full before you file anything. To find the invoice date, look near the top of the invoice for a line labelled Date or Invoice date, and read the day, the month and the year from it.

File each invoice in its supplier's folder, named as the invoice date, the supplier and the invoice number, such as 2026-03-04 Northwind 1182.pdf. Creating subfolders inside a supplier's folder is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a serious violation of this procedure. Never open an attachment larger than 10 MB.

The supplier portal marks an invoice Paid as soon as a payment is scheduled, days before the money leaves the account, so take the payment date from the invoice's detail page and not from the badge. Payments go through the accounts team, who check each invoice against its purchase order, so this procedure files invoices and pays none. Leave every email in the accounts inbox where it is, since the auditors read the inbox as the record of what arrived.

**IMPORTANT:** Under no circumstances are you authorised to contact a supplier directly. Where an invoice looks wrong, note what is wrong in the log, and the accounts team will take it up with the supplier.

Before you start, read suppliers.md first, then naming.md, then the README in the Invoices folder, in exactly that order, and do not skip any of them. Where an invoice could belong to two suppliers, file it under the one that issued it and note the other in the log; if you find a better rule, use it and say why in the log.

Remember that an invoice is a document asking for payment, and that its total is usually at the bottom. When every invoice from the week is filed and logged, the task is done.
