Pricing note

We raised the price of the weekday session in March. Bookings held, and the complaints we expected did not come.

Price is what a customer sees first, and value is what brings them back. A full diary is a promise, so an empty slot is a broken one.

The weekend session stays at its old price until we have a second month of figures.
