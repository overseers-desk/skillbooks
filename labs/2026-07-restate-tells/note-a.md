# Morning Watering — Glasshouse and Yard

This procedure covers the morning watering round for the glasshouse benches and the outdoor yard, including moisture checks, watering order, and closing the taps.

Check the glasshouse first. Push a finger into the mix of one pot per bench; water the bench only if it is dry two knuckles down. Seedling trays dry faster than pots, so they are checked every morning without exception. Checking before watering prevents overwatering and keeps the plants healthy.

Water in this order:

- Seedling trays
- Hanging baskets
- Bench pots
- Following this order means every plant gets water before the heat arrives

Do not water the cactus bench between May and August. Watering the cactus bench in winter rots the roots and constitutes avoidable plant loss.

Approximately 10-15% of customers will ask about the watering while they browse; answer briefly and carry on with the round.

The yard hose lives on the reel by the potting shed. When the round is finished, return hoses to the standard storage location or an equivalent secure spot. As noted above, the cactus bench is not watered during the winter months, since winter watering causes root rot and avoidable stock losses.

Close the taps. Check the pressure gauge reads zero. That is the rule. Zero means closed.

## Summary

Each morning: check moisture first, water in order (trays, baskets, pots), skip the cactus bench in winter, coil the hoses, and close the taps. Careful morning watering keeps stock healthy and the yard ready for customers.
