# Morning Watering — Glasshouse and Yard

Check the glasshouse first. Push a finger into the mix of one pot per bench; water the bench only if it is dry two knuckles down. Seedling trays dry faster than pots: check them every morning.

Water in this order:

- Seedling trays
- Hanging baskets
- Bench pots

Skip the cactus bench between May and August; winter watering rots the roots.

Customers sometimes ask about the watering while they browse. Answer briefly and carry on with the round.

The yard hose lives on the reel by the potting shed. Coil it back after the round.

Close the taps and check the pressure gauge reads zero.
