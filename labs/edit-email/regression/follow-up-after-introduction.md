# Regression case: follow-up to an officer who made an introduction

writing: email

**Scenario.** At a state tourism conference, Sam (owner of Hillside Farm) spoke with Toni, a state tourism support officer, at several stands during the day. Toni's last act was introducing Sam to Shane, cultural consultant at a regional First Nations museum, who offered to connect Sam with Corey at the museum about a cultural partnership. Sam asked for a follow-up email to Toni about that conversation, sent from Sam's address with two staff copied, every name of place and person spelt correctly. Sam is overseas from the day after next for a month; John, the estate and guest experience manager, is to carry it meanwhile.

The case exists because the email took five drafts, and four corrections from Sam, to reach a sendable version, and edit-email never ran: the authoring agent drafted directly while Sam read every line. A change to the skill is tested by whether it would have caught, unaided, what Sam had to catch.

## Non-skilled (round 1)

Subject: "Hillside Farm follow-up: mentoring sign-up, tourism board, Shane". Three numbered items, one per conversation of the day (a mentoring sign-up, an introduction to the regional tourism board, Shane), each with Sam's travel dates and a staff contact.

## Sam's corrections, in order

1. Scope. "This email is about the last meeting, not all meetings. Subject should say so too." The draft had followed up the whole day because the officer appeared in two recordings.
2. A fact from memory. Sam had said the mill dated from "roughly 1919"; the business's own records give circa 1910. The draft carried Sam's spoken figure. "1919 is top of my head, what is real?"
3. No purpose. "Your email mentioned nothing about what problem we want to solve."
4. Framing. The fix to 3 put the problem as a lack ("we do not know who to ask or how"). "Do not write as if the only meaning of the connection is solve the problem; framing is important." The sent version leads with interest in the land's older story and a relationship with the museum; storytelling for guests is one thing it might lead to.

## Human reference (as sent)

> Hi Toni,
>
> Thank you for introducing me to Shane of the regional museum at the conference on Thursday afternoon. I am the owner of Hillside Farm at [road address], at the foot of the mountain where [mountain road] begins.
>
> Our knowledge of the site begins with the arrowroot mill that operated here from around 1910. The land's story is far older, and I would like to learn it. Shane told me the museum holds artefacts from close to our property, including stone axes, and he offered to connect me with Corey at the museum about a cultural partnership. I would welcome getting to know the museum and its people, and in time sharing that history with our guests in whatever way the museum thinks right.
>
> You mentioned you would connect me to Shane and send the other things we spoke about. I would be grateful for both. I am overseas from Saturday for a month, so please include John ([address]), our Estate and Guest Experience Manager, who can visit the museum or welcome Shane and Corey to the farm while I am away.
>
> Thank you again.
>
> Sam

## Companion emails the same afternoon

Two more went out within the hour, with two further corrections. An enquiry to a state science-and-innovation connector service carried Sam's travel dates, which do nothing for an officer answering an eligibility question ("what purpose does it solve?"), and a plan Sam had not made ("we plan to take part in [the training course] and then apply"). A note to Shane lost the travel dates in the over-correction and needed them back: there the absence is what explains why John, not Sam, will meet him. The test both applied is the reader's: what does this reader do or understand differently because of this sentence.

## Gate

A regression is a skill run that, given the round-1 brief and the meeting record, sends any of: a follow-up scoped wider than the meeting asked about; a figure from the speaker's memory where a business record holds one; a request with no purpose stated; a purpose framed only as a lack; a sentence its reader does nothing with; a commitment the sender did not make. The stories behind these sit in the holotapes repository under `behavioural-failures/`, filenames carrying `writing`.
