# Test 16: what belongs in the candidate file

- Date: 28 November 2026
- Model: Sonnet 5.5, one fresh agent with no context
- Under test: `sales-hire/hire-methodology.md`, the passage on the candidate file under "What a run produces", the row `corrections` of its format table, and the first two sentences of "The candidate reviews"
- Rounds: one

## The prompt

> You keep one candidate's file for her job applications. Applications are written by other agents under the method in `sales-hire/` (read `hire-methodology.md` there; read nothing else in that repository and write nothing anywhere).
>
> Writers have sent you six items about the candidate and ask what to do with each. For each one, say in one or two lines where it goes and what happens to it, by the method as written. Quote the sentence of the method you relied on for each.
>
> 1. "Should the line on languages sit above or below Education?"
> 2. "How many people reported to her directly at the logistics firm?"
> 3. "May her eight years running retail stores answer an advertisement's ask for 'enterprise experience'?"
> 4. On reading a draft she said: "Don't call me Head of Operations there. My title was Operations Coordinator."
> 5. On reading a draft she said: "The summary reads like a brochure. Make it two lines."
> 6. "Is she willing to work on site in Rotterdam three days a week?"
>
> Finish with anything in the method's text that left you unsure where one of these belongs, quoting the words that were unclear.

## The reply, and the standard answer

| Item | Standard answer | The agent's answer | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, where a line sits | The method's: settled in Reframe and Examine; not the file | Settled by the writer in Reframe and Examine; not the file, not the keeper | none |
| 2, a fact only she holds | The file, as an open question, after the record is searched | Record searched first; then the file's `open` part and the register | none |
| 3, whether a fact serves an ask | The method's: decided in the application; nothing goes to the candidate | The writer decides and registers it; the page shows the stores' scale and does not use the label; nothing goes back to her | none |
| 4, a title corrected | The file: the words are the fact | The file, in that post's subject, with her words; the draft repaired and read whole | none |
| 5, how the page reads | Not the file: acted on in the draft, and a case for the method | Not the file; acted on, recorded in `examine.md`, a case in `cases/` | none |
| 6, where she will work | The file: her decision; a default where she has not said | The file's record of where she will work; else a default marked unconfirmed and a register row | none |

## What the agent was unsure of

- Whether item 5 also goes in the part `corrections`, whose row then read "What the candidate said in correcting a draft". Minor; the row now reads "about a fact or about what may be said".
- Whether "there" in item 4 means one application or the post everywhere. The method gives no rule; the agent took it as the post. Minor.
- For a gap of fact such as a team size, whether a writer's own decision may reach the page or only the register. Minor, and outside the passage under test.
- That the method names no role for a keeper answering writers. Outside the passage under test.

## Edits made in this session

The opening of the passage on the candidate file says what problem the file solves, what it holds, and that how a page is written is the method's work; the sentence on defaults says "about the candidate"; "The candidate reviews" distinguishes a correction of a fact from an objection to how a document is written; after the run, the row `corrections` was brought in line.
