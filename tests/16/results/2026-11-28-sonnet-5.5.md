# Test 16: what belongs in the candidate file

- Date: 28 November 2026
- Model: Sonnet 5.5, one fresh agent with no context
- Under test: `sales-hire/hire-methodology.md`, the passage on the candidate file under "What a run produces", its format table, and the first two sentences of "The candidate reviews"
- Rounds: two, the second after the format lost its part for what the candidate says in correcting a draft

## The prompt

> You keep one candidate's file for her job applications. Applications are written by other agents under the method in `sales-hire/` (read `hire-methodology.md` there; read nothing else in that repository and write nothing anywhere).
>
> Writers have sent you six items about the candidate and ask what to do with each. For each one, say in one or two lines where it goes and what happens to it, by the method as written, naming the part of the candidate file and the fields you would fill where it goes into the file. Quote the sentence of the method you relied on for each.
>
> 1. "Should the line on languages sit above or below Education?"
> 2. "How many people reported to her directly at the logistics firm?"
> 3. "May her eight years running retail stores answer an advertisement's ask for 'enterprise experience'?"
> 4. On reading a draft she said: "Don't call me Head of Operations there. My title was Operations Coordinator."
> 5. On reading a draft she said: "The summary reads like a brochure. Make it two lines."
> 6. On reading a draft she said: "Take the salary figure off. I never want pay on a page."
>
> Finish with anything in the method's text that left you unsure where one of these belongs, quoting the words that were unclear.

## The reply, and the standard answer

| Item | Standard answer | The agent's answer | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, where a line sits | The method's: settled in Reframe and Examine; not the file | Settled by the writer in Reframe and Examine; not the file, not the keeper | none |
| 2, a fact only she holds | The file, as an open question, after the record is searched | Record searched first; then the file's `open` part and the register | none |
| 3, whether a fact serves an ask | The method's: decided in the application; nothing goes to the candidate | The writer decides and registers it; the page shows the stores' scale and does not use the label; nothing goes back to her | none |
| 4, a title corrected | The file, as the fact now stands: the words are the fact | `facts`, in that post's subject: the entry's `value` becomes the title she gave, with her sentence as `words` and where she said it as `said`; the earlier title is replaced | none |
| 5, how the page reads | Not the file: acted on in the draft, and a case for the method | Not the file; acted on, recorded in `examine.md`, a case in `cases/` | none |
| 6, a figure she wants off every page | The file, as a ruling: her decision about her own information | `rulings`, with her words; the entries it concerns set to `include: never` | none |

## What the agent was unsure of

- Whether a standing wish about length, such as "two lines", could be a ruling, since the row `rulings` then read "about their own applications". Minor; the row now reads "about their own information".
- A remark that is both, such as a number she dislikes the look of: the method gives no test for it. Minor.
- Who adds a default when a writer asks the keeper, and whether a question about one post sits in the subject's `open` or the top-level one. Outside the passage under test.

## Edits made in this session

The passage on the candidate file says what problem the file solves, what it holds, and that how a page is written is the method's work. What the candidate says in correcting a draft is entered as what is now so, a fact as the fact and a decision as a ruling, and a remark on how the page was written becomes a case; the format has no part for corrections. The sentence on defaults says "about the candidate". After the second run the row `rulings` was brought in line.
