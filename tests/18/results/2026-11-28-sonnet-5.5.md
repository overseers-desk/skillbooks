# Test 18: a way of presenting the candidate that the candidate rules out

- Date: 28 November 2026
- Model: Sonnet 5.5, one fresh agent with no context
- Under test: `sales-hire/hire-methodology.md`, the passage on the candidate file under "What a run produces"
- Rounds: one

## The prompt

> You keep one candidate's file for her job applications. Applications are written by other agents under the method in `sales-hire/` (read `hire-methodology.md` there; read nothing else in that repository and write nothing anywhere).
>
> On reading drafts she said four things. For each, say in one or two lines where it goes and what happens to it, by the method as written, naming the part of the candidate file and the fields you would fill where it goes into the file. Quote the sentence of the method you relied on.
>
> 1. "Don't present the airline as my client. It was the airport's customer, and I worked for the airport."
> 2. "Don't make me sound as if I want to manage people again."
> 3. "Open with my years in logistics, then the software."
> 4. "The tone is too keen. Calm it down."
>
> Finish with anything in the method's text that left you unsure where one of these belongs, quoting the words that were unclear.

## The reply, and the standard answer

| Item | Standard answer | The agent's answer | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, a presentation ruled out that rests on a fact | The file: the fact as it stands | `facts`, in that post's subject, with her sentence as `words`; a register row that assumed otherwise marked wrong | none |
| 2, a presentation ruled out | The file, as a ruling | Top-level `rulings`, with her words; each page read against it in the sources check | none |
| 3, what the page opens with | Not the file: acted on in the draft; a case if no check raised it | Nothing in the file; repaired where she pointed; a case | none |
| 4, the tone | Not the file: acted on in the draft; a case if no check raised it | Nothing in the file; repaired; a case | none |

## What the agent was unsure of

- Whether item 2 is a decision about her information or a remark on how the page was written; it took the first on the words "a way of presenting them that they have ruled out". Minor.
- "A correction holds for later documents of that kind", in Interview, beside a file that holds no remark on how a page is written: nothing but a case carries items 3 and 4 to the next application. Minor, and outside the passage under test.
- Whether "worked for the airport" is a `value` or her `words`. Minor.

## Edits made in this session

The candidate's decisions about their own information, as the passage lists them, include a way of presenting them that they have ruled out.
