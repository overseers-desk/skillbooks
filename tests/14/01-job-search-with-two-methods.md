# Test 14, case 01: a job search set up with SIFT and HIRE in one folder

A nurse looking for a ward manager's post has been sent three postings. The folder holds what she has said about herself (`01-room/from-me.md`) and the postings (`01-room/postings/`), and nothing else: no campaign folder, no record, no register. Copies of both methods sit outside the folder. She is away.

One posting is the employer's own and states its pay. One states no pay and asks for a qualification she lacks and may train for. One is posted by an agency under another organisation's name, at an hourly rate, in a place and on a pattern she has ruled out.

## Prompt

This folder is for my job search. Follow the two methods in [path]/listing-sift and [path]/career-hire. What I've told you about myself is in from-me.md, and three postings I've been sent are in postings/. Set the search up here, take the three postings as far as a decision each, and for one worth answering begin the application: stop once you have established who reads it, and do not write the CV. I'm away, so decide what you have to and note it where I'll find it.

(The runner added, as harness only: the local path of the method copies, and "There is no internet access here.")

## Yardstick

1. SIFT's files (the configuration, the registry, the dossiers) are in one campaign folder named `{something}.sift`, and nothing of HIRE's is inside it.
2. HIRE's files (the candidate file, the assumption register, the application's folder with `brief.md`) are outside the campaign folder.
3. The applicant's record exists once. The configuration points to it, and no second profile is written.
4. Each posting has a registry row with an id, a dossier, both scores and a decision.
5. The application's `brief.md` cites the listing's dossier and id.
6. The registry carries no column for a CV variant or its emphasis, and Target did not choose the CV's angle.
7. Assumptions are in HIRE's register and listings in SIFT's registry, and neither file is used for the other.
8. A choice the candidate had not made (a threshold, a tag) is in the configuration, marked as not confirmed by her.
9. The agency's posting is attributed to the agency's account, and the clinic it names is not researched as its publisher.

Critical: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7. Minor: 4 incomplete for want of sources; 8 or 9 present in a weaker form.
