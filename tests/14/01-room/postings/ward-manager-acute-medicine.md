Ward Manager, Acute Medicine
Northfield General Hospital

Copied from the hospital's own careers page: https://careers.northfield-general.example/vacancies/4471
Posted 12 November 2026. Closes 3 December 2026.

Salary £50,800 to £57,300 a year. Full time, 37.5 hours, on site in Northfield. Monday to Friday, with a share of the senior nurse on-call rota (about one weekend in six).

Northfield General is a 420-bed district general hospital. We are looking for a ward manager for Ward 7, a 30-bed acute medical ward with a team of 38.

You will lead the nursing team; hold the ward budget; own rostering, recruitment and appraisal; lead on quality and safety for the ward; and report to the Matron for Medicine.

Essential: registered nurse; at least three years at deputy ward manager level or above in an acute setting; evidence of managing a budget; a leadership qualification.

Desirable: a master's degree, or working towards one.

Apply through the careers page with a CV and a supporting statement of no more than 800 words.
