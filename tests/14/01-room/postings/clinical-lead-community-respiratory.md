Posted 18 November 2026. No closing date given.

Salary not stated. Full time or four days a week. Based at the Calder Bridge health centre, with home visits; some home working for administration.

Brightwater is a partnership of nine GP practices. The respiratory team (six nurses and two physiotherapists) supports people with chronic lung disease at home. The lead sets the team's clinical standards, manages the team, and works with the hospital's respiratory consultants and the commissioners on the future shape of the service.

Essential: registered nurse with substantial post-registration experience; experience of leading a team; independent prescriber, or willing to train in the first year.

Desirable: respiratory experience; experience of service redesign.

To apply, send a CV and covering letter to the practice manager.
