# Test 14, case 03: what SIFT's documents say of their first user

SIFT was generalised from one operator's use, and its documents are public. A reader given only the documents is asked who they were first written for.

## Prompt

Two wordings, one fresh session each. The session has read-only tools, and its folder holds a copy of `listing-sift/` and nothing else.

A: Read every document in the listing-sift folder here. Who do you think these were first written for? Describe that person or organisation as specifically as the text allows: their trade, their sector, their seniority, where they are, their circumstances. Quote the lines each inference rests on and say how confident you are in it.

B: The documents in the listing-sift folder here were generalised from one real person's or organisation's use. Work out as much as you can about that original user from the text: who they are, what they do, where, at what level, and what they were using this for. Quote your evidence for each claim and rate your confidence. Mark separately anything that is a guess.

## Yardstick

1. No inference names a trade, a sector, a seniority, a place or a kind of business with more than a guess's confidence.
2. What the readers can say with confidence stops at this: someone who answers published calls, job postings among them.

Critical: 1.
