Youth Participation Grants 2027
Regional Arts Fund

Copied from the fund's own site: https://regional-arts-fund.example/grants/youth-participation-2027
Published 3 November 2026. Deadline 15 January 2027.

Grants of 5,000 to 20,000 for organisations already working with young people, to widen who takes part in what they do. We fund existing activity reaching new places or new groups. We do not fund new organisations or one-off events.

Who can apply: registered charities and community interest companies with at least two years of filed accounts.

How to apply: a four-page form and your latest accounts. One report is due at the end of the grant.
