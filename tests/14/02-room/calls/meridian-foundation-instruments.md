Instruments for Learning: capital grants
The Meridian Foundation

Copied from the foundation's own site: https://meridian-foundation.example/apply/instruments-for-learning
Published 1 September 2026. Applications are considered every quarter; there is no closing date.

Capital grants towards musical instruments for children's tuition. Awards vary; the foundation does not publish a typical amount. The most it gives to one applicant is 12,000.

Who can apply: schools, and charities that teach in schools.

Conditions: the applicant finds a quarter of the cost from other sources. Instruments bought with the grant are listed in a register sent to the foundation each year for three years.

How to apply: a two-page letter with quotations from suppliers.
