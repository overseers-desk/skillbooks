Commission: a young people's strand for the Summer Festival 2027
City Culture Office

Copied from a public tenders portal: https://tenders-portal.example/notice/2026-118834
The notice carries the City Culture Office's name and crest. It is posted under the account of Proctor Events Ltd, which the text says is "managing the commissioning process on the Office's behalf".
Published 10 November 2026. Deadline 8 December 2026.

One commission of 60,000 to design and deliver a new strand of performances by young people across four weekends in July 2027.

Requirements: the applicant is a company with three years of audited accounts; it names a full-time project manager for the length of the commission; it holds public liability insurance of 5 million.

How to apply: a proposal of up to twenty pages, a budget, and two references from public bodies. Monthly progress reports are required.
