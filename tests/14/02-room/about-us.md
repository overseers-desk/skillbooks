# About us, for the funding search

Harbourside Music Workshop is a small charity. We teach music to children aged 7 to 16 in three primary schools and at our own Saturday school, about 240 children a year. We have two full-time staff, eleven sessional tutors and a volunteer board. Turnover last year was 186,000, most of it from school contracts and parents' fees; grants were about a fifth.

## What we want funding for

- Instruments: half our loan stock is past repair.
- A second Saturday site across the river, where there is nothing.
- We do not want project funding that needs a new member of staff to run it or report on it.

## What we care about, in order

1. Whether it pays for what we already do well, and not for a new project invented for the funder.
2. The amount, against the work of applying and reporting. Under 5,000 is not worth a long form.
3. Whether the funder's name helps with the next funder.

## Facts

- Registered charity since 2014. Accounts filed every year, independently examined, not audited.
- No paid fundraiser: the director writes applications, about one day a month.
- We have had two grants from the Regional Arts Fund (2021 and 2023) and reported on both.
- We are not a school and not a company limited by shares.
