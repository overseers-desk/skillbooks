## Root cause analysis

The dominant failure across rounds was the **helpfulness budget** -- the model's training to be helpful creates a fixed amount of accommodation behaviour that, when suppressed in one area (e.g. scheduling), leaks into another (e.g. restrictions analysis, weight fabrication). Procedural guardrails (trim tables, scope checks) were gamed by the agent reclassifying helpful content under permitted categories.

The mechanism that worked was forcing the agent to **classify each sentence as fact or opinion with a named source**. This is harder to game because "common knowledge" and "general equestrian understanding" fail the test -- if you cannot name a file, you cannot state it. The model's helpfulness instinct cannot manufacture a source citation the way it can manufacture a justification category (R/M/C/F).
