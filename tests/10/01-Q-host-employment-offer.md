**Re Host Employment**

The purpose of host employment is to provide a worker with some rehabilitation duties to assist them back to normal work.  Host employment is regularly offered by other employers such as Autobarn, Repco, Ideal Electrical, aged care facilities, golf courses and many more.  

WorkCover Qld allow the HOST EMPLOYMENT workers to work for your organisation for <u>free</u> to assist their recovery.  If you feel that host employment might be helpful, we have an initial meeting to see if the worker is suitable for your organisation. If the worker is suitable, we then discuss what sort of duties might be appropriate and develop a return to work plan.

I am not sure how much experience your team has had with host employment, so here are some more details on host employment.

1. The program is monitored by either us or a WorkCover Qld representative, depending on the needs of the situation.  You are able to discuss progress and how things are going at any time.
2. A HOST employer can terminate the program without reason at any time should there be any difficulties experienced.
3. WorkCover Qld provide personal injury insurance for the host worker whilst performing the duties detailed in the suitable duties plan (this document specifies tasks and restrictions).
4. As in all employee situations, WorkCover Qld don’t cover or insure against any mistake the worker might make whilst working for you (eg dropping a box on a customer's foot) however the program specifies what they are allowed to do.  Hence, public liability insurance is necessary for you to have but all organisations normally have this.
5. In regard to the insurance arrangements, please find below a link to information about HOST employment from WorkCover Qld. Please read this carefully as this is from WorkCover Qld and best describes the arrangement.

6. If after the meeting, you believe that the worker will be suitable for host employment and helpful to your organisation, WorkCover Qld will send through a host agreement that clarifies how WorkCover Qld insurance arrangements work in the event of injury and roles and responsibilities.
