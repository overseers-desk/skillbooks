## Open

- **E 1 against E 5.** For a fact from a document the candidate approved, E 1 says it "comes out only where judges prefer the version without it"; E 5 says judges "cannot decide a single line". When the cut is one line, both cannot be obeyed. Proposed by the session that ran the night: the line stays and its row goes to the register for the candidate, and judges are called only where the version without it differs by two sentences or more. The line-cut case's second pass line would change with it. Awaiting the owner.
- **A `level` row on a measured result.** prose-stethoscope's maintainer re-ran its readers on one CV of the night with the advertisement given to them as a fact (skillbooks d7e169f8, `--fact reading-for=@path`): the reply-rate line still scored 5 on `level` in all nine runs, each reason being a small sales metric offered as a director's only measured result. On a second CV, three runs each with the advertisement and with the one-sentence reading-for, the same line scored `level` 5, 4, 5 and 5, 5, 5 for the same reason. So the readers were not misled for want of the post, and the rule that keeps an approved line shows only that the line beats nothing. Proposed: check 1 answers a `level` row on a measured result with a stronger result from the record where there is one, and otherwise with a register row asking the candidate for one. Awaiting the owner, with the E 1 question above.
- **A check for the frame.** prose-stethoscope has a `frame` module (skillbooks 941190bb): given the frame as a fact, it scores per sentence how likely the reader is to take the author for someone else. On one CV of the night, with the post's taken-for as the frame, the latest job's heading scored 6 to 7 in every run, and four other lines reached 4 in every run; a whole-CV this-guy-aint verdict, run as the post's trade, broke belief in the same places, the summary's opening and the latest job. A glance test on 26 November found the same: a reason line in the summary reached no screener's picture, and in the latest job's entry it reached half of them, and one post's shortlist fell to 2 of 4. Run on those versions, `frame` scored the latest job's heading 5 to 7 in every run whether the line was there or not, and scored the line itself 4 to 6 in every run, in either place, as the author admitting he had left engineering leadership: the reading behind the fall, from three runs a version. With a reason line rebuilt from the candidate's corrected record, the screeners shortlisted all 16 versions and none took the line into its picture, while `frame` still scored the line 3 to 5 for new reasons; `frame` scores lines screeners do not notice, so its rows on a line are a reader's report and the screeners' picture is the test. Proposed: check 1 names `frame` with `hirer.md`'s taken-for as its fact. Awaiting the owner.
- **Whether the stop rule is reached.** On one CV of the night, run past its limit to five passes on 26 November: the changes a pass led to fell (29, 23, 9, then one bullet), and the sentence readers' rows at 4 or more in both runs did not (10, 13, 8, 17, 12). Most of those rows sit on lines the method says to leave, and unchanged lines cross the cut-off in some runs and not others, so the rows cannot reach zero. The one passage still changing had been at the cut-off in every pass through four wordings. Proposed by the session that ran the night: a passage still at the cut-off in both runs after two rewrites goes to the register for the candidate instead of a third, since each rewrite of that passage moved its rows to another clause and one added a clause shared with another CV; and the stop rule's "no change" is counted over rows the method has not already told the writer to leave. Awaiting the owner.
