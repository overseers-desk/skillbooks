# MOVE Elect — the owner's word, recorded, and the hand-off

Elect is the owner's phase. The AI presents the value table and the recommendation and waits. An unattended run ends here, with `recommended` filled and `elected` null; nothing about where the owner will be is decided without the owner.

## The election

The owner picks an offer, or amends one. An amended offer is a new letter with its stays and its values written before it is elected, so the file shows what was chosen against. The election records the letter, the date, and the reason in the owner's terms. A reason that overrides the recommendation is as valid as any other and more useful to the next run.

With the election, write the foregone list: each in-field event no elected stay attends, and why in one clause, drawn from the value narrative. That list is what a later reader checks when the elected plan is questioned, and what the sweep's next alert is read against.

Deadlines from the map bound the election. An offer whose event closes registration before the owner can decide is elected with that fact in front of the owner, or dropped.

## The hand-off

The `handoff` section is what travel starts from: the presence schedule (region, from, to) drawn from the elected offer's stays away from base, and the constraints around it: events that must be attended with their dates, who travels on each stay, the visa or residency rule the profile names for the region. Travel's own procedures take it from there; nothing about flights, rooms or days is decided here.

The almanac stays as it was. When a ticket is bought or a speaking slot confirmed, that is a fact about participation, and the sweep records it in the year file on its next pass. The election is not that fact; it is the plan to make it one.

## Re-election

A re-election rewrites this file. It starts from Value when the field is unchanged and a factor moved, and from Map when the sweep's alert says events entered or left the range. The new election carries its own reason, which says what replaced the last one and why; git holds the rest.
