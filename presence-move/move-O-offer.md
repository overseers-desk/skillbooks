# MOVE Offer — plans that span the range

Offer turns the map into two to four presence plans, lettered, each one a way of spending the range. It drafts; it does not judge. A plan that looks worse still earns its letter if it anchors on a different cluster, because the comparison in Value is what shows why it is worse, and a plan not written cannot be compared.

## What an offer is

An ordered sequence of stays covering every day of the range: where the party is, from when to when, which in-field events it attends from there, and the move that brings it there (the hub or the leg class, not a booking). A stay at a base attends what is inside that base's event radius. Offers differ in their anchor: the cluster the plan is built around, or the decision not to move.

## Feasible in gross terms

An offer is drafted feasible, so that Value compares plans and not errors:

- one place at a time, with travel days between regions counted: a day each way for a long-haul leg, part of a day within a region;
- every fixed point held: the fixed presence, with its companion model, and the committed events;
- the profile's own rules for a base honoured, the visa rule where one is named, the lead time where the base states one;
- a deadline from the map that has already passed makes the event unattendable, and the offer says so rather than attending it.

Fares, hotels and day plans are not gross terms. They belong to Value where a fare decides between offers, and to travel after the election.

## The set

Between two and four offers. The first is always the plan of not moving: stay at the base the profile fixes or defaults to for the range and attend what is near. It is the baseline the value of moving is measured against, and a run that omits it cannot say what a trip is worth. Each further offer anchors on a different cluster from the map, the strongest anchors first. Where two clusters chain through a region without a return to base, one offer may take both; the chain is its anchor.

An unplaced event appears in an offer only as a condition: the stay is worth taking if the event lands inside it, and the offer names what it is worth without it.

## Output

The `offers` section of the move file: for each letter, a name that says its anchor, and its stays. One sentence per offer on the idea behind it. No values, no ranking, no recommendation.
