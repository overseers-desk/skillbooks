# MOVE Value — the offers compared on a vector

Value compares the offers and recommends one. It reads the rubric before it reads an offer, because the rubric is the owner's weighing of what the year is for, and an offer is valued in those terms or not at all.

## The dimensions

Six, kept apart. A composite would need weights nobody has measured, and would hide exactly the trade the owner has to see.

- **Event value.** What each offer attends: the stars and treatments, the pull-event matches, the shortlist, and what the rubric says those are worth for this owner. Argue it as the sweep argues a rating, in terms a reader can dispute, not by adding stars.
- **Presence.** Which obligations each offer meets and which it departs from. Fixed presence was held in Offer; what is weighed here is the probable default and the companions, since a season away from the default base has a cost the profile states in certainty rather than in money.
- **Cost of absence.** The best events each offer forgoes, and whether they recur. The cache says whether an event is annual. A non-recurring strong event foregone weighs more than an annual one, which can be planned for next year, and an offer's narrative names what it gives up.
- **Travel burden.** Legs by class (long-haul, regional, ground), days in transit, returns to base, time zones crossed. Measured from the map's distances and the offer's stays.
- **Money.** Tickets from the cache, at the tiers that still apply on the election date, plus travel at the order of magnitude the leg class implies, in the almanac's base currency at the rates the data root holds. A price has one home in the cache; here it is cited and summed, not restated.
- **Risk.** Unconfirmed dates, access gated with no route in, sell-out risk, and any deadline the map lists that passes before an election can be made.

## Travel factors

Coarse by default. A long-haul leg is a long-haul leg, and its cost class separates most offers on its own. Where two offers sit within a coin-toss on the coarse view and the deciding difference is a fare, look one up with a flight-search skill if one is available, for the deciding legs only, and record the figure in the move file with the date it was checked. A fare is not an event fact and does not enter the cache.

Companion viability, hotel loyalty and day-level feasibility belong to travel after the election. Value notes where an offer's worth depends on one of them and leaves the check there.

## Valuing without anchoring

Value each offer on every dimension before comparing any two. The first offer read tends to become the frame the rest are judged against, and the baseline offer, read first by construction, would then look like the norm. Where a run delegates, one worker per offer with the same rubric and the same dimensions, then a comparison across the workers' sheets, keeps the frames apart.

## The recommendation

A table, offers by dimensions, with a short entry in each cell a reader can check against the map and the cache. A narrative per offer. Then one recommendation, and the trade it makes stated in one sentence: what the recommended offer buys, what it pays, and what the runner-up would have bought instead. A recommendation without its trade is an election in disguise.

## Output

The `values` and `recommended` sections of the move file. `elected` stays null; that word is the owner's.
