# Invariants — MOVE

Hard rules the rest of this methodology holds. When a phase document, template or example disagrees with one, the invariant wins and the other is the bug to fix.

- **A MOVE run writes only under `moves/` in the data root it reads.** The year files and the cache are the sweep's. An election has one home, the move file, and no rating, shortlist mark or participation status changes because of an election: the ledger is mostly add-only and moves at the sweep's pace, the elected set moves at the pace of fares and family dates, and a plan written into the ledger is stale in both directions.
