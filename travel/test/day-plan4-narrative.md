## The day, scene by scene

**~07:30 — wake, slow start.** Everyone went down around 22:00 and woke early; there is no reason to rush. The hard endpoint is 10+ hours away. Let the kids surface gently.

**08:15–09:15 — breakfast at the hotel.** Express Start breakfast is included and runs to **09:30 on weekdays** — comfortable margin. Feed everyone well; the next proper meal is lunch around noon. *(Last fed: breakfast ~08:30. Next: lunch ~12:00 — a ~3.5 h gap, within the child limit.)*

**09:15–10:00 — pack, and two phone jobs.**
1. Try **China Eastern online check-in** for both PNRs (NX3N7D, NV9FS3) to put the four seats together; expect to still visit the counter for the checked bag and document check.
2. If choosing Flip Out (below), **book the session online now** — it is online-only, no on-site or phone booking.

**~10:45 — check out, store the bags.** Checkout is **11:00** (verify at the desk; some IHG Express properties run to noon). It barely matters: the hotel offers **luggage storage**, so the one checked bag and four cabin bags go into the store room and the family moves bag-free for the rest of the morning. Confirm collection works up to ~13:30.

**11:00–12:00 — one light activity near the hotel.** The choice is decided by the 09:30 look out the window (see Decision A). The strong, grounded options are all within a short walk of Priddy's Yard:

- *Default, showery:* **Flip Out Croydon**, inside the **Centrale** centre directly beside the hotel (Mon–Thu 10:00–20:00; discounted 1-hour weekday slots 10:00–14:00). Beyond trampolines it has mini-golf, laser quest and synthetic ice, so non-jumpers are covered. It burns the kids' energy before the flight — its real value today. **[ENERGY]** Trampolining hard right before 11 h in a seat risks a sprain or an over-tired, cranky Alice at the airport; keep it to a single off-peak hour, or steer Alice to mini-golf/soft attractions rather than the main court.
- *Calm alternative (zero risk):* **Museum of Croydon** at the Croydon Clocktower, Katharine St — free, indoor, open Wed–Sat (so open today), a quiet ~45–60 min. Good if the morning already feels frayed.
- *Dry-bonus only:* a run-around at **Wandle Park** or **Queen's Gardens** (both close, free) — unlocked only if the 09:30 forecast has flipped to dry and bright.

**12:00–13:00 — lunch at BOXPARK Croydon.** 99 George Street, a covered street-food hall two minutes from East Croydon station (Thursday 11:00–01:00). Wide choice suits a 6- and 12-year-old, and it sits exactly on the path to the train, so lunch and the walk to the platform are one move. *(Next food after this: airside dinner ~16:00 — a ~3.5 h gap, fine.)*

**~13:15 — collect bags from the hotel, walk to East Croydon (~8 min).**

**13:30–14:15 — train East Croydon → Gatwick.** Six trains an hour, direct, **14–27 min** (Southern / Thameslink / Gatwick Express). A missed train costs ~10 min, not the plan. With one checked bag and four cabin bags this is easy; it also sidesteps Liansu's motion sickness entirely. Aim for a train around **13:45–14:00**.

**~14:15–14:45 — arrive Gatwick.** Check-in for an international long-haul opens ~3 h before (≈15:00) and the counter closes 60 min before (17:00). Arriving ~14:30 means a short wait before bag drop opens — deliberate, so the airport hour is unhurried with kids rather than a sprint.

**15:00–16:00 — bag drop, security, settle airside.** Drop the one checked bag, clear security, find the gate area.

**~16:00 — airside dinner.** Feed everyone properly before boarding; onboard meal service comes after the 18:00 departure, but a 6-year-old should not wait that long. This closes the food plan with no 4-hour gap anywhere in the day.

**~17:20 — boarding. 18:00 — wheels up.** Hard endpoint met. Tomorrow's Shanghai split is a separate day.

## Decision points

- **Decision A — indoor vs outdoor activity. Decided ~09:30** by the window and the Met Office hourly. *Showery/cool (the likely case):* Flip Out (or the museum if the morning is frayed). *Dry and bright:* swap in a park run-around. Switch cost is near zero — both sit within a few minutes' walk.
- **Decision B — train vs taxi to Gatwick. Decided ~13:00** by a glance at National Rail for East Croydon–Gatwick disruption. *Normal service (default):* train, ~14–27 min, 6/hr. *Major disruption:* black cab / Uber XL, ~30–40 min typical; within Liansu's 45-min passenger threshold on a normal run (**[MINOR]** a bad-traffic 60-min run would just cross it — tolerable for one leg, put her in the front).
- **Decision C — latest safe departure from Croydon.** Plan to leave ~13:45–14:00. The **latest safe departure is ~15:15**: train 27 min + a missed-train ~12 min + ~8 min walk + a 30–45 min security queue still clears the 17:00 bag-drop close with margin. The plan sits ~75–90 min inside that — robust on the bad run.

## Residual risks

- **Rail disruption** on the Brighton main line. Mitigated by 6 trains/hour and the sub-45-min taxi fallback (Decision B). Low.
- **Showers at midday** (60%). Mitigated by indoor-default activity (Decision A). Low.
- **Flip Out before a long flight** — minor injury / over-tiredness. Mitigated by the 1-hour cap and steering Alice to gentler attractions; the museum is the zero-risk swap.
- **Separate PNRs** — seats split. Mitigated by flagging "one family" at the counter (and trying online check-in this morning).
- **[VERIFY]** Hotel checkout time (11:00 per listing; could be noon) and the museum's exact opening hour — neither changes the plan, since bags are stored and the museum is open today regardless.

## Verdict

A robust, low-stress departure day. The transfer is short and frequent, the luggage is light, the food gaps are all under the child limit, and every weather and transport fork has a cheap switch. The only judgement call is comfort, not feasibility: how much to let the kids move this morning against the small risk of over-tiring them before a long flight.
